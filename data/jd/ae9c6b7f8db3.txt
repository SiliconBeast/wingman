Role Overview:
Build production-quality web experiences and backend services for Genesys Cloud Workforce Management (WFM), contributing to technology that supports billions of customer interactions globally. As a Web Software Developer Intern, you will help create the next generation of web user interfaces and cloud-based services while gaining hands-on experience with modern frontend technologies, Java, Clojure, and AWS. You will contribute throughout the software development lifecycle, including feature implementation, bug fixing, unit testing, automation, and peer review, with mentorship from experienced developers. The role provides practical exposure to engineering challenges involving usability, scalability, reliability, and cloud architecture while giving you opportunities to expand your full-stack development skills. At Genesys, you will collaborate with engineers who combine cloud and AI technologies to improve experiences for employees and the customers they serve.
