The Receiver, Exciter & Back-End Processing Architecture (REPA) department is composed of an energetic multi-disciplinary team of over 130 engineers working collaboratively with internal and external customers and programs to provide mission ready capabilities to our warfighters. We research, develop, design, and integrate electrical (Analog, Power, RF/Microwave, and Digital) components, embedded and digital signal processing software, equipment, systems, sub-systems, and networks for the defense of domestic and foreign purposes.

As an Electrical Engineer you’ll have the opportunity to work on a variety of systems and equipment found within our radar systems ranging from receivers, exciters and processing electronics and communications systems. The Department’s products contain Analog, RF, Microwave, Low Noise References, Digital controls and embedded Software.

What You Will Do:

• Work Onsite at our Tewksbury, MA Location

• You will contribute your knowledge of engineering practices and problem-solving in development, test, integration and production support of our receiver, exciter products.

• You will support, contribute, and collaborate in a team environment, receiving inputs from the program or team experts, and work on a wide array of projects, determining and developing approaches and solutions.
