About the Role :

As a Digital Design Engineer Intern at Analog Devices (ADI) , you’ll gain practical experience in the design, simulation, and verification of digital integrated circuits. You’ll work alongside experienced engineers to develop digital models, perform FPGA prototyping, and conduct timing analysis - all while contributing to innovative projects that shape the future of electronics.

This internship is an excellent opportunity for students pursuing an engineering degree to grow their technical skills, gain industry experience, and make meaningful contributions to ADI’s product development.

Key Responsibilitie s :

•
Assist in the design and simulation of digital circuits using Verilog or VHDL

•
Support RTL design and verification activities for digital modules and components

•
Use digital modeling techniques to verify circuit functionality and performance

•
Participate in FPGA prototyping and testing for rapid design validation

•
Collaborate with senior engineers to identify and resolve design issues

•
Contribute to static timing analysis and verification processes

•
Document design work and test results according to team standards

Must Have Skills:

•
Digital circuit design and simulation experience using Verilog or VHDL

•
RTL design and verification knowledge

•
Digital modeling skills to verify circuit functionality and performance

•
FPGA prototyping and testing experience or coursework

•
Static timing analysis and verification familiarity

•
Ability to document design work and test results clearly

Preferred Education and Experience:

•
Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related field

•
Prior coursework or projects related to digital design

•
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills

•
Ability to work effectively in a team environment
