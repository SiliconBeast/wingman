Company Overview

At Bracco Medical Technologies, every employee has an impact on our Mission to empower lifesaving decisions. We create medical devices that give healthcare providers the insights they need to confidently and safely diagnose patients. Our unique line of products includes Intravascular Ultrasound (IVUS), Fractional Flow Reserve (FFR), Cardiovascular Imaging contrast delivery (CVi), and CT and MRI contrast delivery systems. As part of the Bracco Group, Bracco Medical Technologies benefits from the resources of a multinational conglomerate with broad expertise in cath lab and radiology suite technology and a dedication to continuous advancement.

We are currently seeking intern/co-op candidates to learn with us as they engage in a variety of hands-on projects alongside our high-performing and authentic Operations team.

Position Summary

As a Product Security Engineering Intern , you will be expected to analyze architecture, product design with different technology stacks, application, firmware, and hardware from a security perspective. You will be involved in discovering security issues and recommending remediation.

Primary Duties & Responsibilities:

• Engage in security research and develop tooling to enhance the product security team’s capability to detect and classify vulnerabilities within systems

• Engage with product security team to review current toolsets and conduct research on additional security capabilities

• Assist product security team members to perform security testing such as vulnerability testing and penetration testing to ensure that BMT products are resistant to exploitation

• Review security tools integration into the CI/CD pipeline and build adoption guides for the development teams.

• Evaluation of security tools and technologies and build custom internal tools as needed using Python

• Work with cross functional teams including, R&D Quality, Manufacturing and Regulatory to participate in security risk reduction

• Contribute to the development of security training

Relationship development

This role provides the opportunity to learn and work cross functionally with a variety of stakeholders to include product development teams and contribute to product security deliverables and activities.

Training to be Provided

Interns will be exposed to working with product security engineers, software developers, systems engineers, Hardware/Firmware engineers, program managers within multiple business units in Bracco Medical Technologies.

They will have the opportunity to practice and learn advanced security skillsets such threat modeling, penetration testing, security tool integration into the software development lifecycle. Interns will work with product security engineers who will conduct knowledge transfer in their area of expertise.

Additionally, our interns and co-ops benefit from a buddy program, career development programming, and opportunities to build their professional networks virtually and in-person with Bracco Medical Technologies professionals.
