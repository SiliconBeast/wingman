The Boeing Security & Fire Operations (S&FO) is seeking talented, forward-thinking students ready to join our energetic team, gaining hands-on experience across corporate site security, aviation security, emergency preparedness, business resilience, and fire protection engineering. As an intern, you will contribute to real-world projects in your specific function all while supporting daily operations in risk assessments, monitoring access control and camera systems, airport/airfield security protocols, and compliance documentation. This is a unique opportunity to work cross-functionally with facilities, operations, legal, and emergency response teams, and to see how layered protection strategies come together to keep people and operations safe.

In this role you’ll help design and test emergency exercises and business continuity playbooks, analyze vulnerabilities and mitigation options, and assist in industrial fire engineering assessments. You’ll learn to translate technical findings into clear briefings for stakeholders, support aviation security audits and access-control improvements, and contribute to after-action reports that shape future resilience. Ideal candidates are curious, detail-oriented, and driven by purpose—ready to learn complex systems, think creatively under pressure, and make a measurable impact on organizational safety and continuity.

As an intern at Boeing, you’ll gain hands-on experience, learn from experts, and collaborate with teams that do truly amazing things.

Over the course of our 10-12-week internship experience you will have the opportunity to:

• Work directly with a mentor in your field of study while finding solutions to real world problems

• Experience our products up close by touring our state-of-the-art manufacturing and research facilities

• Interact directly with our company leaders to discuss strategy and the future of Boeing

• Build a lasting professional network through social events and engaging activities

• Gain the skills and knowledge to become a future leader in the aerospace industry
