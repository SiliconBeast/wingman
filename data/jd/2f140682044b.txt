The Opportunity:
CACI is seeking talented Software Engineering Interns to join our team in Reston, VA for Summer 2027! The internship will begin in May and last 12 weeks. You will be required to work 100% on-site.

Responsibilities:

• Assist in coding, testing, and debugging new software or making enhancements to existing software and suggest solutions for problems or software enhancements

• Assist in the Integration and development of new Artificial Intelligence capabilities to sift large data sets

• Assist in writing, modifying, and debugging software for client applications and writes programs according to specifications from higher-level personnel

• Assist in developing the critical components of the solution, including but not limited to, security, rules infrastructure, custom adapter implementation, and application interfaces

• Use source debuggers and visual development environments

• Profile, analyze, and optimize the software

• Employ best practices for design, development, unit testing and test plan development and develop software user manuals

• Participate in team meetings, including daily standups and Scrum meetings

Qualifications:

Required:

• Must be legally authorized to work in the United States without the need for employer sponsorship, now or at any time in the future

• Familiarity with Web Development Technologies such as Java, JavaScript, HTML, HTML5, CSS, Groovy, Grails, Angular

• Familiarity with Backend Development Technologies such as Python and Java

• Familiarity with hardware or software tools at an appropriate academic level

Desired:

• Minimum GPA of 3.0 or higher is preferred but not required

• Experience with data science tools and processes

• Previous internship/co-op or other work experience
