Position: Operations Analytics Co-op/Intern
Number of Position(s): 3
Duration: 4 Months (with potential extension for Summer 2027)
Date: January - April 2027
Location: Onsite, Ottawa, ON, Canada

EDUCATIONAL RECOMMENDATIONS

Currently a candidate for a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Engineering, Computer Science, Data Science, Business Analytics, Information Systems, or a related field with an accredited school in Canada.

What you will do:

• Support day-to-day operational activities and process optimization initiatives.

• Analyze operational and business data using Excel and Power BI to develop dashboards, reports, and performance metrics.

• Design and develop demand planning reports using Power BI, Excel, and Power Automate to support staffing forecasts and resource planning activities.

• Build automated reporting solutions that provide actionable insights across regions and user profiles for operational performance reviews.

• Assist in developing and applying AI-based solutions for data analysis, automation, forecasting, and operational optimization.

• Collaborate with engineering, product, and business teams on data-driven projects and continuous improvement initiatives.

• Prepare presentations, reports, and executive summaries to communicate insights and recommendations to stakeholders.

• Maintain documentation and ensure the accuracy, consistency, and integrity of operational data across systems.

You Must Have:

• Knowledge of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning concepts gained through coursework, projects, or practical experience.

• Experience using Power BI for dashboard creation, reporting, or data visualization.

• Strong Microsoft Excel skills, including data analysis, pivot tables, and advanced formulas.

• Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to interpret complex datasets.

• Ability to work independently while collaborating effectively within a team environment.

• Strong attention to detail, organizational skills, and a commitment to data accuracy.

• Excellent communication skills with the ability to present findings clearly to technical and non-technical audiences.

It Would Be Nice If You Also Had:

• Experience with Python, SQL, or similar data analysis and automation tools.

• Familiarity with operational processes and workflows in a technology-driven environment.

• Prior internship, co-op, academic project, or consulting experience related to operations, analytics, business intelligence, or process improvement.

• Experience with Power Automate or other workflow automation tools.

• Exposure to forecasting, demand planning, or business performance reporting.

• Familiarity with AI-driven analytics, automation, or predictive modeling techniques.
