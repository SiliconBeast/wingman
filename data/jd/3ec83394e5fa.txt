IT Infrastructure Intern - Summer 2027
Program Duration: End of May - Early August
Location: Pella, IA (on-site)

Pella Corporation is seeking motivated undergraduate students for our IT Infrastructure Internship in Pella, Iowa.

This internship provides hands-on experience supporting Pella's Information Technology organization through meaningful projects and day-to-day operational activities. Interns are placed on a team based on business needs, educational background, and skills, allowing them to gain real-world experience while contributing to technology initiatives that support the business.

Throughout the summer, interns collaborate with experienced IT professionals, build professional networks, and develop technical and professional skills that prepare them for future careers in information technology.

If you're looking for a team-oriented, real-world learning experience where your work can make an impact, we'd love to connect with you.

Responsibilities:

As a Systems Infrastructure Intern, you will support Pella's Information Technology organization through project work focused on infrastructure, collaboration technologies, systems administration, and enterprise technology solutions. Working alongside experienced professionals, you'll help implement and support technology initiatives that improve business processes and end-user experiences.

Responsibilities are similar to what we expect of entry-level IT professionals who join Pella after graduation, providing meaningful technical experience while contributing to business objectives.

Potential Assignment Areas May Include:

Microsoft Infrastructure

Support projects involving Pella's server environment, endpoint management, and end-user computing solutions. Technologies may include Windows Server, Hyper-V, Active Directory, Exchange, SQL Server, System Center, Intune, Jamf, and Veeam.

Microsoft 365

Support initiatives that enhance productivity, collaboration, security, and data management. Past projects have included SharePoint Online, OneDrive, Exchange Online, Microsoft Copilot, Data Loss Prevention (DLP), and Microsoft Defender.

Unix, Linux & Storage Administration

Assist with infrastructure supporting core business applications through server administration, system enhancements, operational support, and storage area networking technologies.

Communication Services

Support networking and communication solutions including LAN, WAN, voice, video, and security technologies. Technologies may include Cisco and Palo Alto platforms.

Qualifications:

• Currently pursuing a bachelor's degree in Management Information Systems (MIS), Computer Science, Information Technology, Cybersecurity, Computer Engineering, or a related field

• Strong academic standing

• Demonstrated leadership, teamwork, or involvement through campus organizations, work experience, athletics, volunteer activities, or other experiences

• Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, including Excel, Word, PowerPoint, and Outlook

• Strong written and verbal communication skills with the ability to effectively communicate with both technical and non-technical audiences

• Ability to learn new technologies, systems, and technical concepts quickly

• Demonstrated problem-solving and analytical skills

• Experience leading projects, initiatives, or team efforts from planning through execution preferred

• Ability to succeed in a fast-paced, collaborative environment

• Hands-on experience using Claude Code or a similar LLM agent to help define tasks and implement solutions.
