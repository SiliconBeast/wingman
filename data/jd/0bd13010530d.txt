Job Title
Co-op – Mechanical/Process Engineer – Reedsville, PA – 2027

Job Description

Are you interested in an Internship opportunity with Philips? We welcome individuals who are currently pursuing an undergraduate (BS) and/or graduate (MS) degree to participate in paid intern opportunities at our site in Reedsville, PA. Through this role you will gain meaningful, hands-on experience working for a HealthTech company. Your role:

•
Support mechanical design and process improvement work for ultrasound transducer R&D, including tooling, fixtures, prototype builds, test setups, and documentation updates.

•
Assist with design-build-test activities by helping create prototypes, execute experiments, collect data, analyze results, and summarize recommendations for engineering teams.

•
Collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders across mechanical, process, manufacturing, quality, materials, acoustic, electrical, and systems engineering to support product development and continuous improvement goals.

•
Contribute to process robustness, manufacturability, and quality improvement efforts by supporting root cause analysis, process characterization, work instructions, and verification activities.

•
Gain practical experience in medical device engineering, regulated product development, technical documentation, and hands-on problem solving while working with experienced engineering mentors.

You are the right fit if:

•
You are pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Mechanical Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, Industrial Engineering, Biomedical Engineering, Engineering Science, or a related technical field.

•
Your skills include strong mechanical aptitude, hands-on problem solving, data analysis, technical documentation, and proficiency with Microsoft Office tools. Experience with computer-aided design software such as SolidWorks or Creo is preferred.

•
You have completed relevant coursework, projects, internships, co-ops, research, or lab work involving mechanical design, manufacturing processes, materials, testing, or process improvement.

•
You must be able to successfully perform the following minimum Physical, Cognitive and Environmental job requirements with or without accommodation for this position. You are curious, collaborative, detail-oriented, and able to communicate clearly with technical and non-technical stakeholders in a fast-paced engineering environment.
