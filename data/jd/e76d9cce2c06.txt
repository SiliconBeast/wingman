The Finance Intern, reporting into the Finance Manager, will gain exposure to manufacturing financial planning & analysis for our Drinking Water Business Unit. The Intern will provide support to the finance team by performing the following duties:

• Partner with Finance and business team on forecast process

• Reporting improvements/variance analysis

• Profitability analysis – customer/product focused

• Reviewing product costs

• NPD business case development with cross functional team

• Operational metrics for manufacturing

• Complete special projects as needed

Qualifications:

• Pursuing a 4-year degree in Finance, Accounting, or other related field

• Proficiency with Microsoft Office applications and particularly strong Excel skills involving pivot tables and macros.

• Ability to work with others, but highly self-motivated to achieve individual tasks efficiently

• Excellent verbal and written communication skills; attention to detail

• Able to work 40 hours per week during the summer

Capabilities and Success Factors

• Business Insight – Applying knowledge of business and the marketplace to advance the organization’s goals.

• Ensures Accountability – Holding self and others accountable to meet commitments.
