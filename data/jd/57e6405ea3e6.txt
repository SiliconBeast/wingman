The Crypto Accounting team is a critical part of Robinhood's financial operations, responsible for maintaining the integrity of our financial records and supporting our rapid growth. As a Crypto Accounting Intern, you will play a key role in maintaining the integrity of our digital asset financial records, ensuring regulatory compliance, and building scalable processes to support our rapid product expansion. This is a unique opportunity to gain hands-on experience in a dynamic FinTech environment.

The role will require strong analytical skills (including comfort with spreadsheet-based data manipulation), problem-solving ability, clear communication, and a proactive, patient, and positive approach. You will assist with the monthly close process, financial reporting, and a variety of ad-hoc projects, contributing directly to the team's success.

This role is based in our New York, NY office with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

What you’ll do

• Assist with the monthly and quarterly close process, including preparing journal entries and account reconciliations

• Support financial statement analysis and reporting

• Apply U.S. GAAP principles to real-world accounting scenarios

• Collaborate with various teams to gather information and resolve accounting discrepancies

• Contribute to process improvements and automation initiatives

What you bring

• Currently enrolled in a full-time, degree-seeking program in Accounting, Finance, or a related field with an expected graduation date in Winter 2027/Spring 2028

• A foundational understanding of cryptocurrency and blockchain technology and interest in how digital assets impact financial reporting

• Effective written and verbal communication and analytical skills

• Exceptional attention to detail and organizational skills

• Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets
