Job Description

We are looking for enthusiastic and creative people with a passion for learning. Specific skills also may include:

•
Analytical mindset and a can-do approach to problem-solving

•
Hands-on experience with a Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) such as Elastic Stack or Splunk

•
Knowledge of Windows, Linux, iOS, and virtual environments

•
Networking fundamentals (TCP/IP, HTTP protocols; Tools: TCPDump, Wireshark, Nmap)

•
Forensic knowledge (FTK, Autopsy, Volatility)

•
Review logs to identify unusual behavior in networks and devices

•
Endpoint Detection knowledge (Wazuh, Velociraptor, OSSEC, OSQuery, OpenEDR)

•
Experience with network-based intrusion detection (IDS/IPS - SNORT or Suricata)

•
Understand email protection, investigations (analyze email headers), and protocols (IMAP, SMTP, POP3)

•
Experience with cloud environments (AWS, Azure, GCP, etc.)

•
Ability to program and write scripts (Python, Java, Go, or any other modern language)

•
Proactive approach and enthusiasm for problem identification and solving

•
Flexible and able to adapt to changing priorities

•
A strong appetite for continuous learning

•
Great verbal and written skills, and a willingness to apply them to documentation

Nice to have:

•
Training or certifications (CompTIA Network+, Security+)

•
Experience or training with AI or LLM
