Looking for an opportunity to do more than just observe? At Foth, our co-op program gives you real-world experience on real client projects. As an Electrical Engineering Co-op, you’ll apply your classroom knowledge to practical challenges while working alongside experienced engineers. Based in Green Bay, WI, this role offers hands-on involvement in designing and troubleshooting systems, as well as exposure to advanced engineering processes. Spanning Summer - Fall 2027, the position may also include travel to client sites—providing a unique perspective on how engineering solutions come to life in the field.


Primary Responsibilities:
• Apply electrical engineering fundamentals while performing design tasks under the supervision of a lead engineer

• Develop detailed electrical design drawings using AutoCAD and AutoCAD Electrical software packages

• Program PLC’s and HMI hardware

• Assist with validation of the software and drawing databases

Required Qualifications: 
• Pursuing a Bachelor's Degree in Electrical Engineering from an ABET-accredited program

• GPA of 3.0 or greater

• Previous experience with and/or coursework in AutoCAD

• Previous experience with and/or coursework involving theory and/or programming of industrial PLCs
