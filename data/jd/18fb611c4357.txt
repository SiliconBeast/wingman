What you’ll be doing

The CIBC Optimization & Process Engineering (O&PE) department is made up of cross-functional teams, each with distinct mandates and core capabilities. These teams include Process Engineering (PE), Intelligent Process Solutions (IPS), and End-to-End Optimization (E2E), all of which are seeking co-op students to support ongoing efforts to enhance the delivery of products and services to our clients. As a member of one of these teams, you will collaborate in a team setting and focus on activities such as data collection, research, and analysis to assist in the design, testing, and implementation of organizational and process improvements.

Note: All applicants will be considered for all three teams. Candidates are encouraged to state their team preferences within their application and/or interview; however, allocation will ultimately be made based on business needs.

PE Team: You will be responsible for supporting process improvement initiatives by applying tools such as Lean, Six Sigma, and Project Management. You’ll be part of a team that acts as internal consultants, partnering with stakeholders across the business to enhance operational efficiency in all areas.

IPS Team: You will use your analytical skills to measure how well existing automations are performing and driving value by identifying opportunities to improve current processes through data-driven insights. Along the way, you’ll gain hands-on exposure to innovative Low-Code/No-Code tools.

E2E Team: You will be responsible for supporting end-to-end analysis to help optimize business processes and improve overall organizational performance through Low/No-Tech enhancements. You’ll be part of a team dedicated to identifying and implementing practical solutions that quickly streamline operations across the organization.

At CIBC we enable the work environment most optimal for you to thrive in your role. You’ll have the flexibility to manage your work activities within a hybrid work arrangement where you’ll spend 1-3 days per week on-site, while other days will be remote.

How you’ll succeed

• Process improvement - Assess current business processes and recommend improvements to reduce costs and error rates, improve the client experience, and decrease cycle time.

• Process mapping and analysis - Document current-state processes, conduct detailed process analysis, and design future-state process and client journey flows using Microsoft Visio and other process mapping tools.

• Collaboration - Partner with team members and stakeholders to identify process improvement opportunities, support solution design, and help implement enhancements.

• Project support - Define project requirements and prepare project summaries, including post-implementation reviews and findings from operations centre visits and other data-gathering activities.

• Communication and reporting - Use Microsoft Office tools to prepare clear recommendations, plans, and results for management presentations and reporting.

• Data and technology - Conduct data collection, time studies, and analysis using statistical techniques and tools such as Python, VBA, Power Query, and Power BI, while applying AI concepts to improve processes and personal productivity.
