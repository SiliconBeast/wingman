Ensure Trimble Next-Gen Tech as a Hardware/Software Testing Intern!

Step into the action and help test the future of industrial technology before it reaches the hands of global customers. As a Hardware/Software Testing Intern at Trimble, you will bridge the gap between field and office, analyzing performance, documenting workflows, and ensuring our cutting-edge tools deliver maximum impact and quality.

About Us:

Trimble is an industrial technology company transforming the way the world works by delivering solutions that enable our customers to thrive. We create technologies that connect the digital and physical worlds, helping our customers increase productivity, quality, safety, and sustainability. From purpose-built products to enterprise-level solutions, our technology empowers professionals in construction, geospatial, government, transportation, and more.

What Makes This Role Great:

In this role, you will play a critical hands-on part in validating next-generation hardware and software solutions, gaining direct exposure to both field-testing methodologies and advanced data analytics alongside industry-leading technical teams.

Key Exciting Responsibilities:

•
Validate and test next-generation Trimble products in real-world field and office environments to ensure peak software and hardware performance.

•
Gather, analyze, and document operational data to actively shape new product features and workflow optimizations.

•
Collaborate directly with organizational teams to develop testing tools, troubleshoot technical issues, and improve testing standards.

•
Author clear, actionable technical content and pre-release documentation to support product launches and global customer support teams.

Essential Skills & Experience:

•
Currently pursuing a degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics, or a related field.

•
Strong problem-solving capabilities, high attention to detail, and a creative approach to testing complex systems.

•
Adaptability to changing priorities and a strong dedication to exceeding customer expectations.

•
Excellent written and verbal communication skills with the ability to collaborate effectively across teams.

Bonus Points For:

•
Experience using Python, MatLab, or Robotics Operating System (ROS).

•
Prior experience with hardware tools such as multimeters and oscilloscopes.

•
Previous exposure to software testing methodologies, data acquisition, and data analysis.

•
Willingness to participate in hands-on outdoor field testing when required.
