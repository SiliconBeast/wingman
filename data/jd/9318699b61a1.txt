Job Description

Overview

AtkinsRéalis is seeking an ITS/Traffic Engineering Intern – Summer 2027 to join our Tampa, FL office.

Your role

• Support the ITS/Traffic Engineering team with transportation design, plan production, calculations, and project documentation.

• Assist with traffic signalization, ITS, signing, pavement marking, and related transportation design tasks.

• Utilize computer software such as OpenRoads Designer and/or Civil 3D to support drawings and CADD production.

• Review field notes, as-builts, and existing conditions information to support design development.

• Participate in field reviews when applicable.

• Participate in project coordination meetings, and team discussions.

• Support administrative and engineering tasks as assigned.

About you

• EXPERIENCE: No experience is generally required if accepted into a Bachelor's degree program in a related technical field.

• EDUCATION: Must have completed 60 hours towards a Civil or Electrical engineering degree from an ABET accredited program by the start of the internship ( Summer 2027 ).

• SPECIAL SKILLS:

• Basic computer skills required to enter data into spreadsheets, forms and/or databases.

• Proficiency in Microsoft Office, especially Excel.

• Math skills to perform simple calculations and willingness to learn technical design workflows.

• Interest in traffic engineering, ITS, and transportation design.

• Experience with OpenRoads Designer and Civil 3D is preferred.

• Strong attention to detail, communication skills, and ability to work in a team environment.

• Ability to work from the Tampa, FL office during the internship period.
