About the Job

Controls Engineering interns will have the opportunity to work on factory controls and automation systems that provide crucial support to manufacturing operations. They will gain hands-on experience working with programmable logic controllers (PLCs), SCADA systems, field instrumentation, motor drives, industrial networks and more. They will work in cross-disciplinary settings, often working alongside other facilities mechanical, electrical, and chemical teams on projects to improve the automated control and remote monitoring of their respective systems.

About Texas Instruments

Texas Instruments Incorporated (Nasdaq: TXN) is a global semiconductor company that designs, manufactures and sells analog and embedded processing chips for markets such as industrial, automotive, data center, personal electronics and communications equipment.
