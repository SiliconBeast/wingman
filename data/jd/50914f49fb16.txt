Johnson & Johnson is currently seeking (2) Access & Pump Engineering Co-ops to join our Cardiology team located in Danvers, MA .

Requirements:

• Conduct Feasibility testing including, but not limited to: part inspection, root cause investigations, fixture development, component prototyping, mechanical testing, thrombus loop testing, component interaction, test method development and test method validation.

• Contribute to team discussions on device design. Prototype devices either through rapid prototype manufacturing methods or performing secondary operations and assembly on procured subcomponents.

• Contribute to team discussions on development of test methods, including test method validation

• Independent fixture design and rapid fixture manufacturing

• Execute prototype device evaluations for key metrics including, but not limited to: usability, maintaining hemostasis, insertion/removal/repositioning forces through straight and tortuous anatomy, insertion characteristic to the arteriotomy, single access compatibility, and thrombus formation mitigation

• Perform statistical analysis of collected data, comparing to historic data or previously collected baseline data.

• Provide flexible support across access device, component, and kit development

Qualifications:

• Student pursuing a degree in Mechanical or Biomedical Engineering

• Experience in Computer Aided Design (SolidWorks proficiency preferred)

• Knowledge of or experience in rapid prototype manufacturing

• Familiarity with statistical analysis

• Knowledge of experimental design and test method development

• Skills in Root Cause Analysis and problem solving

• Knowledge of mechanics and fluid flow

• Prior medical device experience preferred, but not required

• Knowledge of medical device development or development in a regulated environment preferred, but not required
