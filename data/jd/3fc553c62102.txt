ABOUT THE ROLE:

Are you a top Finance or Accounting student who would enjoy a challenging internship with a competitive company? Are you interested in exposure to multiple industries and finance jobs? Does experiencing life in cities like Charlotte, Atlanta, Rosemont, or Houston sound exciting? This is the job for you! Join top university students at Honeywell Technologies!

You will participate in our world class Internship Program where you’ll work through important projects and experience corporate finance at Honeywell Technologies. In addition to hands on learning, you’ll be invited to our intern event series (to learn about Honeywell, Finance / Accounting and business), social activities, and present what you accomplished during your internship to senior finance leadership. Most of our full-time hiring is done from our intern program - so take this opportunity to become part of the Honeywell Technologies Future Finance Leaders Program!

After graduation, our Future Finance Leader’s Program is a comprehensive program that combines senior leadership interaction and cross-business experience with real situations and work on important tasks and initiatives. With the ability to do these rotations in all our businesses across the United States – it is an opportunity to put academic knowledge into real-world settings within a competitive company. Honeywell Technologies Future Finance Leader Program is a unique experience that provides candidates with exposure to multiple industries and finance disciplines, offering an opportunity to build a rewarding career with the support of our world-class Finance team.

We look for top caliber students who have exhibited a superior record of achievement both within and outside the classroom, along with aggressive career goals We look for geographically mobile students for our Automation business. Summer Internships will be located at our US locations: Charlotte NC, Atlanta GA, Houston TX, and Rosemont, IL . The exact worksite location will be selected based on business requirements and the candidate’s relevant experience and qualifications.

Looking for a challenging role in an exciting company? Think Honeywell Technologies.
