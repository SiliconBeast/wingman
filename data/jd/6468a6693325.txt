Who are we?

At Tradeweb, we’re constantly moving forward. We were the first to electronify the bond market, and we’ve been innovating ever since. Come join our team and find out what it means to lead the way into the future of electronic trading. Collaborate with our creative thinkers who have the vision and the drive, and learn how you too can make an impact of your own.

What’s in it for you?

As an intern, you’ll work with our technologists and business experts to gain practical, hands-on experience that you can actually use. Our program combines traditional classroom-settings with project-based work where you can get involved with the products and services the firm is working on. Along the way, you’ll have guidance from people at all levels of the organization so you can start to build a long-lasting professional network and build relationships with the intern class through interactive outings and projects.

At the conclusion of your internship, you may be considered for full-time employment, to commence upon successful completion of your degree program.

Who will you work for?

Tradeweb’s quantitative teams sit at the intersection of artificial intelligence, data science, financial markets, and technology. These teams use advanced analytics, machine learning, and AI to solve complex problems, uncover insights from large datasets, and develop innovative solutions that support Tradeweb’s products, clients, and businesses.

As a quantitative Intern, you will gain hands-on experience applying data science and AI techniques to real-world challenges within electronic trading and financial markets. You may work on projects involving predictive modeling, machine learning, data analysis, generative AI, or the development of analytical tools and applications.

Throughout the internship, with guidance from a mentor, you’ll explore a real-world trading or business challenge using Tradeweb data, develop and evaluate an analytical solution, and present your findings to both technical and business stakeholders. Projects may include analyzing execution quality, modeling trading activity or market behavior, or evaluating the use of AI tools to solve business challenges.

About Tradeweb Markets:

Tradeweb is a global leader in electronic trading across asset classes. As financial markets become increasingly interconnected, our technology enables efficient, multi-asset trading on a global scale. We serve more than 3,000 clients in more than 85 countries, including many of the world’s largest banks, asset managers, hedge funds, insurers, corporations, and wealth managers.

Creative collaboration and sharp client focus have helped fuel our organic growth. We facilitated average daily trading volume (ADV) of more than $2.8 trillion over the past four fiscal quarters, topping $3.3 trillion in ADV for the first quarter of 2026.

Since our IPO in 2019, Tradeweb has completed four acquisitions and doubled our revenues – and 2025 was our 26th consecutive year of record revenues.

Tradeweb plays a central role in modernizing market structure by developing innovative trading protocols, embedding analytics into execution, and building technology infrastructure that supports the convergence of traditional and digitally native financial markets. Tradeweb is a great place to work, recognized in 2025 by Forbes as one of America’s Best Companies and by U.S. News & World Report as one of the Best Financial Services Companies to Work For .
