Job Responsibilities:

ABOUT THE ROLE:

An IT Support Intern provides foundational technical assistance within the IT department, helping to maintain and troubleshoot hardware, software, and network systems. This role offers hands-on experience and exposure to real-world IT operations, making it ideal for students pursuing degrees in computer science, information systems, or related fields.

YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES:

· Help Desk Support: Respond to and resolve user-submitted tickets for hardware, software, and network issues.

· Hardware Setup: Assist in configuring and deploying new employee workstations, including computers, monitors, and peripherals.

· Software Maintenance: Install, update, and patch applications and operating systems to ensure security and functionality.

· Involvement in IT Security audits including ITGC, IATC and Tisax.

· Process automation through use of scripting or AI. Use of Microsoft Copilot agents to automate daily IT tasks and reduce manual workload.

· Network Assistance: Troubleshoot connectivity problems and help users access network resources.

· Inventory Management: Maintain accurate records of IT assets including hardware, software licenses, and mobile devices.

· Documentation: Create user-friendly guides and document technical processes for internal use.

· Security Support: Assist with user account management and implementation of basic security protocols.

· Project Participation: Contribute to ongoing IT projects such as system upgrades, data migrations, and technology rollouts.

WHO WE ARE LOOKING FOR:

· High School Diploma

· Currently pursuing a degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Software Engineering, or a related field.

YOUR PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS:

· Ability to review current IT audit controls and determine methods to automate data collection and processing.

· Strong knowledge of AI models and the ability to create processes and automations to streamline IT workload.

· Strong verbal and written communication skills.

· Self-motivated and service-oriented with a strategic mindset.

· Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a team environment.

· Basic understanding of A/V integration and installation is a plus.

· Strong understanding of Microsoft Copilot agents.

· Familiarity with Microsoft Office Suite and basic troubleshooting tools.

· Exposure to Active Directory, network infrastructure, and IT ticketing systems.

· Experience with scripting (e.g., PowerShell, Bash) and database management is beneficial.

SPECIAL KNOWLEDGE / SKILLS:

· Proficient Computer knowledge and skills, Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)

· Professional Communication skills (written and verbal)

PHYSICAL DEMANDS / WORK ENVIRONMENT:

· Normal amount of sitting or standing, average mobility to move around an office environment, able to conduct normal amount of work at a computer.

· Office-based with potential for remote support tasks.

· May require flexible hours to support executive staff or urgent technical issues.

· Interns may be involved in onboarding activities and walk-up support traffic.
