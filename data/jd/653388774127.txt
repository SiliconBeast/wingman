Job Title

Start Date

June 2027

Location

Blackstone Technology & Innovations (BXTI)

Blackstone Technology & Innovations (BXTI) is the technology organization powering one of the world's leading alternative asset managers. We build the platforms, applications, data systems, and automation capabilities that help Blackstone operate at scale, make investment decisions, manage risk, and deliver value to investors and portfolio companies.

At BXTI, engineers work directly on business-critical systems that support Blackstone's global operations. Our teams are highly collaborative, entrepreneurial, and focused on solving complex problems with technology. Interns are treated as engineers from day one and are given opportunities to contribute meaningful work alongside experienced developers, architects, and product teams.

Job Description:

Blackstone is seeking a Data Engineer Summer Analyst who will develop innovative technologies that are changing the Alternative Asset Management industry. You will work with other engineers and business analysts to design, build, deploy, and support ETL/ELT data pipelines and infrastructure across multiple business units. Our custom-built applications are mostly developed in Python and hosted within AWS. We take full advantage of cloud native container-based and serverless architectures. We build robust data pipelines connecting to Snowflake for data analysis and visualization. You will devise elegant solutions to Blackstone's most meaningful technical challenges and put your own best ideas to work in an entrepreneurial environment inside one of the leading companies in its industry.

•
Familiarity with Snowflake, Prefect, or similar data orchestration tools

•
Object-oriented programming experience in any language (Python a plus)
