About the team
Annapurna Labs designs the custom silicon at Amazon - including Graviton (our server processors), Trainium and Inferentia (our machine learning training and inference accelerators), and the Nitro system that powers modern EC2. We own the full stack, from silicon through the software that makes it work (compilers, runtimes, drivers, and the frameworks that let customers run workloads on our hardware). As a Software Development Engineer Intern, you'll write the software that turns custom silicon into products used by millions.

Annapurna has ASIC teams across a range of products and disciplines, and we work to place you on a technical team aligned with your interests and strengths. Example teams and focus areas include:

- RTL design for machine learning accelerators (Trainium/Inferentia)
- Design verification and functional validation
- Physical design, timing closure, and place-and-route
- SoC integration and chip architecture
Throughout the interview process, we will partner with you and our hiring managers to match you to a team where your background and interests fit best, so your project is something you're genuinely excited to work on.

As an ASIC Engineer Intern, you'll contribute to real chip development work, which may include:

- Designing and implementing RTL for digital logic blocks using Verilog/SystemVerilog
- Developing and running verification environments to validate design functionality and coverage
- Supporting synthesis, timing analysis, and physical design flows
- Debugging design issues through simulation and analysis
- Collaborating with architecture, verification, and physical design teams to take features from specification to silicon
- Documenting your design decisions and presenting your work to the team

You'll be paired with a mentor and manager who will help scope your project, support your growth, and set you up to deliver meaningful, measurable impact by the end of your internship.

Basic qualifications
- Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's degree program in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field, with a final graduation date of December 2027 or later
- Coursework or project experience in digital logic design and computer architecture
- Familiarity with a hardware description language (Verilog, SystemVerilog, or VHDL)
- Understanding of fundamental digital design concepts: combinational and sequential logic, finite state machines, timing, and clocking

Preferred qualifications
- Experience in practical semiconductor ASIC design work including owning end-to-end design of major SOC blocks
- Experience using multiple verification platforms: UVM test bench, FPGA, emulator, software environments, and system testing
- Experience working with fast-moving, high-performance teams and driving innovative solutions tailored to unique business environments
- Can show strong verbal and written communication skills
- Pursuing a degree with a focus on VLSI, digital design, or computer architecture
- Experience with industry-standard EDA tools (Cadence, Synopsys, or equivalent)
- Scripting experience (Python, Perl, TCL, or shell) for automation and design flows
- Prior internship, research, or lab experience in chip design, embedded systems, or hardware development
