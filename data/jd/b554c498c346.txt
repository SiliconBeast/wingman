We are seeking a Facilities Project Engineer Intern in our Broomfield, CO, location. 
 
All applicants for placement in safety-sensitive positions will be required to submit to a pre-employment drug test. 


Key Responsibilities: 
•

Create and implement project management solutions to meet the evolving needs of our facilities improvement and footprint projects

•

Create and implement change order process from internal stakeholders. Assist in change orders from both internal teams and selected contractors for leadership approval.

•

Work alongside current Project Managers, Site Operations Managers, Facilities Technicians and engineers with planning and execution of facility improvement and infrastructure projects.

•

Participate in project meetings and document action items, schedules, and key decisions.

•

Assist with contractor coordination, site walks, and construction observation activities.

•

Develop project dashboards, metrics, and presentations for leadership review.

•

Support facility planning efforts, including space utilization, infrastructure upgrades, and capital project development.

•

Complete a capstone project and present recommendations and results to site and facilities leadership at the conclusion of the internship.
