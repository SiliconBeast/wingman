About the team:
The FABRIC team is building a machine-managed AI and Operations platform that captures and codifies organizational knowledge across Compute, Storage, Mainframe, and HP environments. This platform converts institutional expertise into reusable automation, standardized workflows, and Infrastructure-as-Code patterns, accelerating operational excellence across critical payments infrastructure and traditional platforms.
Responsibilities:
- Codify organizational knowledge across Compute, Storage, Mainframe, and HP platforms into controlled AI-driven operations, creating a consistent foundation for automation, governance, and operational excellence.
- Develop and maintain a machine-managed AI & Operations platform that transforms institutional expertise into reusable automation assets, Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC) patterns, and standardized workflows.
-Deliver operational dashboards that provide real-time visibility into tasks, objectives, automation adoption, platform health, workflow execution, and key performance indicators across critical payments infrastructure.
-Accelerate modernization and resiliency by enabling self-service automation, measuring outcomes through objective-based dashboards, and driving continuous improvement across traditional and mission-critical platforms.

What we’re looking for:
- Currently enrolled in a Bachelors degree, with an anticipated graduation date between December 2027 and June 2029
- Experience with Python development and scripting
- Familiarity with Ansible for automation and configuration management
- Experience using Git or other source code management tools
- Working knowledge of Unix/Linux environments
- Exposure to AI tools such as Microsoft Copilot, Claude, or similar technologies
- Preferred Experience with Terraform and infrastructure as code (IaC)
