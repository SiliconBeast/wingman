Job Description

T he Future Talent Program features internships that last up to 12 weeks and will include one or more projects. These opportunities in our Research and Development Division can provide you with great development and a chance to see if we are the right company for your long-term goals.

We are seeking an enthusiastic and motivated undergraduate intern with a background in Data Science, Computer Science, Biomedical Engineering, or Bioinformatics, and an interest in vivo Imaging and Analysis. This internship offers a unique opportunity to contribute to the automation and standardization of optical imaging data analysis and management within a preclinical research environment, all while learning about preclinical imaging and how it impacts drug discovery.

Key Responsibilities:

•
Develop or implement automated workflows for optical imaging data processing and analysis

•
Create standardized, publication- and presentation-ready visual outputs from imaging datasets

•
Assist with data visualization and preparation of scientific materials

•
Gain hands-on experience with preclinical in vivo optical imaging workflows

•
Collaborate with scientific team members to identify opportunities for process improvement

Required Skills:

•
Experience with Python, R, MATLAB, or similar tools

•
Exposure to image processing or scientific data workflows

Preferred Skills:

•
Experience with data management, documentation, or workflow standardization

•
Interest in preclinical research and biomedical imaging

Please note that this position may be closed before the posted end date or may remain open longer, at the discretion of the company.
