Aerospace Corporation is hiring Cloud Solutions Engineer / Site Reliability Grad level Interns for the Cloud Platforms and Architectures Department in the Information Systems and Cyber Division (ISCD). Opportunities are available in Chantilly, VA, El Segundo, CA, or Colorado Springs, CO.

Job Summary

Information Systems and Cyber Division (ISCD) staff couple the latest in information system technologies, such as elastic compute clouds, containerization, microservices, real-time operating systems, and visualization frameworks, with expertise in cyber security, software architecture, software engineering, data science, Artificial Intelligence, process improvement, and software development to deliver responsive, resilient, high-performance Cloud systems and architectures to our Intelligence Community, DoD, and civilian customers.

The Cloud Platforms and Architectures Department is seeking applicants with a passion to learn cloud systems and solutions, focused on research and development with state-of-the-art technologies. Interns will be given the ability to work on multiple projects over the summer, covering different customers, focus areas, and tools. Internship tasking is intended to allow students to complement their academic studies with hands-on, career-building experience.

Position will be 100% onsite at our office in Chantilly, VA, El Segundo, CA, or Colorado Springs, CO.

What You’ll Be Doing  

• Perform software and hardware development for various cloud-related rapid prototyping activities

• Research and experiment with state-of-the-art software and hardware technologies

• Manage Linux systems to provide Software-as-a-Service (SaaS), Platform-as-a-Service (PaaS), and Infrastructure-as-a-Service (IaaS) capabilities

• Implement DevOps technologies to streamline development, testing, build, and deployment of applications

• Leverage Cloud tools and platforms, such as Kubernetes and Cloud services from Amazon Web Service (AWS), Microsoft Azure, and/or Google Cloud Platform (GCP)

• Investigate bleeding edge cloud technologies for current and future implementation by customers in the space domain

• Receive training and mentorship from cloud and software development subject matter experts

• Learn about projects, tools, and technologies used across the enterprise

What Corporate Skills You’ll Bring

• Strong written and oral communication skills

• Must work well in a team environment

• Possess organizational, time management and project management skills

• Demonstrate flexibility and ability to adapt to changing organizational need

• Interpersonal skills to coordinate efforts and work with other internal and external organizations

What You Need to be Successful

• Currently enrolled full-time in an accredited college/university program pursuing a master’s degree in computer science, engineering, or related technical field(s) from a recognized institution

• Availability to work full-time for a minimum of 10 weeks outside of university term and ability to return to a degree program full- time after completion of the internship

• Minimum GPA of 3.0

• Software development experience with at least one major language (e.g., Python, Java, C/C++, Go, Rust, etc.)

• Familiarity with Linux/Unix systems

• Project experience with DevOps tools, such as Git, Jenkins, and/or ArgoCD

• This position requires ability to obtain and maintain a security clearance, which is issued by the US government. U.S citizenship is required to obtain a security clearance.

• Transcripts required

How You Can Stand Out

• Experience with cloud and virtualization tools, such as Docker, Kubernetes, and/or VMware technologies

• Experience with Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, and/or Google Cloud Platform (GCP)
