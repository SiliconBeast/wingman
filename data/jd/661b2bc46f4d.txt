Job Description

The IT Business Systems Intern will learn a mixture of business & D365 processes, work with the IT team to support the development and implementation of low-code/no-code business solutions using Microsoft Power Platform tools—Power Apps, Power Automate, Power BI - while gaining hands-on experience in software development, and process automation.

The right candidate is analytical by nature, has aptitude toward detail and loves a fast pace. This Intern will get the opportunity to frequently be involved hands-on in various aspects of the working with business stakeholders, mainly in the support areas of customers and sales order processing.

Key Accountabilities

•
Assist in designing, configuring, development and deploying Power Apps (Canvas and Model-driven).

•
Build and automate workflows using Power Automate.

•
Participate in Agile development cycles and team projects.

•
Conduct testing and validation of app features and data flows.

•
Document business processes, user requirements, and technical specifications.

•
Present project outcomes and recommendations to stakeholders.

•
Apply lean thinking to identify and eliminate inefficiencies

•
Create and maintain business tools that facilitate proactive decision making, effectiveness and efficiency within the team.

•
Analyze/ understand AS-IS & TO-BE model, and be an advocate for change.
