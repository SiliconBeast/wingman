As an RPA & Agentic AI Software Technologies Intern on the Digital Automation team, you will gain hands-on experience working at the intersection of Robotic Process Automation (RPA) and Agentic AI. You will collaborate with our Information business, and development teams to identify opportunities to automate and improve business processes using technologies such as UiPath, Copilot Studio, and AI/agentic automation frameworks.

This internship provides exposure to real-world enterprise automation projects and the opportunity to learn how AI agents and RPA can work together to transform business processes.

Job Description

Projects/responsibilities could include:

• Assist in designing, developing, testing, and deploying RPA solutions using UiPath, with a focus on process automation, reliability, scalability, and operational efficiency.

• Explore and assist with the development of AI-powered and agentic automation solutions, including AI agents that can interpret information, make decisions, orchestrate tasks, and interact with enterprise systems.

• Gain hands-on experience with Microsoft AI and automation technologies, such as Microsoft Agent Framework, Copilot Studio, Foundry, App Insights.

• Assist with integrating AI agents, RPA bots, APIs, applications, databases, and enterprise systems to enable end-to-end automated workflows.

• Participate in the development and testing of proofs of concept (POCs) for emerging AI and automation technologies and help evaluate their potential for enterprise adoption.

• Apply process analysis and problem-solving techniques to identify opportunities to eliminate manual work, improve cycle times, reduce errors, and enhance the user experience.

• Work on real-world automation projects throughout the software development lifecycle, including requirements gathering, solution design, development, testing, deployment, and support.

• Collaborate with cross-functional teams to gather requirements, understand user needs, contribute to UX design, and troubleshoot technical issues.

• Assist with documenting business processes, automation workflows, AI agent designs, technical specifications, test cases, and solution architecture.

• Learn and apply best practices related to responsible AI, security, governance, exception handling, monitoring, and human-in-the-loop automation.

• Stay current with emerging trends in Agentic AI, RPA, generative AI, low-code development, and intelligent automation, and recommend innovative ideas for improving business processes.

Qualifications:

• Junior standing with a minimum 3.0 GPA preferred.

• Background in Computer Science, Information Systems, Software Engineering, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence or related field

• Strong interest in automation, artificial intelligence, RPA, or intelligent automation

• Basic understanding of software development concepts, including APIs, databases, application integration, and software development lifecycle (SDLC)

• Familiarity with programming languages such as Python, JavaScript, C#, or similar languages is a plus

• Prior experience with UiPath, Copilot Studio, or similar platforms is beneficial but not required

• Interest or coursework in Generative AI, machine learning, natural language processing, or AI agents is a plus.

• Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to break down complex business processes into logical steps.

• Ability to learn new technologies quickly and apply them to real-world problems.

• Strong communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work effectively with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

• Positive attitude, curiosity, and willingness to experiment with emerging AI and automation technologies.
