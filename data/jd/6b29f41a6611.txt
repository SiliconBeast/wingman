The Allied Solutions internship program is a robust and consistent internship program for juniors and seniors. The program will focus on learning and development as well as hands-on experience. The program will immerse the intern into a broad range of responsibilities, allowing you to execute projects within your area of professional interests.

Interested applicants must be available during the 12-week program (May 17th – Aug. 6th, 2027). This is a full-time internship (40-hours per week) based at our headquarters in Carmel, IN.

The Sales Analytics Intern at Allied Solutions will gain hands-on experience working with sales data to uncover insights that drive strategy and performance. The intern will support pipeline management, help build visual dashboards, and collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure data accuracy and usability. This role is ideal for someone who enjoys working with data, solving problems, and contributing to impactful business decisions in a dynamic, team-oriented environment.

Job Duties and Responsibilities ​

• Assist with pipeline management by updating and maintaining sales activity data across products

• Learn how to clean and organize data for accuracy and consistency across reports

• Support the design of Power BI dashboards to visualize sales trends, volumes, and account activity

• Review pipeline data to spot patterns and trends that can inform sales strategies

• Translate findings into clear summaries and visuals for internal presentations and reports

• Contribute ideas for streamlining processes to make data entry and reporting more efficient

• Collaborate with Sales Enablement and Analytics Consultants and stakeholders to ensure data reflects current opportunities

• Gain hands-on exposure to reporting tools (Smartsheet, Excel, Power BI) to support decision-making
