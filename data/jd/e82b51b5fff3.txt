Electrical Engineering candidate willing to be a part of a dynamic electronic repair facility supporting manufacturing equipment. The purpose of the job is to repair, upgrade, modify, or verify sub components of a wide variety of complex automatic/logic controlled electronic or electro-mechanical equipment at a component level.

Important duties include:

• Using advanced diagnostic techniques;

• improvising circuits or equipment to special needs;

• working independently on assigned projects;

• performing complex analysis of major malfunctions to the component level;

• performing calibrations traceable to the National Bureau of Standards.

Important skills and abilities include:

• setting up, operating and adjusting complex instrumentation;

• knowledge of analog, digital, and RF electronics;

• moderate circuit design abilities;

• high level of mechanical ability (i.e. considerable knowledge of pneumatics, hydraulics and fluidics, and safe working procedures)

Qualifications:

• In the process of completing BS Electrical Engineering Degree with a focus on electronics

• Capable of working on projects without supervision

• Fundamental knowledge of AC and DC electronics

• Root cause problem solving skills

• Collaborative thinker

• Organized - project management

• Ability to effectively communicate findings, train others, and write documents

In order to be considered for a summer internship with NXP you must be returning to school or graduating at the conclusion of the internship term. If you are graduating prior to July 2027 please apply to Entry Level full-time roles.
