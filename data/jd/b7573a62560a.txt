Job Description

RBC Wealth Management - US is seeking talent to join the 2027 Wealth Management Summer Internship Program.

Business Overview

RBC Wealth Management – U.S. is a full-service investment, advisory, and wealth management firm, providing clients with tailored financial strategies to meet their personal, family, and business goals. With approximately 2,200 financial advisors in the U.S. and $4.1+ trillion in global assets under administration, we provide comprehensive financial solutions tailored to our clients' unique goals and life stages.

We serve clients across multiple segments—from individuals and families building wealth, to business owners navigating succession planning, to high-net-worth clients managing multi-generational wealth through our RBC Private Wealth and RBC Echelon™ programs. Our mission is to help clients pursue their financial goals with confidence by delivering personalized wealth planning, sophisticated investment strategies, estate and trust services, lending solutions, and insurance and retirement planning.

We accomplish this by:

• Building lasting client relationships grounded in client-first stewardship and integrity

• Creating customized wealth plans aligned with each client's values, aspirations, and financial objectives

• Helping clients strategically build, preserve, manage, and transition their wealth across generations

• Leveraging global resources, multidisciplinary expertise, and advanced financial planning tools

• Offering comprehensive solutions spanning investments, advisory programs, lending, insurance, and fiduciary services

Program Overview

RBC Wealth Management is committed to attracting, developing, and retaining exceptional students interested in a career in Wealth Management. Our goal is to build a strong pipeline of future talent and strengthen our workforce across Wealth Management divisions.

Over the ten-week summer program, participants will have the opportunity to:

Gain hands-on experience: Work alongside high-performing teams in your assigned business area, gaining practical exposure to wealth advisory, portfolio management, financial planning, operations, compliance, or client services. You will develop analytical capabilities and professional communication skills through active involvement in day-to-day projects and client-focused initiatives.

Learn from RBC leadership: Participate in virtual and in-person speaker series featuring RBC executives who share insights on career development, industry trends, and pathways to success in wealth management.

Build your professional network: Engage with fellow summer interns and full-time team members through networking events and community involvement opportunities, creating meaningful connections and expanding your professional circle.

Multiple intern positions are available across RBC Wealth Management's businesses, offering hands-on experience in wealth advisory, investment management, financial analysis, estate and trust services, business operations, and strategic projects in both client-facing and operational environments.

WHAT DO YOU NEED TO SUCCEED?

• Bachelor’s Degree with a focus in Business, Economics, Finance, Mathematics, or related field

• Graduating December 2027 or Spring 2028

• Basic market knowledge and a demonstrated keen interest in the financial markets

• Strong analytical and personal skills

• Demonstrated problem-solving skills, business sense and the ability to multi-task and prioritize in high pressure environments

• Proficient with the use of Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook

• Knowledge and experience in one or more of the following areas recommended: Customer Service, Accounting, Financial Planning/Analysis, Data Entry

WHAT’S IN IT FOR YOU?

We thrive on the challenge to be our best, think progressively, continue our growth, and deliver trusted advice to help our clients thrive and communities prosper. We care about each other, reaching our individual/collective potential, making a difference to our communities, and achieving success that is mutual.
