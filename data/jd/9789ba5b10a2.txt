Summer 2027 Internship ( Must not have graduated before fall 2027.)

100% onsite

We are looking for a Validation Intern for our summer session.

Key Responsibilities:

• Support creation and execution of validation plans and perform validation activities for new silicon products, including DDR5 and DDR6 Data Buffers.

• Partner with cross-functional teams—such as Design, Test, and Marketing—to interpret product requirements and develop robust validation strategies.

• Operate lab equipment, including oscilloscopes, signal generators, BERT, DTG, and other instrumentation, to conduct electrical characterization and performance measurements.

• Prepare detailed test documentation, including procedures, results, and engineering reports, while contributing to the ongoing refinement of validation methodologies.

• Participate in silicon debug efforts, collaborating closely with Design and Test Engineers to identify root causes and implement effective corrective actions.

• Support the development and optimization of automated test frameworks and validation scripts to increase efficiency and streamline validation workflows.

• Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related discipline. Must not have graduated before fall 2027.

• Availability for a full-time internship during the summer term.

• Strong foundation in digital and analog circuit design, semiconductor device fundamentals, and programming

• Familiarity with lab equipment and measurement techniques used in silicon validation, including Oscilloscopes, Power Supplies, and Signal Generators.

• Proficiency in Python for test automation, data analysis, and scripting.

• Hands-on experience with Python-based automation of lab instruments such as programmable Power Supplies, Multimeters, and Oscilloscopes.

• Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities, with the capability to diagnose complex technical issues and propose effective solutions.

• Excellent communication and teamwork skills, with a proven ability to collaborate across multidisciplinary teams.

• Demonstrated eagerness to learn, adapt, and apply new technologies and methodologies in silicon validation.
