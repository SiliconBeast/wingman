The Role

Fidelity is seeking to hire a Quantitative Analyst Intern to join its Quantitative Research and Investments division. The intern will work alongside quants, portfolio managers, and traders on the Fixed Income team to develop quantitative techniques, models, and tools that enhance the investment process.

This is a unique opportunity to work alongside high-caliber investment professionals on one of the world's leading fixed income investment teams. The ideal candidate will demonstrate strong academic performance, exceptional analytical and communication skills, and unwavering integrity. Success in this role requires creative thinking, independent decision-making, and the ability to develop and clearly communicate well-researched investment recommendations.

Key Responsibilities

• Understand, maintain, and improve analytical infrastructure supporting the investment process

• Collaborate closely with other investment and technology professionals across the division

• Communicate complex quantitative concepts to non-technical audiences

• Demonstrate initiative and leadership in advancing investment research and processes

The Expertise and Skills You Bring

• Master's or PhD student graduating in late 2027 or early 2028 with a degree in financial mathematics, engineering, science, or a related quantitative field

• Proficiency in Python (or similar modeling language) and SQL

• Familiarity with fixed income markets and analytics

• Strong analytical, quantitative, and problem-solving skills

• Structured thinking and intellectual curiosity

• Effective communicator with the ability to explain complex ideas clearly

• Collaborative team player with a passion for investing and financial markets

The Team

Quantitative Research and Investing (QRI) is an investments and research division within Asset Management at Fidelity. QRI is responsible for managing and developing quantitative and hybrid quant/fundamental investment strategies and solutions while also providing high quality quantitative, data-driven research to Fidelity’s investment professionals, ensuring they have access to the most relevant data and advanced quantitative analysis.

This role supports Fidelity's Fixed Income division, which is one of the leading managers of bond and money market instruments with over $2 trillion in fixed income assets. The Fixed Income team combines fundamental research on domestic and international debt issuers with macroeconomic and quantitative analysis to develop investment decisions that stay true to each portfolio’s defined investment mandate, while aiming to maximize risk-adjusted total return for our clients.
