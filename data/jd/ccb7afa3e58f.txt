Job Description

As an Embedded Engineering Co-Op you will work alongside full time employees and industry professionals improving existing products in an agile environment over 7 months from May or June 2027 - December 2027 or Jan 2027 - August 2027. This position requires you to take an academic term off of school to work full time in this role for 7 months.

Your Responsibilities:

You will work alongside industry professionals developing new and existing products in an agile environment. Each co-op has a dedicated mentor to help navigate Rockwell, ramp up in our code base, and remove daily blockers. If you enjoy technical challenges where software and hardware come together, our embedded teams are the place for you. You will be exposed to a variety of software engineering knowledge areas including: OOD/OOA, full development life cycle, agile methodology, system design, test driven development, test automation, and build and configuration management.

Experience with C++ and Python
