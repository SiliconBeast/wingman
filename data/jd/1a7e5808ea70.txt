Job Description Summary
Looking to make a global impact and solve problems? GE Vernova Wind is offering a portfolio of proven technology and driving decarbonization by delivering reliable, affordable, sustainable, renewable energy for our customers and communities around the world.

Job Description

What impact you’ll make:

If you love technology, sustainability, and want a career in engineering, an internship in Wind at GE Vernova is for you! As a Wind Engineering Intern, you will be an active contributor to our commitment to safety, high-quality scalable workhorse products, and decarbonizing the globe. Engineering Interns are the pipeline for Wind Edison Engineering Development Program (EEDP).

Wind Engineering Interns experience technically challenging, meaningful, and creative assignments. As an intern, you'll have the opportunity to work on one of a variety of high priority teams, including product design, systems, component design (such as towers, machine head, power conversion), controls and operability, advanced technology and manufacturing, testing and validation, aerodynamics, performance, quality, services, cost-out, and more.

Engineering Interns are the pipeline for the GE Vernova Wind Edison Engineering Development Program (EEDP). Wind EEDP graduates have the technical and business foundation to proactively and continually make creative contributions to GE Vernova's future. Interns who are eligible candidates (rising undergraduate seniors or graduating master’s candidates) will be able to interview for Wind EEDP while they are interns at GE Vernova. All internships will take place between May and August 2027.
