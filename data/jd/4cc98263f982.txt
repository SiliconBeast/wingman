About the Role

As an Information Technology Intern, you will gain hands-on experience supporting technology solutions that enable business operations and drive organizational efficiency. Working alongside experienced IT professionals, you will contribute to software development, systems support, automation, infrastructure initiatives, and technical problem-solving while developing practical skills in information technology and digital transformation.

Key Responsibilities

• Assist in the design, development, testing, and maintenance of software applications, automation solutions, and IT systems.

• Support troubleshooting, debugging, and resolution of technical issues across applications, systems, and infrastructure.

• Collaborate with cross-functional teams to gather requirements and support technology projects.

• Contribute to system implementation, configuration, testing, and documentation activities.

• Help analyze business processes and identify opportunities for process improvement and automation.

• Participate in the development and maintenance of technical documentation, reports, and system specifications.

• Support ongoing operational activities and adapt to changing business and technology needs.

Must Have Skills

• Basic understanding of software development, information systems, or computer engineering principles.

• Knowledge of programming concepts, data structures, algorithms, and software design fundamentals.

• Familiarity with version control tools such as Git or similar platforms.

• Strong analytical, problem-solving, and troubleshooting skills.

• Effective written and verbal communication skills.

• Ability to work collaboratively in a team environment and manage multiple priorities.

• Eagerness to learn new technologies, tools, and business processes.

Preferred Education and Experience

• Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's Degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Computer Engineering, Information Systems, or a related field.

• Experience through coursework, projects, research, or internships involving software development, automation, databases, systems administration, cloud technologies, or IT support.

• Familiarity with one or more programming languages such as Python, Java, C#, C++, JavaScript, or similar.

• Exposure to operating systems, networking concepts, databases, or enterprise applications.

• Interest in software development, infrastructure, automation, data analytics, cybersecurity, or other IT disciplines.
