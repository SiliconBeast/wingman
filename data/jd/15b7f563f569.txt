Job Responsibilities:

About the Role:

As an ADAS Functions Software Development Working Student, you will work closely on cutting-edge ADAS driving functions such as Braking, Lateral Control, and Adaptive Cruise Control (ACC), as well as Perception Functions and customer-delivered software modules integrated into the MCU environment.

Your Main Responsibilities:

•
Contribute to the development and enhancement of advanced driving, perception, and customer-focused software functions.

•
Analyze, optimize, and support software development tools such as ARXML Converter and Contract Header Generator used within the CFB environment.

•
Help shape and expand a common software repository for next-generation Driving Functions.

•
Support the setup, maintenance, and continuous improvement of development hardware benches and Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) environments.

Who we are looking for:

•
Strong programming skills in Embedded C with a passion for software development.

•
Practical experience with Git and GitHub in collaborative development environments.

•
Solid Python programming skills and interest in automation and tool development.

•
Strong analytical thinking and problem-solving capabilities.

•
Confident communication skills in English, both written and spoken; German is a plus.

•
Curiosity, initiative, and a strong desire to continuously learn and grow.

•
A collaborative mindset and enthusiasm for working with international teams across Europe, Asia, and the USA.

•
Ability to manage multiple priorities in a dynamic and fast-paced environment.

•
Self-motivated working style combined with strong teamwork abilities.

•
High level of commitment, energy, and willingness to take on new challenges.

Your preferred qualifications:

•
Basic knowledge of AUTOSAR OS, RTE, and CDD configurations.

•
Hands-on experience with EB tresos tools and AUTOSAR Builder.

•
Understanding of AUTOSAR Application Software Component (APP SWC) configurations.

•
Experience with software debugging using WINIDEA and/or TRACE32.

•
Familiarity with software build environments and tools such as Make and CMake.
