Business Unit/Role Specific Info

Global Commercial Services (GCS) serves millions of business customers around the world, from mom-and-pop shops to Fortune 500 companies. We back businesses so they can do more business, with a mission to be the undisputed leader in financial and membership services - responsibly driving double-digit revenue growth. We do that by offering a diverse range of payment and cashflow tools, from a wide range of traditional card products, to working capital and supply chain financing, to new digital solutions that make it easy for our customers to manage a full range of their financial and payment needs.

How will you make an impact in this role?

We’re looking for graduate level interns to support the Global Commercial Services (GCS) product vision as a Product Development Intern. The successful candidate will own the product strategy for one of our commercial banking, cash flow management, or payments products. Product Managers at American Express play a central role identifying customer needs, building outstanding experiences, and leading teams to manage and market these products. This is a fast-paced team driving the development of commercial customer solutions at American Express.

As a GCS Product Development Intern, you will:

•
Develop business cases for new products, features, and customer experiences.

•
Shape product marketing strategies to drive customer engagement and new growth.

•
Derive insights from the competitive landscape and customer feedback.

•
Synthesize analytics and financials to develop data-driven recommendations, define success metrics, and evaluate product performance

•
Support product development activities by documenting requirements, coordinating cross-functional deliverables, and maintaining product documentation and backlogs.

•
Collaborate with cross-functional teams including Marketing, Finance, Technology, Legal, Compliance, Design, and other stakeholders to support the development, launch, and enhancement of commercial products.
