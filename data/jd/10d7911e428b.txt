About the role:

Our R&D engineers are tasked with designing and developing implant products for the Active Implantable Systems (AIS) group. This includes technologies that monitor, support diagnosis and treat irregular heart rhythms, heart failure, and sudden cardiac arrest. AIS provides devices and therapies for a patient’s entire continuum of care, from diagnostic to treatment. This division strives to innovate and create a paradigm shift that can solve unmet clinical needs and change what is possible for patients.

Your responsibilities will include:

• Collaborate on the design, development, and testing of prototype medical devices informed by clinical feedback, while contributing to software solutions using Java as a primary language and frameworks like Spring, Hibernate, and J2EE.

• Analyze large clinical databases to extract insights that guide device design and procedural improvements, applying SQL and backend development skills to support data-driven decision-making.

• Observe live and recorded clinical procedures to replicate real-world use through custom test methods, while supporting full-stack development using JavaScript, HTML, and CSS.

• Assist in deploying and scaling applications in cloud environments using AWS and Kubernetes, contributing to infrastructure that supports both software tools and device testing workflows
