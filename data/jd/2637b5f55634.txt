The Customer Analytics Summer Intern will support the Customer Analytics team in delivering customer insights, business reporting, data analysis, and strategic research programs. You will provide hands-on experience working with customer, marketing, and customer experience data while helping support main business questions related to customer growth, retention, and engagement.

Responsibilities ​

• Help maintain customer analytics reporting, and recurring business insights used by leadership and partners.

• Help with data validation, quality assurance, and documentation activities as we continue to expand analytics capabilities within Google Cloud Platform (GCP).

• Partner with analysts to conduct ad hoc analyses, build presentations, and develop visualizations that communicate customer and performance.

• Support process improvement and automation plans designed to improve reporting efficiency, data accessibility, and analytical consistency.

Requirements ​

• Bachelor's or Master's degree in Analytics, Data Science, Statistics, Economics, Mathematics, or a related quantitative field.

• Experience working with data in Excel and at least one analytical or reporting tool (SQL, Tableau, Power BI, Python, R, or similar).

• Coursework or academic experience involving data analysis, statistics, forecasting, research methods, or data visualization.

• Experience with relational databases, data structures, and data quality concepts.

• Experience creating charts, dashboards, reports, or presentations that communicate data-driven insights.
