At Belvedere Trading, Quantitative Trading Interns are some of the industry’s highly motivated most quantitative problem solvers. They have a love for financial markets and enjoy working in teams to learn about various trading concepts. Our Quantitative Trading Interns participate in a hands-on rotation between Belvedere’s trading desks and collaborate with Developers and Strategists in research to improve our trading systems. Our Interns are at the forefront of technology immersion as they learn best practices in software development and focus on projects related to trading logic development, electronic trading strategies, and quantitative research. If you love technology and financial markets, then this position might be for you. 
 
Don’t know a lot about trading? No sweat. Learn as much as you can in the meantime (check out our FAQ page for some resources to get you started) but know our Belvedere Trading University (BTU) program will teach you everything you need to know once you join the team.


What our Quantitative Trading Interns do 
•

Daily trading activities – Assist with analyzing daily trades and recapping significant trends

•

Rotations – Rotate between product groups to gain a broad understanding and knowledge of the market and Belvedere’s trading strategies

•

Desk strategy – Engage in daily collaborative strategy adjustments with your team to adapt to market dynamics

•

Research – Participate in quantitative research and the application of quantitative strategies while learning concepts behind our core market making operations

•

Data Analysis – Analyze extensive datasets to extract insights that bolster our trading strategies

•

Training – Benefit from our industry-leading BTU program, complemented by ongoing practical training within our trading operations, covering a spectrum from foundational knowledge to advanced proprietary concepts.
