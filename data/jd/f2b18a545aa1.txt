What impact you’ll make

As a student of the HDNU Global Operations, you will be an active contributor to the digitization of AI prompts supporting one of the most critical business department: the Worldwide Project Execution community. You will play a key role in boosting the usage of the AI (Artificial Intelligence) to promote operational efficiency and innovation across the organization.

Job Description

What you’ll do (Job Responsibilities)

• Support the design, development, and digitization of AI prompts tailored to Project Management workstreams

• Collaborate with global, cross-functional teams to identify key processes suitable for AI-driven automation and optimization

• Assist in mapping current business processes and translating them into structured AI prompt frameworks

• Conduct research on AI best practices and emerging prompt engineering techniques relevant to operations

• Test and validate AI prompt outputs to ensure accuracy, consistency, and alignment with business objectives

• Document prompt libraries, workflows, and standard operating procedures for future scalability

• Support the AI Big Swing initiative by tracking progress, reporting on milestones, and identifying improvement opportunities

• Participate in team meetings and contribute ideas for continuous improvement of AI-enabled processes

• Prepare presentations and reports to communicate project outcomes to Project Execution Leadership

What you’ll bring (Basic Qualifications)

• Currently enrolled in an undergraduate or graduate degree program in Project Management, Engineering, Computer Science, Data Science or a related field

Eligibility Requirements

• Must be available for a minimum of 6 months

• Must have valid work authorization for the country of employment

What will make you stand out (Desired Qualifications)

• Strong interest in digital transformation, and process optimization

• AI early adopter with proven examples of usage of AI technology

• Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite (Excel, PowerPoint, Word)

• Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with attention to details

• Effective written and verbal communication skills in English

• You have a passion for AI innovation and digital transformation in industrial operations

• You are someone who brings a proactive, self-starter mindset with the ability to work independently and in global, cross-functional teams

• You have experience with AI tools, prompt engineering, or process automation platforms

• Familiarity with Project Management execution processes
