Internship Program Overview:

The Polaris D&IT Predictive Data Science Internship is a 12-week paid summer internship designed for emerging juniors and seniors interested in applying data science, analytics, and AI to solve real business challenges within the global powersports industry.

As a Predictive Data Science Intern, you will work with large datasets to uncover insights, build predictive models, and develop data-driven recommendations that support business decisions across Polaris. Projects may include analyzing customer, dealer, supply chain, and operational data to identify trends, improve forecasting, optimize processes, and drive business performance.

You will partner with cross-functional teams across analytics, technology, operations, and the business to turn data into actionable solutions and meaningful outcomes.

Potential Project Areas :

•
Predictive analytics and forecasting

•
Machine learning and AI solutions

•
Data management and engineering

•
Business intelligence, data visualization, and decision support

Internship Location: D&IT interns are typically based at Polaris Headquarters in Medina, MN, where they collaborate closely with technology, analytics, and business teams across the organization.

The Selection Process:

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis throughout the fall. Selected candidates will be invited to participate in a phone screen followed by a virtual interview with Polaris HR leaders. Candidates who successfully complete the interview process may receive an internship offer. Internship assignments are finalized in February based on candidate interests, strengths, and business needs .

Intern Relocation Assistance:

Polaris provides relocation support for eligible interns working 35+ miles from their assigned location. Benefits may include a relocation stipend and assistance with securing temporary housing accommodations. Interns are responsible for daily transportation to and from their work location.

About Polaris

As the global leader in powersports, Polaris Inc. (NYSE: PII) pioneers product breakthroughs and enriching experiences and services that have invited people to discover the joy of being outdoors since our founding in 1954. Polaris' high-quality product line-up includes the Polaris RANGER®, RZR® and Polaris GENERAL™ side-by-side off-road vehicles; Sportsman® all-terrain off-road vehicles; military and commercial off-road vehicles; snowmobiles; Slingshot® moto-roadsters; Aixam quadricycles; Goupil electric vehicles; and pontoon and deck boats, including industry-leading Bennington pontoons. Polaris enhances the riding experience with a robust portfolio of parts, garments, and accessories. Proudly headquartered in Minnesota, Polaris serves more than 100 countries across the globe. www.polaris.com
