Summary   

CAE USA, Defense & Security requires exceptional engineering development candidates who will create and improve Weapons System solutions for real-time aircraft simulation & training. This is a hands-on software engineering internship position.

Essential Duties and Responsibilities   

Reasonable accommodations may be made to enable individuals with disabilities to perform the essential functions.  

• Learn from a diverse and driven team of skilled engineers

• Apply computer science, engineering, and mathematical analysis concepts and principles

• Create new weapon simulation solutions using both standard and innovative modeling techniques

• Design, develop, code, integrate and test Weapon simulation solutions to meet system requirements

• Maintain, upgrade, and improve existing Weapon simulations

• Collaborate with experienced engineers to solve complex problems in a fast-paced, agile environment

• Develop software test procedures, software programs and related documentation

• Utilize modeling tools to conduct experimental tests and evaluate data

• Perform integration and configuration of weapon simulations

Qualifications and Education Requirements   

• Bachelor’s degree (in progress) in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, Physics, Game Development or related field

• Provide positive contributions, energy and directed curiosity as part of a highly collaborative team

• Programming or software development experience

• Ability to operate in a fast -paced, mission critical environment

• Dedication to continuous learning

• Due to U.S. Government contract requirements, only U.S. citizens are eligible for this role.  

Preferred Skills   

• One or more of the following programming languages:  C++, FORTRAN, Ada, Python

• Linux OS

• MATLAB

• Gaming engine experience (Unreal / Unity / other)

• Docker
