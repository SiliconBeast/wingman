Job Description:

Intel is seeking a motivated Module Engineering Intern to join our dynamic semiconductor manufacturing team. In this role, you will support the development and implementation of manufacturing processes for specialized modules, contributing directly to Intel's production of world-class semiconductor products. Through hands-on experience with cutting-edge equipment and process optimization, you will collaborate with engineering teams to address challenges, explore innovative solutions, and play a key role in shaping the future of technology.

Business group
As part of Intel Foundry, you will join a team dedicated to advancing semiconductor technology and manufacturing excellence. Our group focuses on optimizing processes and equipment for high-quality production, supporting Intel's broader goals of delivering innovative solutions for global technology challenges.

Key Responsibilities
- Assist in the development and optimization of manufacturing processes for specific modules used in semiconductor production.
- Support equipment troubleshooting, maintenance, and qualification activities to ensure operational excellence.
- Collaborate with engineering teams to sustain and improve manufacturing processes and equipment.
- Apply process characterization methodologies and Design of Experiments (DOE) principles to enhance quality and production efficiency.
- Conduct structured data analysis and experiments to identify areas for process improvement.
- Participate in hands-on engineering projects and activities in a cleanroom environment.

- Perusing a PhD in Mechanical Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Materials Science, Chemistry, Polymer Engineering, or a related field .

3+ months of experience listed above should be in one or more of the following:
- Experience with semiconductor manufacturing equipment and process development methodologies.

Preferred Qualifications

- Hands-on experience with process optimization, troubleshooting engineering tools, and cleanroom environments.
- Familiarity with DOE principles, structured problem-solving techniques, and statistical data analysis.
