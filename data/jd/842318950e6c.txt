About the team:
The Data Center and Network Engineer is responsible for delivering reliable, scalable, and cost-effective network and data center infrastructure solutions in a complex, multi-vendor, multi-platform environment. This role supports the design, implementation, maintenance, and optimization of enterprise network and data center technologies while ensuring operational excellence within a mission-critical production environment.
The position requires a hands-on technical professional with expertise in network communications, hardware installation, infrastructure support, and incident resolution. The engineer will lead and participate in regional infrastructure projects, managing planning, resource utilization, implementation activities, and operational support while maintaining high standards of quality, reliability, and performance.
Working independently and collaboratively, the successful candidate will evaluate and resolve complex technical challenges, integrate emerging technologies into the production environment, and support continuous improvement initiatives across network, server, and storage platforms. Strong communication skills, attention to detail, and a commitment to established processes are essential, along with the ability to mentor team members and contribute to a high-performing engineering organization.
Key areas of focus include network design and optimization, data center operations, enterprise hardware deployment, project execution, technology integration, and operational support of critical infrastructure services. The ideal candidate is a self-motivated professional with a passion for technology, strong troubleshooting capabilities, and the ability to thrive in a fast-paced, customer-focused environment.

What we’re looking for:
- Currently enrolled in a Bachelors degree, with an anticipated graduation date between December 2027 and June 2028
- Demonstrated strong attention to detail with the ability to produce accurate, high-quality work.
- Strong organizational, process-oriented, and documentation skills, with the ability to follow and improve established procedures.
- Analytical and problem-solving mindset with the ability to break down complex challenges into manageable solutions.
- Exposure to a variety of technical, academic, project, or extracurricular experiences that demonstrate curiosity and adaptability.
- Self-motivated learner who is eager to gain new experiences, take on diverse assignments, and contribute in a dynamic team environment.
