Position Title : Embedded Software Co-op

Number of Positions : 2

Date : January - May - August - December 2027

Duration : Winter/Summer/Fall (4-8-12-16 Months)

Location : Hybrid - Sunnyvale, CA

Educational Requirements:

•
Currently enrolled in an accredited U.S. university pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Engineering or Electrical Engineering, with a preference for 3rd- or 4th-year undergraduate students and Master's students.

•
Develop and maintain embedded software and firmware using C/C++ for optical transport systems.

•
Design, implement, integrate, and verify real-time embedded solutions from requirements through deployment.

•
Support development on RTOS-based platforms, including low-level drivers, interrupt handling, and control firmware.

•
Collaborate with cross-functional teams to integrate software with hardware, ARM processors, and FPGA-based components.

•
Troubleshoot and resolve complex system-level issues involving software, hardware, optics, and multi-processor architectures.

•
Experience developing embedded software in C/C++ on RTOS-based platforms.

•
Knowledge of firmware development, interrupt handling, low-level drivers, and timing-critical systems.

•
Experience working with ARM processors, hardware interfaces, and multi-processor architectures.

•
Familiarity with FPGA/CPLD integration and hardware-software interaction.

•
Strong troubleshooting, problem-solving, and teamwork skills.

It would be nice if you had:

• Experience with embedded Linux or other operating systems used in embedded development.

• Exposure to optical networking, telecommunications, or networking protocols.

• Experience using debugging tools, version control systems, and software development best practices.
