POSITION SUMMARY

The ERS Business Analytics Intern will work cross-functionally across the organization to develop reporting and dashboards that will inform business decisions regarding service delivery, resource planning, and pipeline management. They will assist with needed business analysis and development of dashboards and reporting to drive business process improvement and efficiency

​​ RESPONSIBILITIES

• Analyze internal processes to identify opportunities for efficiency in service delivery and execution

• Develop AI agents to automate repetitive and manual tasks

• Improve reporting on pipeline management

• Develop models to improve resource planning and utilization

​ COMPETENCIES

To perform the job successfully, an individual should demonstrate the following competencies:

• Experience with various AI models

• Ability to work collaboratively with team and remote resources

• Strong analytical skills to problem-solve challenges and bring new ideas to the team

• Strong written and oral communication skills / presentation skills

• Ability to work independently, can prioritize their time, and take initiative

• Eager to learn and learns quickly in a fast-paced environment

• Ability to manage multiple priorities in dynamic environment

• Proficient in Microsoft Office suite of products – Word, PPT, Excel; PowerBI experience a plus

• Performs with integrity and establishes themselves as a trusted member of the team

Qualifications

Education and/or Experience

• Business Analytics, Data Science, or Business major with AI experience through employment or organizational involvement

About Vertiv

Vertiv (NYSE: VRT) brings together hardware, software, analytics and ongoing services to enable its customers’ vital applications to run continuously, perform optimally and grow with their business needs. Vertiv solves the most important challenges facing today’s data centers, communication networks, and commercial and industrial facilities with a portfolio of power, cooling and IT infrastructure solutions and services that extend from the cloud to the edge of the network. Headquartered in Westerville, Ohio, USA, Vertiv employs around 34,000 people and does business in more than 130 countries. Visit Vertiv.com to learn more.
