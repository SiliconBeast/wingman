Johnson & Johnson is currently seeking a Advance Development Engineering Co-Op to join our Engineering team located in Danvers, MA .

Program Dates: January 11, 2027 – June 25, 2027

Principal Duties and Responsibilities

• Assist in the design, prototyping, and testing of embedded systems focused on optical sensing technology.

• Contribute to system prototypes, integrating mechanical, electrical, and software components.

• Support hardware development for fiber optics-based systems, including PCB design and fabrication.

• Participate in experiments involving fiber optics, light-based sensors, and interferometry.

• Troubleshoot and optimize system performance in both optical and electrical systems.

• Document project work, including test plans, results, and technical reports.

• Present progress in design reviews to peers and leadership.

• Ensure project activities comply with company quality policies, regulatory standards, and design controls.

Job Qualifications

• Currently pursuing a BS or MS in Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Computer Science, Optical Engineering, or a related field.

• Interest in interdisciplinary work that combines electrical, mechanical, and software skills in embedded systems development.

• Exposure to fiber optics, light-based sensors, interferometry, or optical systems a plus

• Exposure to embedded systems, microcontrollers, or PCB design (e.g., Altium, Eagle).

• Interest or exposure to optical and electrical testing equipment such as spectrometers, light sources, and power meters.

• Hands-on experience or interest in prototyping and building mechanical or electrical systems.

• Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to work in a collaborative environment.

• Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite and basic coding skills (e.g., Python, C/C++).

• Good verbal and written communication skills in English.

Preferred Qualifications

• Experience in the medical device industry.

• Knowledge of optical design software (e.g., Zemax, Code V).

• Familiarity with PCB design software (e.g., Altium, Eagle, KiCad).

• Experience with embedded software design (e.g., STM32, Arduino, Python).

• Familiarity with fabrication tools such as 3D printers or machining.

• Experience with 3D CAD software (e.g., SolidWorks).

Permanently authorized to work in the U.S., must not require sponsorship of an employment visa (e.g., H-1B or green card) at the time of application or in the future.
