The Technology Development (TD) Intern will support the LV/MV EFK technology development team in advancing semiconductor device performance, reliability, and manufacturing capability. This role provides hands-on experience in wafer characterization, defect analysis, reliability testing, and yield improvement activities within a semiconductor development environment.
Key Responsibilities

• Support wafer inspection and defect analysis activities to identify process and device-related issues.

• Perform data analysis to help improve device yield and manufacturing performance.

• Assist with device characterization and electrical measurements at wafer level.

• Support reliability characterization activities, including:

• TDDB (Time-Dependent Dielectric Breakdown)

• WL-UIL (Wafer-Level Unclamped Inductive Load) measurements

• Other device reliability and qualification testing

• Analyze test data and prepare technical reports summarizing results and recommendations.

• Collaborate with Device, Process Integration, Product, and Reliability Engineering teams.

• Support root-cause investigations and corrective action initiatives for yield and reliability issues.

• Participate in design of experiments (DOE) and statistical analysis to optimize device performance.

• Document findings and present project updates to the engineering team.

Qualifications – External

Requirements:

Currently pursuing a Bachelor's, Master's, or Ph.D. degree in Electrical Engineering, Materials Science, Physics, Semiconductor Engineering or a related technical field.

• Basic understanding of semiconductor devices and fabrication processes.

• Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

• Experience with data analysis tools such as Excel, JMP, Python, MATLAB, or similar.

• Excellent communication and teamwork skills.
