Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) Summer 2027 Internship

Are you curious about how businesses make big decisions and turn data into action? As a Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) Intern, you'll work alongside the Tektronix Finance team to solve real business challenges that help shape the future of a global technology company. Paired with a dedicated mentor and FP&A manager, you'll develop skills in financial analysis and strategic decision-making while gaining exposure to areas such as Commercial Sales, Manufacturing, Product Development, and Service. We're looking for students who are curious, adaptable, and eager to learn by asking great questions, bringing fresh perspectives, and taking on meaningful work. You'll collaborate with cross-functional teams, contribute to high-impact projects, engage with senior leaders, and discover how finance drives business strategy. Best of all, our internship program serves as a primary pathway to full-time opportunities, giving you the chance to launch your career through our highly regarded Finance rotational program after graduation. Throughout the summer, you'll build a portfolio of meaningful accomplishments and showcase your recommendations and project outcomes to Finance leadership and the CFO.
