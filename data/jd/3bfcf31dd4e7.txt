The Fidelity Intern Program (FIDTERN Program) – Corporate Accounting

Fidelity's full-time, paid, 10-week FIDTERN Program offers students the opportunity to contribute to meaningful, real-world work and projects as valued members of a dedicated team.

Our program is designed to provide students with a firsthand experience of what it's like to work here full-time. We deliver a best-in-class program experience to ensure interns feel a sense of belonging and community while receiving critical training and development to prepare them for a future career at Fidelity.

Here's the experience you can expect:

Professional & Financial Development Sessions: Join exclusive webinars designed to equip you with essential skills for both your career and personal growth. Learn directly from senior leaders, strengthen your professional communication skills, explore workplace well-being strategies, and build financial confidence through financial literacy workshops.

Mentorship: Opt-in to our intern mentorship program and connect with experienced professionals who will support your growth and help you navigate your career journey.

Regional Events: Build connections with leaders and fellow interns through engaging onsite experiences and fun local outings.

The Role

A Corporate Accounting internship at Fidelity is designed for students interested in developing a strong foundation in accounting and financial reporting. Interns will gain hands-on experience supporting key accounting processes and partnering with accounting professionals responsible for maintaining the financial integrity of a large and complex organization.

This internship provides exposure to core accounting functions, including monthly close activities, financial statement preparation, account reconciliations, and financial reporting processes. Interns will work with accounting data, assist in preparing and analyzing financial information, and help support projects that improve efficiency and enhance reporting accuracy.

Through this experience, interns will develop an understanding of how accounting supports business operations, regulatory requirements, and strategic decision-making across Fidelity.

Day-to-day responsibilities may include:

• Supporting month-end and quarter-end close activities

• Assisting with preparation and review of financial statements and supporting schedules

• Performing account reconciliations and researching variances

• Analyzing financial data to identify trends, discrepancies, and opportunities for improvement

• Supporting process improvement and documentation initiatives

• Assisting with accounting projects related to systems, reporting, and operational efficiency

• Collaborating with accounting professionals across multiple business areas

This role provides broad exposure to traditional accounting disciplines and prepares students for future careers in corporate accounting, financial reporting, auditing, and related fields. Other opportunities may include:

• Defining and developing operational procedures for Accounting’s key process

• Leveraging technology, including automation tools, to streamline and automate manual accounting processes

• Support transformation initiatives within our investment accounting COE
