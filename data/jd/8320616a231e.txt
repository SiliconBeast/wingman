Interns are entrusted with meaningful responsibilities and exposed to cutting-edge technologies, fostering both technical skill development and professional growth.

The company’s commitment to sustainability, including its goal of achieving net-zero emissions by 2040, enables interns to engage in projects that align with global environmental priorities.

• Flexible work hours aligned with academic schedules

• Access to Employee Resource Groups (ERGs) which foster a sense of belonging and provide opportunities for mentorship and community engagement

• Networking events, professional development workshops, and project showcases that build career readiness and visibility across the organization

Internship roles span multiple disciplines including engineering, business operations, data analytics, and finance, and are designed to align with onsemi’s strategic goals of innovation and workforce development. Structured programs such as rotational field sales and finance analyst tracks offer additional career pathways for interns transitioning into full-time roles.

onsemi is seeking a self-driven and motivated intern to join their Intelligent Sensor Group (ISG) Test Engineering team, located in Nampa, Idaho.

As a Test Engineering Intern you will have the opportunity to work closely with Test, Validation, and Product Engineers in the semiconductor industry; helping to support automotive, commercial, and industrial CMOS image sensors moving through the new product development (NPD) cycle.

This opportunity will ask a student to fulfill the following responsibilities:

• Work closely with Test, Validation, and Product Engineering teams to implement production test requirements on high priority image sensor products ramping to production.

• Help develop test code and test methodologies to improve coverage and margin for pre-qualified devices testing at the manufacturing facility in Nampa, ID.

• Develop test routines for novel device features and leading edge product capabilities

• Gain experience with automated test equipment related to wafer probe and package test in a high-volume production environment

• Collaborate with domestic and international team members to solve product test issues

• Perform product debug and data analysis on test results

• Focus on strategic project aimed to help the departments become more efficient and effective

To be considered for an internship, you must be a student currently enrolled in school pursuing an accredited BS/MS degree program the following semester. Major and portfolio should be in alignment with pursuing a future career in Electrical Engineering or related field. Necessary qualifications:

• Excellent spoken and written English skills for communicating with team members from various domestic and international locations.

• Candidate is self-motivated with the ability to work independently and a strong wiliness to learn and contribute technically.

• Experience programming in high level languages such as C, C++, Python, and/or Visual Basic.

• Electric circuit coursework, preferably micro-electronics through lab and coursework.

• Ability to learn and understand electrical test equipment.

• Ability to interpret data distributions and make engineering judgements to guide debug and troubleshooting.
