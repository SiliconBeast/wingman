We are seeking an onsite Metasurface Resonant PhotonicDesign Intern in our Broomfield, CO location.


Key Responsibilities:
• Design, simulate, and optimize resonant photonic devices.

• Collaborate with larger photonics and optics teams to develop novel and impactful photonic devices

YOU MUST HAVE:
• Currently enrolled in a Physics or Engineering PhD program intended on graduating on/after August 2027

• Due to national security requirements imposed by the U.S. Government, candidates for this position must not be a People's Republic of China national or Russian national unless the candidate is also a U.S. citizen.

• Due to Contractual requirements, must be a U.S. Person. defined as, U.S. citizen permanent resident or green card holder, workers granted asylum or refugee status

WE VALUE:
• 3+ years’ experience (PhD inclusive) with computational modeling of photonic devices

• Experience with modeling and design of photonic devices using resonant mechanisms such as quasi-bound states in the continuum, Fano resonances, Mie resonances, guided mode resonances, non-local metasurfaces, etc.

• Experience with photonic modeling tools such as COMSOL (or other FEM), Lumerical, tidy3D (or other FDTD), RCWA (various)

• Experience with scientific coding tools (MATLAB, Python, etc.)

• Willingness and ability to communicate and collaborate with a larger team

• Interpersonal skills: cross-group and cross-culture collaboration

• Openness to learning new computational methods and software, and enthusiasm for problem solving

• Commitment to return to degree-program after the completion of the internship/co-op
