Your Team, Your Impact
The CnM team is responsible for the Control and management Subsystem in Marvell Switching SoCs. The CnM is the switch internal management system, incorporating the CPU subsystem, DDR, Host DMA, PCIe, USB, and Security.
The team hires some of the biggest problem solvers in Silicon and has a huge impact on the work done at Marvell. The customers served by this team are often other chip companies and big tech companies, familiar names to all candidates.

What You Can Expect

Be part of the CnM team, working on CnM IPs for the Switching projects:

• You'll be responsible for the design and verification of cutting-edge IP development

• Involve with next-generation CnM development

• Collaborate with engineers from other teams, including system, architecture, design, verification, and software.

• You will implement and perform verification different design IPs and for multidisciplinary systems (that combine hardware, firmware and software), write test documents, define testing processes and validate system requirements.
