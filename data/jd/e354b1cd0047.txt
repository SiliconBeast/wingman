This internship is a minimum of 3 months but can be extended up to a year depending on the project and candidates availability Responsibilities As a Semiconductor Advanced Packaging Process Engineering Intern within the Keysight Labs/High Frequency Technology Center, you will contribute to the development and optimization of next-generation heterogeneous integration and advanced packaging technologies. Working alongside process and manufacturing engineers, you will support the development, characterization, and continuous improvement of semiconductor assembly processes used to manufacture advanced System-in-Package (SiP) products and interposer technologies. Key Responsibilities include: Assist with the planning, execution, and documentation of engineering experiments to evaluate process, material, and equipment performance. Collect, analyze, and interpret process, metrology, and electrical test data to identify trends and improvement opportunities. Work directly with advanced semiconductor assembly equipment, including thermocompression bonding (TCB), pick-and-place, inspection, and supporting process tools. Support process development activities focused on improving assembly cycle time, throughput, yield, and process robustness. Execute hands-on equipment experiments, collect process data, and partner with engineers to identify and implement process improvements. Assist in failure analysis, defect characterization, and troubleshooting of assembly-related process issues. Develop and maintain technical documentation, including experiment plans, procedures, reports, and presentations. Present technical findings and recommendations to engineering teams and project stakeholders. Follow established safety, quality, and cleanroom operating procedures while working in a semiconductor manufacturing environment. This internship is highly hands-on and provides extensive opportunities to work directly with manufacturing equipment, engineering experiments, process characterization, and data analysis in a fast-paced R&D environment. Successful candidates are naturally curious, eager to learn, comfortable working in a laboratory or manufacturing setting, and willing to ask questions to accelerate their understanding and impact.
