JOB SUMMARY

OpenGov is on a mission to power a more effective and accountable government — and the software our engineering teams ship every day makes that real for thousands of public agencies across the country. As a Software Engineering Intern, you'll contribute to production features used by real customers, working side by side with full-time engineers across the full stack. This is a hands-on internship where you'll write code that ships, not code that sits in a sandbox. Expect to leave with a deeper understanding of how modern SaaS engineering teams build, test, and deliver enterprise-grade software at scale.



The 10-week internship will run from June 7, 2027 through August 13, 2027.




RESPONSIBILITIES

 - Build and ship features across our production codebase, contributing to web applications and APIs that serve government customers every day

 - Design, document, and implement REST API endpoints in collaboration with senior engineers, learning how scalable service contracts are built and maintained

 - Write unit and integration tests alongside feature work, developing habits around code quality and test coverage from day one

 - Participate in code reviews — both receiving and giving feedback — to sharpen your craft and understand how the team maintains a high bar for quality

 - Collaborate with engineers during sprint planning, standups, and retrospectives to experience the full rhythm of an agile engineering team

 - Investigate and resolve bugs and performance issues, tracing problems through the stack to develop debugging instincts in a real production environment

 - Explore AI-assisted development tools to accelerate your workflow and get exposure to how modern engineering teams are integrating AI into their day-to-day




REQUIREMENTS AND PREFERRED EXPERIENCE


REQUIREMENTS:

 - Currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent program

 - Proficiency in at least one modern programming language (TypeScript, JavaScript, React, or Node)

 - Experience building with AI-driven UX, agents, and workflow automation (direct ML experience not required)

 - Ability to collaborate effectively in a team environment and communicate clearly about technical problems

 - Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail — you spot edge cases and ask good questions

 - Curiosity and a self-starter mindset; eager to learn from feedback and iterate quickly


PREFERRED EXPERIENCE:

 - Familiarity with front-end frameworks such as Angular, React, or Redux

 - Basic experience with Git or similar version control tools

 - Personal or open-source projects you're proud of — GitHub link welcome

 - Strong written or verbal communication skills; bonus if you've shared your work via blogs, talks, or demos

 - GPA 3.0+
