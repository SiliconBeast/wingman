Solidigm is seeking talented and motivated undergraduate students for our Hardware & Product Development Engineering Internships in the United States. This evergreen posting supports multiple internship opportunities across hardware engineering and product development for Solidigm's data center SSD products.

As an intern, you will work on real engineering challenges supporting hardware design, evaluation, and debug, as well as new product development from concept through release.

This internship is ideal for undergraduate students who enjoy hands-on problem solving, working at the intersection of hardware and system-level design, and helping bring data center storage products from concept through development and release.

Key Responsibilities

Interns may be assigned one or more of the following responsibilities based on project needs, team priorities, and individual strengths:

• Assist with design, evaluation, and debug of hardware subsystems and components.

• Support schematic review, board-level bring-up, and hardware/firmware interaction analysis.

• Support new product development activities from concept through design, prototyping, and release.

• Help track technical inputs, product requirements, and project milestones across engineering disciplines.

• Help analyze component and system-level test data to identify trends, risks, and improvement opportunities.

• Use or help develop scripts and tools (e.g., Python) to automate data collection, analysis, and reporting.

• Collaborate with cross-functional teams including hardware, firmware, validation, and product engineering.

• Help document designs, test methods, and project deliverables.

Minimum Qualifications

• Currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Electronics, or a related technical discipline.

• Foundational coursework in electrical design, analog and digital electronics, or hardware systems.

• Exposure to one or more programming or scripting languages, such as Python.

• Familiarity with Linux-based development or lab environments.

• Strong analytical, debugging, and problem-solving skills.

• Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a technical team environment.

• Strong communication skills and eagerness to learn.

• This role is not eligible for candidates requiring VISA sponsorship.

Preferred Qualifications

• Coursework or academic project experience in electrical design, circuit analysis, or hardware/component evaluation.

• Exposure to hardware bring-up, board-level debug, or system integration.

• Interest in product development processes or cross-functional engineering coordination.

• Exposure to data analysis, scripting, or automation through coursework or projects.

• Strong interest in data center storage, SSD technology, or hardware/electronic systems.

What You'll Gain

As a Solidigm intern, you will have the opportunity to:

• Work on meaningful projects tied to real data center SSD products and engineering priorities.

• Gain hands-on experience with hardware engineering and product development workflows.

• Learn how high-performance storage products move from concept through hardware design and release.

• Collaborate with experienced engineers, technical leads, product engineers, and cross-functional teams.

• Develop practical skills in hardware evaluation, data analysis, and technical documentation.

• Present your work, receive feedback, and contribute to solutions that may influence future products.

• Grow your professional network through mentorship, team engagement, and intern program activities.

Additional Information

Work locations may include Rancho Cordova, CA; San Jose, CA; or other U.S. Solidigm locations based on business need and internship assignment.

Internship availability and project scope may vary by team, degree level, and business requirements.

All your information will be kept confidential according to EEO guidelines.
