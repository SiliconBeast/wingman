Business Unit/Role Specific Information

Enterprise Shared Services (ESS) comprises key functions that are core to the company’s operations, including Global Real Estate & Workplace Experience, Global Supply Management, Business Resilience & Corporate Functions Governance, Aviation, Internal Fraud, and Global Security. ESS helps keep our company growing and puts our colleagues at the center of everything they do by delivering best-in-class services that power safe, resilient, and efficient operations around the world.

Global Supply Management (GSM) provides procurement, third-party risk management and payment services that help American Express manage third-party relationships throughout the lifecycle. Within GSM's Third-Party Center of Excellence, the Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) team establishes and maintains the requirements, processes and oversight used to identify, assess, manage and monitor third-party risk.

How will you make an impact in this role?

As a Third-Party Risk Management Intern, you will contribute to projects involving program requirements, data analysis, reporting, process improvement, documentation, and stakeholder engagement. Through this experience, you will gain exposure to the third-party lifecycle while strengthening your analytical, communication, and project management skills. Assignments will be based on business priorities and organizational needs during the internship.

Key Responsibilities

•
Support projects and initiatives aligned with Third-Party Risk Management priorities.

•
Develop an understanding of the processes used to identify, assess, manage, and monitor third-party risk throughout the lifecycle.

•
Conduct research and analyze data to identify trends, risks, opportunities, and business insights.

•
Prepare reports, presentations, dashboards, and other materials for internal stakeholders.

•
Support the maintenance and enhancement of program documentation, guidance, procedures, and other resources.

•
Track project milestones, action items, decisions, and key deliverables.

•
Collaborate with colleagues across GSM, business units, risk functions and subject matter expert teams.

•
Review program outcomes and processes and contribute recommendations that improve efficiency, strengthen risk management practices, or enhance the stakeholder experience.
