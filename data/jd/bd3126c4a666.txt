Responsibilities About the Job: You will join our team for a 3-month project in data converter circuit design aimed at future products. You will get experience with the tools, skills, insights and tradeoffs necessary for successful integrated circuit design. Keysight fosters a collaborative engineering environment which emphasizes teamwork, mentoring, enthusiasm, and excellence in design. About the Team: Our Keysight Labs team develops (and often publishes) world’s-fastest data converters and other mixed-signal integrated circuits. Our chips provide the speed and accuracy performance that enable many of Keysight’s world-leading electronic test and measurement products. Responsibilities: You will do design and simulation of data converter circuits. Design may be at the block or circuit level, and simulation may include circuit simulation, electromagnetic simulation, and/or high-level custom software simulation. Qualifications Required Qualifications: Enrolled in a MS or PhD EE program Strong skills in transistor-level design of mixed signal CMOS and/or BiCMOS circuits Familiar with schematic entry / layout / simulation tool flow (preferably Cadence) Familiar with the use of one or more software scripting tools to support design, such as Bash, Python, PERL, Awk or SKILL Desired Qualifications: Experience with high-speed designs (> 1 GHz) Experience in data converter calibration and analysis Good communication and collaboration skills Other Information: Right to work in the US is required.
