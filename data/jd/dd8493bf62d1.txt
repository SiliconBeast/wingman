We are looking for a motivated finance intern to join our global Professional Tools finance team. The intern will work closely with the Director FP&A and global Professional Tools finance team to assist with financial analysis, long term planning and performance reporting to help drive data-based decision making across the organization.

In This Role, Your Responsibilities Will Be:

• Assist in preparing monthly reporting including P&L, variance analysis and other dashboards

• Assist in long term financial planning Profit Plan by gathering inputs, validating assumptions and updating financial models

• Support cross-departmental initiatives (pricing actions, cost-savings programs, headcount plans, etc.)

• Assist with other projects as assigned

Who You Are:

You are proactive and readily accept new challenges. You work cooperatively with others across the organization to achieve shared objectives. You deliver messages in a clear, compelling, and concise manner.

For This Role, You Will Need:

• Ability to work independently and as part of a team

• Proficient in Microsoft Office

• Strong communication, interpersonal, organizational and collaboration skills

• Strong intellectual curiosity, with a clear passion for finance and readiness to learn from real-world work

Preferred Qualifications That Set You Apart:
