Tech & Data Program Summer 2027 – Software Engineer Intern

Program Overview

At The Hartford, we foster an inclusive and collaborative environment where every voice is valued and every idea can make an impact. Our culture is built on integrity, innovation, and a customer-first mindset. We believe in empowering employees through continuous learning, mentorship, meaningful work, and opportunities to solve real business challenges.

As a Software Engineer Intern , you will help design, build, test, and enhance applications that support our customers, employees, and business operations. You'll work alongside experienced engineers in a collaborative Agile environment, applying modern software development practices to create solutions that deliver measurable business value.

Bring your curiosity, technical mindset, and willingness to learn. Your voice and perspective matter here.

About the Experience

The Hartford’s Technology, Data, AI, and Operations organization offers an immersive summer internship program designed to help students explore careers in software engineering, data engineering, cybersecurity, AI, analytics, data science, and technology operations within the insurance industry.

As part of The Hartford’s growing investment in AI and advanced technologies, interns will gain exposure to emerging capabilities such as generative AI, machine learning, and intelligent automation while contributing to meaningful projects that support business and customer outcomes.

This internship provides hands-on experience developing software solutions, collaborating with Agile teams, and learning how technology drives innovation across the enterprise.

This role is a hybrid position in Hartford, CT. Candidate must be authorized to work in the US without company sponsorship. The company will not support the STEM OPT I-983 Training Plan endorsement for this position.

What You'll Gain

• Hands-on experience developing software applications and services that support business and customer needs

• Exposure to modern software engineering practices, including Agile delivery, DevOps, cloud technologies, and application development frameworks

• Opportunities to work with programming languages, development tools, and cloud platforms used across the enterprise

• Mentorship, coaching, performance feedback, and self-directed learning opportunities

• Networking, volunteerism, and employee engagement experiences with peers and leaders across the organization

• A deeper understanding of how technology enables innovation, efficiency, and customer experiences within the insurance industry

Ways You'll Learn, Grow, and Contribute

Depending on your role and project assignments, your experience may include:

• Design, develop, test, and maintain software applications and services that support business and customer needs.

• Collaborate with engineers, product owners, and business stakeholders in an Agile environment to deliver technology solutions.

• Participate in the full software development lifecycle, including coding, debugging, testing, deployment, documentation, and code reviews.

• Leverage modern programming languages, development frameworks, databases, and cloud technologies to build scalable and reliable solutions.

• Use AI-powered development tools and explore emerging technologies to enhance productivity, improve software quality, and support innovation.

• Apply technical and analytical problem-solving skills to improve application performance, security, and customer experiences, and present outcomes to stakeholders.

Required Qualifications

• Undergraduate or graduate student expecting to graduate in May 2028 with a Bachelor's or Master's degree and a GPA of 3.0 or higher

• Pursuing a degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, Cybersecurity, Engineering, or another STEM-related field

• Strong analytical, problem-solving, and critical-thinking skills

• Strong communication, collaboration, and interpersonal skills

• Curiosity and willingness to learn new technologies and challenge assumptions

• Ability to analyze complex problems and develop practical technical solutions

• Commitment to ethical decision-making and responsible technology practices

Experience and exposure to some or all of the following:

• Programming Languages: Java, Python, C#, JavaScript, SQL, .NET

• Web Development: HTML, CSS, React, Angular, APIs, or modern web application frameworks

• Cloud Technologies: AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud Platform

• Software Engineering Practices: Object-oriented programming, testing frameworks, source control, CI/CD, or DevOps concepts

• Cybersecurity: Secure coding practices, cyber defense concepts, or security frameworks

• AI & Emerging Technologies: Familiarity with AI/ML concepts, generative AI tools, or interest in applying emerging technologies to business challenges

• Agile Ways of Working: Experience working on Agile teams, collaborative projects, or product-focused development efforts

Our internship program is designed as a pipeline for future full-time opportunities. High-performing interns may be considered for full-time roles within The Hartford’s Technology & Data organization.
