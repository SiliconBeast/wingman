We are seeking a highly motivated Engineering Student to join our growing 300mm Post Fab team in East Fishkill, NY as a summer intern in a Module Engineering Team. This Team is critical to our ability to improve manufacturing performance and drive best in class Quality, Cycle-time, and Cost as we develop new products and ramp volume manufacturing.

Key Duties and Responsibilities:

Learn about and help Module Engineers collect data and improve performance of the equipment and processes in EFK onsemi manufacturing.

Internship Project may include:

• Develop new process or controls.

• Evaluate and qualify new materials, processes, and equipment for our Post Fab wafer operations.

• Troubleshoot process issues and implement corrective actions to improve yield and device performance.

• Learning and practicing good clear communication

• Collaborate with cross-functional teams

• Ensure compliance with safety and quality standards.
