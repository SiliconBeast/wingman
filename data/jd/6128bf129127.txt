About our Summer Internship Program

RF-SMART's Summer Internship Program gives you real work, real responsibility, and direct access to leaders across the organization. This is a 12-week, full-time experience—not a resume line item, but a launchpad.

RF-SMART has been transforming warehouse and inventory management since 1982, serving 3,000+ customers across industries like healthcare, manufacturing, and wholesale distribution. Our Oracle Cloud team builds mobile-first software that integrates deeply with Oracle Cloud, helping warehouse teams work faster, smarter, and with greater accuracy. As a Software Developer Intern, you'll join a collaborative engineering team building a modern, cloud-native SaaS platform across mobile, web, and server components—contributing to software that impacts businesses around the world.
