Department Overview
Test Systems Engineering: Designs, builds, and deploys test solutions into our various global manufacturing sites. These solutions are used to program, tune, and test the company's product portfolio. This helps ensure that our products are performing per customer specifications. This process is accomplished by working closely with our product development engineering and manufacturing facilities.

Job Description

In this role you will do the following:

•
Design, develop and implement test hardware and software for production testing of new products. Evaluate deadlines, goals and develop project schedules.

•
Design, develops, and implement solutions to improve test yields, reduce over all Test time, provide for test capacity increases, minimize downtime, and optimize test/process flows. This includes implementing new processes and technologies, utilizing statistical tools, and developing innovations to existing problems and issues.

•
Analyze and critiques equipment designs/processes relative to market requirements, manufacturability, costs and technical requirements.

•
Identify technical and quality problems, determines alternatives and evaluates them to arrive at the most acceptable approach including conformity to specifications, reliability, cost effectiveness, testability and reparability.

•
Identify need for, assesses and/or develops technical/process specification standards and participates in establishing the procedures to ensure they are followed.

•
Prepare and present technical reports to influence the decision making process. Provide technical information and participates as a speaker in technical programs.
