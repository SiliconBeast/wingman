The Product Strategy & Marketing team within Membership & Deposit Products (MDP) is responsible for shaping product growth strategies, improving member experiences, and driving business outcomes through strategic planning, data-driven insights, and cross-functional collaboration. Our team serves as a bridge between business strategy, product management, analytics, marketing, and operations, helping bring ideas from concept to execution while measuring outcomes and informing future decisions.

As part of the team, the Summer Associate (Product Strategist) will gain hands-on experience supporting strategic initiatives, product research, analysis, and experimentation efforts. The associate will work alongside product strategists and business leaders to explore member needs, evaluate emerging trends, analyze business performance, and contribute to recommendations that support product growth and innovation. The role will also provide exposure to data analytics, artificial intelligence applications, testing and learning methodologies, and modern product management practices, helping prepare the associate for future careers in strategy, product management, consulting, analytics, or related fields.
