Job Description

The UI/UX Designer intern will work closely with our developer/designer team and our marketing team to ensure seamless web/mobile design and the successful implementation of UI/UX best practices and principles across all our digital platforms. This includes helping out with creating wireframes, designing new web templates using our UI/UX design tool, building web forms, and creating pages in our Content Management System.

Key Accountabilities

•
Contribute to projects from their inception to launch (will include research, flows, wireframing, prototyping, content authoring & more)

•
Collaborate with marketing and designer teams on product requirements and focus on the user experience, ease-of-use, performance, and efficiency of websites

•
Work independently and in collaboration with other designers and the marketing team to quickly produce multiple concepts and prototypes

•
Work with web developers to ensure the technical feasibility of designs and guide the interface through the development process

•
Design UI elements such as navigation menus, search boxes, tabs, and page designs for our websites

•
Author content for landing pages across multiple brand sites.

Qualifications / Requirements

•
Currently pursuing a bachelor’s degree with a major or minor in UX Design, Computer Science, Human Computer Interaction, or other related disciplines.

•

•
Has a passion for usability and effective, attractive UI

•
Should be Interested in latest digital trends & web technologies

•
