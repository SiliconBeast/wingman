Product Engineer Intern - Summer 2027
Program Duration: End of May - Early August
Location: Ocala, FL (on-site)

Pella Corporation is seeking motivated engineering students for our Product Engineering Internship.

This internship provides hands-on experience supporting product development and engineering initiatives through meaningful projects that contribute to quality, cost, and product performance objectives. Interns work alongside experienced engineers and cross-functional teams while developing technical, analytical, and professional skills in a real-world environment.

If you're interested in applying engineering principles to product development, solving technical challenges, and gaining experience similar to that of an entry-level engineer, we'd love to connect with you.

As a Product Engineering Intern, you will support engineering projects and initiatives while developing engineering competencies and an understanding of how engineering supports business objectives.

Responsibilities are similar to what we expect of entry-level engineers who join Pella after graduation. Interns work closely with an assigned manager and mentor on strategic projects and focused development opportunities.

As a Product Engineering Intern, you may:

• Support engineering projects focused on quality, cost, performance, and product improvement

• Apply engineering principles and problem-solving techniques to assigned projects and tasks

• Partner with engineering and cross-functional teams to support business initiatives

• Utilize engineering tools, technologies, and processes to deliver project results

• Analyze data and communicate findings and recommendations

• Gain exposure to product development and engineering processes within the fenestration industry

• Develop technical, professional, and leadership skills through project ownership and collaboration

All projects and assignments will be determined based on business needs and team priorities.

Additional Information

• Minimum 10-week, full-time internship assignment

• Assigned manager and mentor support throughout the internship

• Minimal travel may be required

• Periodic exposure to manufacturing equipment and production environments

• Occasional lifting of materials up to 30 pounds may be required

• Work may be performed in office, laboratory, and manufacturing settings

• Currently pursuing a bachelor's degree in Engineering, Mathematics, or a related scientific discipline

• Strong academic standing

• Demonstrated leadership, teamwork, or involvement through campus organizations, work experience, athletics, volunteer activities, or other experiences

• Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, including Excel, Word, PowerPoint, and Outlook

• Strong written and verbal communication skills

• Demonstrated analytical and problem-solving abilities

• Ability to learn new technologies, systems, and engineering tools

• Ability to succeed in a collaborative, team-based environment

• Previous internship or co-op experience preferred

Compensation: The starting pay rate for this position is $27.90 per hour . Compensation for this role is established through Pella Corporation’s internship program. Eligible participants may receive transition assistance in accordance with company policies and program guidelines.
