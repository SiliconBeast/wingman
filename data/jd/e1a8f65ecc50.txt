BMO Capital Markets

BMO Capital Markets is a leading, full-service North American-based financial services provider offering equity and debt underwriting, corporate lending and project financing, merger and acquisitions advisory services, securitization, treasury management, market risk management, debt and equity research and institutional sales and trading. BMO Capital Markets has over 2,700 professionals in 30 locations around the world, including 20 offices in North America.

Program Overview:

Summer analyst intern work in the Global Markets Group of BMO Capital Markets, comprising Client Coverage, Origination, Digital and Liquid Trading, Structured Products Trading, Equity Products, and Financial Resource Management. Global Markets serves institutional, corporate, and government clients.

A variety of desks host summer analysts including Equity Sales and Trading, Clearpool E-trading, Prime Finance, Structured Notes Sales and Trading, Leveraged Finance, Securitized Products Sales and Trading, Origination, Debt Capital Markets, and US Interest Rates Sales and Trading.

Summer analyst will work on one desk in Global Markets over a 14-week internship period. With a specific role available in Calgary for the winter term, the potential candidate would work on the commodity derivatives desk in Calgary and will work alongside a team of energy professionals in fast paced and competitive environment.

Throughout the term there will be opportunities for continuous professional development activities. These activities include teach-ins, trading games, meetings with senior leaders, and networking events with both peers and Senior Leadership.

Desk Specific Description:

BMO’s Energy commodities market making desk (market maker is defined as a liquidity provider that both buys and sells tradeable assets in a given market) serves clients primarily based in Canada and the US. Four commodity market professionals are based in Calgary with additional team members across Toronto, London, and New York.

•
Serve clients across the energy value chain: upstream (producers), midstream (pipelines & infrastructure), downstream (refining), and end use consumers

•
Ability to transact in multiple commodities including Crude Oil, Natural Gas, and Refined Products (both financially and physically)

•
Tailor structures and products to client needs with varying complexity, including fixed price swaps, basis swaps, option structures, inventory finance and storage

In this internship, Summer analyst are fully integrated into an assigned desk, in this case the Calgary Energy Sales and Trading desk. Responsibilities will vary widely from day to day based on the needs of the business and the current market environment. Depending on the assigned desk, analyst skill set, and business needs, responsibilities may include:

Development and deployment of business solutions such as pricing and risk models

•
Market data capture and display, spreadsheet development

•
Providing insight into trading strategies leveraging quantitative modelling

•
Improving on existing technology involving system infrastructure, internal reporting, client/management presentations, etc.

•
Bespoke projects requiring market, portfolio, and/or client research and data analytics

•
Creating client presentation materials using PowerPoint and Excel

•
Developing client trade ideas

•
Planning/supporting client events

•
Recapping and organizing market data and events

•
Shadowing and learning from Trading, Sales, and Strategy professionals

Desk Specific Responsibilities:

Summer interns will focus on analysis of market fundamentals as it relates to global and Canadian crude oil and gas markets, along with daily responsibilities and ad hoc projects. Fundamentals analysis will be used to inform the decision making of sales and trading as well as BMO’s clients. Specifically, the candidate will be expected to (with the help of the sales & trading teams) work on the following initiatives:

Review and organize existing models Buildout Canadian Crude data feeds using existing third-party subscriptions and web scrapes when necessary, improve on existing infrastructure, and further adhoc projects for both sales and trading. Beyond the work on energy fundamentals, the candidate will also be responsible for assisting with the maintenance and distribution of existing marketing infrastructure, pitch books, and market analysis
