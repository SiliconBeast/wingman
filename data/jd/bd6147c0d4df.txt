Hybrid Work Program

OCC operates under a Hybrid Work Program which, absent an approved exception, requires employees to work from an OCC office at least 3 days per week. To maximize in–person collaboration, Tuesday and Wednesday are considered anchor days, and all employees are expected to work in the office both days. Employees meeting performance expectations are generally permitted to select 2 other days per week to work remotely if desired. When working remotely, employees are generally expected to work from the state in which they reside, absent an exception approved by Human Resources. Additionally, OCC generally does not permit employees to work while traveling internationally.

About OCC:

A World Class Clearing Organization

The Options Clearing Corporation (OCC) is the world's largest equity derivatives clearing organization. Founded in 1973, OCC is dedicated to promoting stability and market integrity by delivering clearing and settlement services for options, futures and securities lending transactions. As a Systemically Important Financial Market Utility (SIFMU), OCC operates under the jurisdiction of the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), the U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC), and the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System. OCC has more than 100 clearing members and provides central counterparty (CCP) clearing and settlement services to 21 exchanges and trading platforms.

OCC also provides free and engaging educational resources about the benefits and risks of options through its Options Industry Council (OIC ).

OCC is building a culture that empowers continued learning, authentic innovation, and constant creativity.

About the Team & Role

QRM's core function is the development, validation, and ongoing maintenance of risk models used for margin, clearing fund, and stress testing at OCC — the world's largest equity derivatives clearing organization. The team's work spans model specification, performance monitoring, and analysis of model behavior against realized market outcomes. The intern will contribute directly to this function, working alongside quantitative analysts to support ongoing model development and monitoring efforts — gaining exposure to how risk models are evaluated, challenged, and maintained in a live central counterparty clearing environment.
