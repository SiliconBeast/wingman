There’s something really inspiring about the idea of innovators teaming up to build something incredible. If you agree, then you might be a perfect fit for a tech job at Cox!

Across the Cox family of businesses, we’re looking for Test Automation Engineer Interns to join our team and learn from the best. Ready to collaborate with us and work at a great company that truly cares about you and your future? Keep reading!

What’s In It For You?      

• We value your time and potential. At Cox, you’ll be exposed to meaningful and challenging work. 

• You won’t be left alone to figure things out. You’ll have a team of supporters (plus an assigned program mentor) who will show you the ropes, guide you to excellence and cheer you on.

• You’ll experience real accountability to develop your professional skills.  

• You’ll expand your network and professional toolbox through exposure to senior leaders. 

• You’ll help us build a bold future that is sustainable, accessible and inclusive.

We are looking for a talented individual to join our Workday Human Capital Management (HCM) software quality assurance team. In this position, you will collaborate with Employee Life Cycle (ELC) test engineers on STLC process to learn the test methodologies, agile concepts and test automation tool TOSCA to build and maintain automation test scripts.

Responsibilities:

• Understanding of Agile methodology

• Work with Lead Test Engineer to create, update, and manage test automation scripts.

• Responsible for planning, creating & executing manual test activities in an Agile environment.

• Use of defect tracking tools to log, manage, resolve, and report on bugs/defects.

• Participate in Agile ceremonies (daily stand-up meetings, sprint planning, story writing, etc.)

• Collaborate with various teams across HCM space on test automation.

Qualifications:

• Graduate/Undergraduate junior or senior level student with Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Information Systems or related Engineering field

• Internship positions are located in Atlanta, GA; Cox does not provide a housing allowance.

Preferred:

• Knowledge of basic SQL, any QA Test Management Tools/qTest, databases

• Proficient in Microsoft Office Suite, MS Teams, SharePoint

• Manual Testing, API's, Data Validation, Test Automation tools

• Agile concepts
