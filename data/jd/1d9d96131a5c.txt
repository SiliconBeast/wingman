Business Unit/Role Specific Info
The Finance Organization drives business value by being a strategic partner in every decision made in support of the Company’s success. We bring a powerful combination of financial expertise, a deep understanding of complex analytics, a thorough knowledge of the risk environment, and a commitment to upholding the highest standards of financial integrity.

The internship will be a general Finance internship that will provide an opportunity to work in the Controllership function of American Express Finance. This internship position will be based in Phoenix, AZ.

How will you make an impact in this role?
• Complete a project solving a real business challenge for the organization
• Participate in other initiatives and projects as assigned
• Assist with monthly general ledger close activities including completing journal entries and account reconciliations
• Assist in producing and reviewing monthly and quarterly financial reports
• Perform variance analysis and analytical procedures for assigned general ledger accounts
• Establish relationships and partner with subject matter experts to key to Controllership accounting activities

Minimum Qualifications
• Currently enrolled in a full-time bachelor’s degree program
• Students must have a graduation date between December 2027 and June 2028

Preferred Qualifications
• Analytical Skills – ability to manipulate large amounts of data, spot trends & generate significant insights
• Communication Skills – synthesize data into an easy-to-understand story & articulate key messages across multiple audiences.
• Agile Mentality – positive, can-do attitude, adapt to change, and highly motivated self-starter
• Leadership – act & lead with courage, establish relationships, make decisions quickly & influence change

Employment eligibility to work with American Express in the United States is required as the company will not pursue visa sponsorship for these positions.
