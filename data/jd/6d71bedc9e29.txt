IT Business Analyst Intern, Systems Enablement - Summer 2027
Program Duration: End of May - Early August
Location: Pella, IA (on-site) or Urbandale, IA (on-site)

Pella Corporation is seeking motivated undergraduate students for our IT Business Analyst Internship in Pella, Iowa.

Business analysts play a key role in how new technology reaches the people who use it. At Pella, we're implementing modern systems that improve how our teams work, and success depends on understanding user needs, supporting adoption, and connecting business teams with the technical teams who build and support these solutions.

This internship provides hands-on experience supporting a major ERP technology migration while working alongside experienced IT professionals. Interns gain practical experience in system implementation, user enablement, and technical support while building technical and professional skills that prepare them for future careers in information technology and business analysis.

If you're eager to learn, enjoy solving problems, and want to help people get the most out of new technology, we'd love to connect with you.

As an IT Business Analyst Intern, you will support the implementation and adoption of a new enterprise technology platform through project work and collaboration with cross-functional teams. Working alongside experienced professionals, you'll gain exposure to system implementation, user enablement, and technical support practices while contributing to real business initiatives.

Responsibilities are similar to what we expect of entry-level business analysts who join Pella after graduation.

As an IT Business Analyst Intern, you may:

• Support end user training and adoption for a new core system, including walkthroughs, job aids, and reference documentation

• Triage reported issues by reproducing problems, gathering details, and escalating to the appropriate engineering team

• Gather business requirements and translate them into clear documentation for engineering and product teams

• Serve as a communication link between business users and technical teams

• Support testing, data validation, and cutover activities as the new platform is rolled out

• Participate in Agile ceremonies, including standups, sprint planning, and retrospectives

• Contribute to an individual internship project and collaborate with fellow interns on a cross-functional project focused on improving a business process or solution

• Currently pursuing a bachelor's degree in Management Information Systems (MIS), Computer Science, Information Technology, Business Analytics, Computer Engineering, or a related field

• Strong academic standing

• Strong written and verbal communication skills with the ability to effectively communicate with both technical and non-technical audiences

• Experience using AI tools such as Claude, ChatGPT, or Copilot to solve problems

• Demonstrated leadership, teamwork, or involvement through campus organizations, work experience, athletics, volunteer activities, or other experiences

• Demonstrated problem-solving and analytical skills

• Ability to learn new technologies, systems, and technical concepts quickly

Preferred Qualifications

Experience teaching, training, tutoring, or supporting other people's use of technology, including campus IT help desk or service desk work, peer tutoring, or student technology roles

•
Ability to create multimedia training assets such as screen recordings, instructional videos, or visual walkthroughs
