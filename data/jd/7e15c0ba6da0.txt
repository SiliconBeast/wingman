Job Title
Intern - Electrical Engineering - Cambridge, MA - Summer 2027

Job Description

Are you interested in an Internship opportunity with Philips? We welcome individuals who are currently pursuing an Electrical Engineering degree to participate in paid intern opportunities. Through this role you will gain meaningful, hands-on experience working for a HealthTech company.

Your role:

• Provide Electrical Engineering support for the development of next generation cardiac ultrasound imaging transducers

• Participate in the development of new circuitry for active imaging systems (integrated circuits, printed circuit boards, ASIC) including ASIC Test development

• Hands-on electrical engineering laboratory work (test, development, verification)

• Work alongside a multidiscipline group of engineers ranging from mechanical, electrical, materials, acoustic, and system/software engineers

• Deep-dive into real engineering problems and learn to implement products through the engineering design process (Design-Build-Test-Learn-Repeat)

You’re the right fit if:

• Currently pursuing a degree in Electrical Engineering

• A strong team player (Social skills, collaboration, and teamwork are all integral to a successful designs)

• An independent, self-sufficient, and responsible student

• Strong critical thinker who embraces new and challenging problems

• You must be able to successfully perform the following minimum Physical, Cognitive and Environmental job requirements with or without accommodation for this Office/Remote position.
