Business Insights & Analytics Intern - Animal Nutrition

Pay : $25/ hr

Position Duration: May 2027 – July 2027

Position Location: Arden Hills, MN

As a Business Insights & Analytics Intern , you'll work alongside business leaders to help optimize pricing decisions and drive profitable growth for the Animal Nutrition business unit . Through hands-on projects and cross-functional collaboration, you'll gain exposure to how data, market dynamics, and business strategies come together to influence decision-making.

During the internship, you will:

•
Analyze pricing, sales, market, and profitability data to identify business opportunities and support strategic recommendations.

•
Partner with Pricing, Supply Chain, Sales, Marketing, and Finance teams to understand how commercial decisions impact business performance.

•
Learn how market trends, customer needs, competitor activity, and commodity dynamics influence pricing strategies.

•
Develop data analysis, business storytelling, and decision-making skills through real-world projects.

•
Create and present insights and recommendations to business stakeholders.

•
Gain exposure to the agricultural and animal nutrition industries while building a strong understanding of commercial strategy and revenue management.

•
Collaborate with peers, mentors, and leaders across the organization.
