The Cybersecurity Intern will help protect AbbVie's systems, data, and applications across one of four focus areas: Cybersecurity, Security Operations Center (SOC), Identity Access Management (IAM), and Application Security. Specific responsibilities could include:

•
Monitor security alerts and incidents, assist with vulnerability scanning and remediation, support security awareness initiatives, and document and report security issues

•
Perform initial triage of security events and alarms, escalate potential threats to senior analysts, maintain logs and incident records, and assist with threat analysis and resolution

•
Process access requests and account provisioning, run access reviews and audits, maintain account and permission documentation, and support password management and authentication troubleshooting

•
Support secure code review and testing and assist with application vulnerability assessments

This is an 11-week onsite internship. The majority of BTS Interns will be placed in Lake County, IL, though there is a possibility of being placed within any U.S. AbbVie or Allergan Aesthetics location. While location preferences are considered, placements will ultimately be based on business need.

Minimum Qualifications:

•
Pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, AI or related majors with a minor or concentration in Cybersecurity. Relevant exposure through extracurricular or professional experiences can be considered in lieu of a Cybersecurity minor

•
Graduation date between December 2027 – June 2028

•
Must be enrolled in university the semester following your internship and graduate within 1-year post internship

Preferred Qualifications:

•
Experience with Python scripting and automation tooling (SOAR, Ansible), networking fundamentals (TCP/IP, ports, firewalls), and Windows/Linux OS basics

•
Ability to read and assess code (JavaScript, Java, or C#) and write securely, with a willingness to learn secure API design

•
Foundational knowledge of cloud security concepts across AWS, Azure, and GCP and cloud-native security tooling

•
Previous internship or related experience

•
Technical and business insight, strong analytical skills, and creative problem-solving abilities

•
Proven track record of teamwork, adaptability, innovation, initiative, and integrity

•
Excellent communication, leadership, and project management skills

•
Cumulative GPA of 3.00 or higher
