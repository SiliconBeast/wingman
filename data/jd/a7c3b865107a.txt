What you’ll be doing

Join CIBC’s Technology Operations team as a Cloud Engineering Co-op and have a real impact in making our clients’ ambitions a reality! As a Cloud Engineering Co-op, you'll contribute to meaningful work that gives you hands-on exposure to cloud engineering while learning from experienced team members. You'll support the deployment of cloud resources, help build and maintain environments that enable application teams to migrate to the cloud, and work with DevOps tools and automation practices. You'll also participate in Azure-focused work while gaining exposure to Amazon Web Services (AWS) and Google Cloud Platform (GCP). In this role, you'll have opportunities to build, test, and support solutions in production and non-production environments, with training and guidance provided along the way.

At CIBC we enable the work environment most optimal for you to thrive in your role. Details on your work arrangement (proportion of on-site and remote work) will be discussed at the time of your interview.

How you'll succeed

• Cloud Engineering Support - Contribute to the deployment, configuration, and maintenance of cloud resources, with a primary focus on Microsoft Azure and exposure to AWS and Google Cloud Platform (GCP).

• Environment Enablement - Assist in building and supporting cloud environments that help application teams migrate, test, and operate their solutions effectively.

• DevOps Practices - Participate in automation, scripting, and DevOps activities that improve the efficiency, consistency, and reliability of cloud operations.

• Continuous Learning - Build your cloud engineering knowledge through hands-on experience, training, and collaboration with teammates across Technology Operations.
