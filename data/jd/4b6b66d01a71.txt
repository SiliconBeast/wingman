Energy Supply (Generation) Compliance Programs is seeking a highly analytical, detail oriented, and self motivated intern with a strong interest in data driven decision making, process optimization and continuous improvement. The Data Analyst intern will work closely with Reporting & Compliance Consultants to map process data, build out queries in multiple databases, develop data checks within PowerBI or similar analytics tool, automate reporting functions, manage software tickets with vendors of reporting tools, and translate work into process guidance documents, procedures and training.

Typical intern responsibilities may include but are not limited to:

•
Support analytical and continuous improvement initiatives under the guidance of Reporting & Compliance Analysts or Planning and Process Consultants.

•
Collaborate with operations, engineering, IT support teams, and vendors to support data mapping, report development, automation, and development/improvement of process documents and training.

•
Collect, validate, analyze and organize operational data used for reporting and trend analysis that will support root cause analysis, problem-solving, and development of solutions/process improvements.

•
Enhance existing dashboards or develop new dashboards that track key operational metrics. Ensure the dashboards are meaningful and usable for plant teams. Ensure dashboards remain accurate, current, and aligned with evolving business needs.

•
Identify outdated program and training content, develop updated program and training documentation, and partner with learning management systems (LMS) to migrate training content into LMS-based training.

•
Participate in team meetings, brainstorming sessions, and project planning with internal and external stakeholders.

•
Project manage small to medium improvement projects per technical implementation plans.

This position is for a May 24, 2027 start date. This position may have the possibility to extend beyond the internship’s initial term based on the candidate’s successful performance and Xcel Energy’s business needs.
