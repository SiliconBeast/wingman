Overview

We are looking for talented and passionate college students for the summer to join our Barrios team in support of work we do for government and commercial industries in human spaceflight. This is a unique opportunity for a college student (Sophomore by credit or higher) to have an internship supporting missions to low-Earth orbit, the Moon, and to Mars and beyond. This position will be in our Digital Solutions department, supporting one of the various Barrios disciplines, conducting digital infrastructure support and or supporting application and data development across multiple disciplines. The goal of this internship is to introduce the successful candidate to multiple digital solutions disciplines while performing a project in a designated area that is integral to achieving mission success. Each candidate will present an out-brief at the end of the internship.

Note: We will be accepting applications from September 28th until November 6th. Interviews will begin no earlier than October 21st.

**An unofficial transcript is required at the time of application. Please upload your transcript in addition to a resume .**
