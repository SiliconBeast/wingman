Who We Are

Verkada is transforming how organizations protect their people and places with an integrated, privacy-sensitive AI-powered platform that includes solutions for video security, access control, air quality sensors, alarms, intercoms, and visitor management.

We’ve got serious momentum in the market: more than 30,000 customers (including 100+ of the Fortune 500), a $5.8B valuation , more than $1 billion in annualized bookings, and backing from CapitalG, Sequoia Capital, General Catalyst, Felicis Ventures, Next47 and more. The warehouse that uses AI-powered alerts to make sure its team is protected on the floor with proper PPE. The school that’s alerted to a threat in real-time and triggers a lockdown in seconds, not minutes. We’re rapidly scaling this impact: today, more than 2 million Verkada devices are deployed across 170+ countries.

About the Role

Verkada is looking for an Mobile Engineering intern to join our team for an exciting and challenging internship. As part of the Mobile Engineering team, you will collaborate cross functionally to enable a native mobile experience in both our existing and new iOS and Android applications, enabling our best-in-class security systems.

What You'll Do

• Support the entire mobile application lifecycle (concept, design, test, and release) in a fast paced environment for both existing and new mobile applications

• Work in partnership with cross-functional teams to grow, design, and implement new features

• Develop in Swift or Kotlin in conjunction with backend work

• Work in iterative sprints to design, develop, and test solutions

• Build automation and tests to streamline development

What You Bring

• Ability to work at Verkada HQ during winter or summer 2027

• Actively pursuing a Bachelors degree in Computer Science (or similar degree plan) with a graduate date in early to mid 2026

• Proficient understanding of Swift and iOS SDK OR Kotlin and Android SDK, and a willingness to learn about both

• Experience writing multi-threaded, asynchronous code

• Experience working with backend server APIs

• Solid UI development and unit testing skills

• Thorough understanding of fundamental Computer Science concepts

• Excellent interpersonal skills and ability to work well alongside engineers of all levels
