ABOUT THE ROLE & TEAM

As Bedrock scales deployments nationwide, every machine has to be reliably built, provisioned, calibrated, updated, monitored, and kept healthy. Fleet Engineering builds the platforms and internal tools that teams across the company use to manage our growing fleet of autonomous machines.

You'll spend the summer designing, building, and shipping a critical internal tool end to end, one we'll continue using after you leave. Along the way, you'll work with teams across engineering and operations, and see firsthand what it takes to run robots in production.




WHAT YOU'LL DO

 - Understand, design, and build an internal tool that addresses a key challenge in the robot lifecycle, and define what "done" means

 - Interview the people who'll actually use the tool and translate their pain into requirements

 - Build the tool end to end: data, backend, and interfaces, working with cross-functional teams to unblock yourself

 - Instrument usage so you can tell whether it's helping

 - Iterate fast on real feedback from real operators, traveling to the field if needed

 - Document and hand off so what you build survives past the summer




WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR


REQUIRED

 - Currently pursuing a BS or MS in computer science, software engineering, or a related field, or equivalent hands-on building experience

 - Ability to design and build full-stack software applications

 - Proficiency in at least one high-level programming language (Python, Rust, C++, TypeScript, etc.)

 - Product sense: you'd rather ship the useful version than the elegant one

 - Comfort working with ambiguity and defining your own scope

 - Clear communication with teams outside engineering. Your users may operate excavators, repair hardware in the field, or work directly with customers.




PREFERRED

 - Experience with data visualization or operational dashboards

 - Experience shipping software to production

 - Exposure to fleet telemetry, IoT, robotics, or operations tooling

 - Familiarity with cloud infrastructure and CI/CD
