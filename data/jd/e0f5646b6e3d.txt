Tokyo Electron (TEL) is a leader in semiconductor manufacturing equipment. We provide key parts of the manufacturing process for the world’s largest foundries. TEL Technology AI and Design Inc is our Silicon Valley AI lab, where we do groundbreaking, long-term research and technology development in AI/ML for semiconductor equipment.

We are looking for a new team member who can contribute to our AI research roadmap.

TTAD fosters a creative culture where ideas are freely exchanged inside our lab and with our colleagues around the world.

TTAD Requirements

• Creative, out-of-the-box thinker

• Good communicator who works well in a team and across cultural barriers

• Low prestige problem solver: willing to be wrong and accept a better solution

• Productive in translating ideas to code

• Present to support colleagues – gives freely of own time

• Interested in learning, e.g. a complex application domain, new programming languages

Role Requirements

• Proficiency in Python and/or C/C++; ability to read and modify existing codebases.

• High proficiency in algorithmic thinking and software development

• High familiarity with current AI approaches and research

• Clear written and verbal communication for producing technical deliverables.

• Graduate level (M.S. or Ph.D.) preferred, strong candidates who are rising Junior or Senior level Bachelor’s level students in the named fields will be considered

Role Responsibilities

• Drive development of assigned research tasks

• Research and propose solutions to assigned research topics

• Convert ideas to code in a productive and flexible manner, in existing and new code bases

• Actively contribute to TTAD knowledge accumulation by sharing research topics and insights with team
