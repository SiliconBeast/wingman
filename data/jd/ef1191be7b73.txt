Instrumentation Intern - Summer 202 7

Location

Oakdale, MN

Duration

Approximately 12 weeks, beginning May 2027

About Us

At Neogen, we are committed to developing future talent by providing meaningful internship experiences that offer real-world exposure, professional development, and opportunities to contribute to business priorities.

Position Summary

The Instrumentation Intern will support key initiatives within the department while gaining hands-on experience in a professional corporate environment. This internship provides an opportunity to apply classroom knowledge to real business challenges, collaborate with experienced professionals, and develop valuable career skills.

Internship Project

The intern will work on a structured project designed to:

• Address a meaningful business need

• Deliver measurable outcomes

• Be completed within the internship timeframe

• Provide exposure to cross-functional collaboration

• Result in a final presentation and/or deliverable

Key Responsibilities

• Support the design, analysis, testing, and debugging of electronic circuits and subsystems.

• Assemble and evaluate electronic prototypes.

• Participate in team meetings and project discussions

• Collaborate with cross-functional partners as needed

• Present project findings and recommendations at the conclusion of the internship

• Complete additional duties as assigned

Qualifications

Required

• Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in:

• Electrical Engineering

• Electronics Engineering

• Minimum GPA requirement: 3.0

• Strong communication and organizational skills

• Ability to manage multiple priorities and meet deadlines

• Understanding of analog and digital circuits.

Preferred

• Experience with embedded platforms including raspberry pi, Arduino, or other microcontrollers

• Proficient with Altium Designer

• Previous internship or leadership experience

• Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities

What Interns Will Gain

• Hands-on experience with food safety testing instruments

• Exposure to real business challenges and projects

• Mentorship from experienced professionals

• Networking opportunities with leaders and team members

• Professional development opportunities

• Experience presenting business recommendations to leadership

Learning Objectives

By the end of the internship, the student will:

• Develop knowledge of the industry and business operations

• Strengthen professional communication skills

• Gain practical experience applying academic concepts

• Build project management and problem-solving capabilities

• Deliver a final project demonstrating business impact

Compensation & Benefits

• Participation in intern events and learning sessions
