Job Description

Internships within Lifecycle Services (LCS) provide unique opportunities to develop your engineering, analytical, and customer-centric skills.

As a Content IDE Software Development Intern, you will support the in-house software development of a tool for RAU (Rockwell Automation University) that will enable our instructors to more seamlessly develop training material. There may also be opportunities for software development within Microsoft's PowerBI and PowerApp environments.

The Essentials - You Will Have:

• Active enrollment in a four year or advanced degree program at an accredited university

• Legal authorization to work in the U.S. We will not sponsor individuals for employment visas, now or in the future

The Preferred - You Might Also Have:

• Met us through an event or interned with us in the past

• A cumulative GPA of 3.0 on a 4.0 scale

• Demonstrated desire to deliver high-quality customer service

• Possess a valid U.S. driver's license

• Demonstrated ability to approach challenges with innovative solutions

• Experience programming with Object Oriented Programming

• Experience with Git or GitHub

• Experience with web development or Visual Studio Code
