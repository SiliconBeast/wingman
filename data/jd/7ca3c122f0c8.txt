What you'll be doing

Technology Operations helps keep our systems, processes, and teams running smoothly so we can deliver reliable experiences across the bank. As an AI & Data Analytics and Reporting Analyst Co-op, you'll support reporting and analytics initiatives by partnering with stakeholders to understand business needs, connect to and organize data from Excel, flat files, databases, and other organizational repositories, and turn information into meaningful insights. Reporting and analytics are the core focus of the role: you'll create, enhance, and maintain Power BI data models, dashboards, and reports, including reliable refresh processes. You'll also explore how CIBC AI solutions, Copilot, AI agents, automation, and digital tools can simplify manual work, improve processes, and support informed decision-making. This is a hands-on opportunity to build your technical, analytical, and stakeholder-management skills while delivering practical solutions that help teams work more effectively.

At CIBC we enable the work environment most optimal for you to thrive in your role. You'll have the flexibility to manage your work activities within a hybrid work arrangement where you'll spend 1-3 days per week on-site, while other days will be remote.

How you'll succeed

• Data Analytics, Reporting & Insights – Connect to and retrieve data from Excel, flat files, databases, and other organizational repositories. Clean, combine, validate, and analyze data to identify trends, risks, opportunities, and actionable business insights.

• Power BI Solutions – Build and maintain Power BI data models, reports, and dashboards; establish reliable refresh processes; and translate business requirements into clear, decision-ready reporting solutions.

• AI Enablement & Agent Solutions – Use CIBC AI solutions, Copilot, and related tools to support analytics, build practical agents, summarize information, and automate repeatable reporting or business activities.

• Project Delivery & Stakeholder Engagement - Manage reporting and analytics initiatives as small projects by gathering requirements, coordinating activities, engaging stakeholders, preparing communications and launch materials, and incorporating user feedback to improve adoption, usability, and business value.

• Process Improvement, Digital Enablement & Compliance – Use data and technology to improve existing processes, digitize manual activities, and provide reporting support for audit, compliance, governance, and operational requirements.

• Technical Enablement – Apply practical coding and data skills—including Power Query, DAX, SQL, HTML, or similar technologies—to transform datasets, automate reporting steps, and improve reporting outputs.
