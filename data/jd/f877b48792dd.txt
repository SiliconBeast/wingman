Your Team, Your Impact
As part of the Cloud Switch / Design Verification team at Marvell, you will help verify complex semiconductor solutions that power next-generation cloud data center switching silicon. Our Switch group builds the switching systems that is used by hyperscale customers, so the quality of our verification work directly determines whether these chips work correctly in production.

What You Can Expect

• Use industry-standard verification tools and methodologies — Verilog/SystemVerilog, UVM, and C — to verify SoC designs.

• Help design, implement, and maintain UVM testbench components and other verification collateral to support functional verification.

• Develop simulation test environments and components such as testbenches, checkers, and monitors.

• Analyze design specifications to help build test plans, develop test cases, run simulations, and analyze results against coverage goals.

• Use problem-solving skills to debug failing simulations, work with designers to resolve issues, and analyze functional/code coverage.

• Automate data collection and analysis using Python or other scripting languages.

What We're Looking For

• Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical/Computer Engineering, Computer Science.

• Coursework or project experience with Verilog/SystemVerilog; familiarity with UVM-based verification methodologies is a plus.

• Understanding of digital logic design, finite state machines, and computer architecture fundamentals.

• Working knowledge of C/C++ and scripting languages (Python, Perl, or Tcl).

• Comfortable working in a Linux environment.

• Strong communication and problem-solving skills; enthusiasm for learning new verification methodologies.
