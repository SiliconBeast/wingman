The Role:

• You will work closely with a hardware engineering team to support ground and airborne navigation products

• You will assist with capturing technical requirements

• You will create electrical design artifacts including schematics, printed circuit boards (PCB), and circuit card assemblies

• You will perform preliminary design and analysis activities

• You will support integration and verification testing

• You will generate and review electrical design documents, drawings, procedures and reports.

• You will assist in transitioning products to manufacturing and provide electrical design support

• You will support design and integration efforts with other engineering disciplines including mechanical, software, systems, and test engineering as necessary.
