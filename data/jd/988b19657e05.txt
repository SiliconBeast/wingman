Job Description

The Robotics Research Co-op is apart of the Robotics Centre of Excellence (RCoE). The RCoE is the "innovation engine" of Rockwell Automation robotics. We are a dynamic, research group dedicated pre-programmed automation and ushering in the new era of Autonomous Operations. Here, you'll work with world-class experts in a culture that prizes rapid prototyping and the desire to see "impossible" ideas working on the factory floor.

We are looking for a passionate Robotics Research Intern who is at the intersection of AI theory and hands-on implementation. You will work with senior researchers, gaining invaluable hands-on experience with the latest embodied intelligence. Your work will directly help develop next-generation Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, World Models, and Reinforcement Learning (RL) frameworks.

Relocation assistance is not provided.

Your Responsibilities:

•
Data Science: Collect, manage, and process physical AI datasets to promote development of industrial tasks

•
Physical AI Development: Train, fine-tune, and evaluate multi-modal robotics models for real-world industrial tasks.

•
Simulation-to-Real: Develop "sim-to-real" pipelines using them to accelerate policy training.

•
Software Excellence: Apply modern system programming languages and following software engineering best practices.

•
Distribution: Document your research through technical reports, white papers, and publications.
