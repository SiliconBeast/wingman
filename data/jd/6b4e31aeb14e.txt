Currently pursuing a bachelor’s degree, diploma, or Electronic Engineering Technology program at an accredited Canadian post-secondary institution (2nd/3rd year or equivalent), with relevant academic or co-op experience.

The team you will be a part of

Join Nokia’s IP Routing division, where we build industry-leading networking technology that powers high-speed connectivity around the world. From next-generation network processors to large-scale routing systems, our innovations help connect people, businesses, and communities globally.

As a student in the hardware group, you’ll work with an experienced team developing hardware solutions for Nokia’s routing platforms. Gain hands-on lab experience with prototype circuit boards and receive guidance, feedback, and career support from a dedicated mentor. This is an on-site role with office attendance required.

As part of the team, you will:

• Verify and debug circuit designs using oscilloscopes and other test gear.

• Test hardware under thermal and environmental conditions.

• Complete the final assembly of new prototype units.

• Qualify new circuit components

• Develop custom automation scripts (Perl, Python, TCL) to control test equipment.

You must have:

• A solid foundation in hardware electronics concepts.

• Familiarity with electronic test equipment such as oscilloscopes and multimeters.

• Have completed at least one co-op work term, a 4-month paid internship, or have equivalent paid work experience.

• A proactive attitude, a willingness to learn quickly, and the ability to grasp technical concepts with ease.

It would be nice if you also had:

• Excellent communication skills, both written and oral.

• Good problem-solving skills.

• Ability to manage tasks with minimal supervision.
