Blackstone Technology & Innovations (BXTI)

Blackstone Technology & Innovations (BXTI) is the technology organization powering one of the world's leading alternative asset managers. We build the platforms, applications, data systems, and automation capabilities that help Blackstone operate at scale, make investment decisions, manage risk, and deliver value to investors and portfolio companies.

At BXTI, engineers work directly on business-critical systems that support Blackstone's global operations. Our teams are highly collaborative, entrepreneurial, and focused on solving complex problems with technology. Interns are treated as engineers from day one and are given opportunities to contribute meaningful work alongside experienced developers, architects, and product teams.

Job Description

As a Summer Analyst in Software Engineering, you will help design, build, and support technology solutions used across Blackstone's businesses. Depending on team placement, you may work on software applications, cloud platforms, data systems, infrastructure automation, AI-enabled solutions, or internal developer platforms.

You will collaborate with engineers, product managers, data practitioners, and business stakeholders to solve real-world problems and deliver solutions that are scalable, resilient, and impactful.

Our teams leverage modern software engineering practices, cloud technologies, automation, and data platforms, including AI-assisted development tools and firm approved standards, to build systems that support critical business functions across the firm.

How You'll Make an Impact

•
Design, build, test, and support software solutions used across the firm

•
Develop applications, services, APIs, and automation capabilities

•
Build scalable systems that process and manage large volumes of data

•
Contribute to cloud-native architectures and platform engineering initiatives

•
Partner with engineers, product managers, and business stakeholders to turn ideas into production solutions

•
Participate in code reviews, testing, and software delivery processes

•
Write and review code with AI-assisted development tools, using firm-approved plugins, skills, and standards that accelerate the development lifecycle

•
Improve operational efficiency by automating repetitive tasks and workflows

•
Learn and apply modern engineering practices, tools, and technologies
