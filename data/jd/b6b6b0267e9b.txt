Key job responsibilities
We are seeking data-driven, business-minded, results-oriented interns pursuing a bachelor's or sub-matriculation master's degree in accounting. Your internship will consist of a project unique to you and your team, where you'll flex your accounting, analytical, and business acumen to optimize processes, drive quality, and solve real problems. In this role, you will partner with teams across accounting, finance, operations, and technology organizations within Amazon. Internships culminate with a capstone project presentation, where you'll showcase your results to stakeholders and leadership. If Amazon extends an inclined hire decision, you may receive an offer to join Amazon's full-time Accounting Finance Rotation Program (AFRP) after completing your degree.
