WHAT WE DO 

Gecko Robotics is helping the world’s most important organizations ensure the availability, reliability, and sustainability of critical infrastructure. Gecko's complete and connected solutions combine wall-climbing robots, industry-leading sensors, and an AI-powered data platform to provide customers with a unique window into the current and future health of their physical assets. This enables real-time decision making to increase the efficiency and safety of operations, promote mission readiness, and protect the environment and civilization from the effects of infrastructure failure.

Role at a Glance

Gecko Robotics is seeking a Full Stack Software Engineering Intern to join our Engineering team in New York for Summer 2027. This is a full-time, onsite internship, Monday through Friday.

Gecko’s Engineering teams build customer-facing applications that turn robot, sensor, and document data into decisions about the health of critical infrastructure. You will work across the stack on a defined project: building frontend workflows, backend services, and data pipelines that make complex asset data usable by the people who maintain it. You will ship real features into a product that customers already rely on, with clear direction from a mentor and the room to own your piece of it.

Due to government program requirements, candidates must be authorized to work in the United States.

What you will do

 - Own a scoped full-stack project from design through deployment under the guidance of experienced engineers.

 - Build user-facing features in React and TypeScript that support real operational workflows.

 - Develop backend APIs and services in Python that ingest, transform, and serve structured and unstructured data.

 - Design data models and schemas, and write the logic that parses, associates, and validates data from multiple sources.

 - Use version control, code review, testing, and structured debugging to produce maintainable code.

 - Collaborate with engineers, product managers, and Forward Deployment Engineers to understand how customers use the product, with the option to travel to a customer site during the summer.

 - Present your project, results, and recommended next steps at the end of the internship.

Technologies We Use

React, TypeScript, Python, PostgreSQL, REST APIs, Git, Linux, and cloud infrastructure (GCP). Depending on the project, you may also work with LLM APIs and data-processing pipelines, 3D visualization, or containerized deployments. The specific tools will depend on the assigned project.

About You

You are a builder who likes seeing your work used. You are comfortable moving between frontend and backend, you break large problems into shippable pieces, and you communicate clearly about what you built, what you learned, and what you would do next.

 - Ability to work onsite in New York, Monday through Friday, for the full Summer 2027 internship.

 - Foundational programming experience in Python and JavaScript or TypeScript, including object-oriented or functional programming concepts.
