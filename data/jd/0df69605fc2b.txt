As a Test Engineering Intern, you will work alongside experienced engineers developing and optimizing test solutions for advanced application processors and SoC products. You will gain hands-on experience with new product introduction (NPI), silicon characterization, generative AI tools, and Automated Test Equipment (ATE) while contributing to real engineering projects.

Job Summary:

• Develop test solutions for advanced SoC devices using Automated Test Equipment (ATE)

• Assist with silicon characterization, validation, and debug activities

• Develop scripts and tools to improve engineering productivity and test efficiency

• Analyze test data to identify trends, yield opportunities, and quality issues

• Collaborate with Design, Product Engineering, Validation, Quality, and Manufacturing teams

Learning Opportunities:

• Advanced application processors and SoC products

• Automated Test Equipment (ATE)

• Silicon characterization, debug, yield improvement, and data analytics

• Engineering automation, scripting, and AI-assisted engineering workflows

Skills / Requirements:

• Pursuing a BS/MS in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or related field

• Strong analytical, communication, and teamwork skills

• Experience with Python, C#, VBA, Linux, generative AI tools or similar technologies

• Coursework or interest in computer architecture, digital design, verification, and programming
