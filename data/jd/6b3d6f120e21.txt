This early career Intern Electrical Engineering position allows you to grow your skills as a member of the Product Development team - a multi-disciplinary engineering development team that includes System Engineering, Electrical System and Power Conversion Design, Mechanical Design, Control Hardware Design, Grid Regulation coordination, Software Design, and Integration / Test.

Job Description

What You will Do:

Wind Engineering Interns on the Salem, Virginia design team experience technically challenging, meaningful, and creative assignments within the Electrical & Grid Lifecycle Subsystem. As an intern, you'll have the opportunity to work in one of several functional areas: Electrical Systems Design, Firmware Engineering, Controls Engineering, Printed Circuit Board Design, Mechanical Design, and Lab Validation. Your work will support design and development of wind turbine Blade Pitch control systems, and wind turbine Power Converter systems.

You will work in a hands-on lab environment, with equipment for full-scale testing of megawatt-level power conversion equipment for agency compliance, grid compatibility, reliability, and more. You will work on custom build-to-print hardware (cabinets, circuit boards, etc.), with the opportunity to see your schematics and drawings grow into physical products that GE Vernova supports through their entire lifecycle of design, manufacturing, and fleet support.

Engineering Interns are the pipeline for roles at GE Vernova. Candidates (rising undergraduate seniors or graduating master’s candidates) with a strong interest in and aptitude for technical development for Power Conversion, Controls, and Electronics design will have the opportunity to apply for full-time roles supporting these functions during the internship.

All internships will take place between May and August 2027.
