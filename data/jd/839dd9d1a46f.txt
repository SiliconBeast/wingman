Job Title: 2027 Summer Analyst – Sales & Trading and Equity Research

Location: New York, NY

Duration: 10 weeks

Start Date: June 2027

Citizens Bank is seeking Summer Analysts to join the Sales & Trading and Equity Research teams in New York, NY or Boston, MA. Summer Analysts will have the unique opportunity to rotate between the Sales & Trading and Equity Research teams within one of the largest regional banks in the country. Over the course of the 10-week internship program, Summer Analysts will work closely with the teams’ junior and senior professionals to gain significant industry exposure and business insights.

Summer Analysts will split their time between the Sales & Trading team and the Equity Research team, transitioning halfway through the summer program. At the conclusion of the 10-week internship program, high-performing Summer Analysts will be considered for a potential offer for direct employment with the Equity Sales & Trading and/or Equity Research teams (post-graduation).
