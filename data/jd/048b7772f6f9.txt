Job Description

Build the future with us
Are you driven by software development and eager to the purpose of a company that aims to ensure its clients feel confident and secure about their future? As an Intern – Developer , you will play a key role in the analysis, development, integration, and quality assurance of our technological solutions in order to meet various business needs.

This position represents a great opportunity to showcase your skills and fully leverage your potential within a caring and reliable company. Here, people and their development are at the heart of priorities, fostering an environment that encourage collaboration and innovation.

What you will accomplish with us

As an Intern – Developer , you will be at the heart of our mission. Here are the main responsibilities:

• Collaborate in the analysis and implementation of technical solutions for different functionalities;

• Actively contribute to the development and integration of our functionalities;

• Test developed functionalities by writing and executing test plans to validate development quality;

• Participate in the delivery of solutions in different technological environments;

• Work in collaboration with IT and business teams to propose solutions for improving and optimizing our existing solutions.

What could accelerate your success in this role

We are looking for someone who:

• Is currently a student in a bachelor’s degree in computer science and must complete a credited internship in Winter 2027 as part of their studies (the student will need to provide a duly completed internship agreement);

• Has a good knowledge of JavaScript;

• Is recognized for their motivation to learn and their interest in Web development;

• Stands out for their ability to collaborate in a DEVOPS and AGILE environment;

• Has an interest marked knowledgean interest marked for the use of artificial intelligence tools and the Office Suite;

• Demonstrates great autonomy in carrying out technical tasks and problem-solving;

• Has an advanced level of knowledge of the French language since the person will be required to collaborate with unilingual French-speaking internal partners and consult technical documentation in French on a daily basis;

• Has an intermediate level of knowledge of the English language since the person will be required to collaborate with unilingual English-speaking internal partners and consult technical documentation in French on a daily basis.

Why you will enjoy working with us

• A work environment where learning and development merge with a collective pursuit of excellence.

• Artificial intelligence is in everyone's hands, enabling you to increase your personal efficiency and supporting the organization's growth.

• A healthy, safe, equitable, and inclusive environment where potential can express and develop freely.

• The possibility of working in a hybrid work environment, supported by flexibility and access to inspiring and innovative workspaces.

• Benefits for students: access to internal training, mentoring, involvement in real projects, flexible schedules adapted to studies, and a rewarding experience for your professional path.
