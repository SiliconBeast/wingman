Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all .

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

As an intern in the Dry Etch Equipment Development Engineering group at Micron, you will gain hands-on experience in developing, crafting, and optimizing plasma etch equipment for Micron's groundbreaking memory parts. Together, we will tackle ambitious technical problems to further develop innovative equipment to enable the Micron product roadmap.

This position requires that you have a strong curiosity about engineering problem solving and innovation, and the skills to work both independently and in a team. We strive to provide an immersive experience into semiconductor dry etch hardware development.

Responsibilities include, but are not limited to:

• Demonstrate personal ownership of safety, quality, and compliance while working in lab, fab, and equipment-development environments.

• Presenting and clearly articulating your work.

• Conducting experiments to understand the technical limitations of current equipment capability.

• Developing in-process hardware and analytical techniques.

• Maintain high standards of integrity in data collection, analysis, documentation, and communication of technical results.

• Solving sophisticated engineering problems related to plasma etch hardware.

• Collaborate respectfully with cross-functional teams, valuing diverse perspectives to solve complex equipment and process challenges.

Minimum Qualifications:

• Pursuing a career in semiconductor industry and working towards a B.S., M.S., or Ph.D in Engineering or a related Science field (Physics, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, etc.)

• Must be a current student, and cannot graduate before September 2027.

• An electromechanical or hardware-derived work history/education with an understanding of semiconductor equipment.

• Strong digital literacy with the ability to quickly learn new programs, responsibly use AI-enabled tools, and apply data analysis to support guided technical decisions..

Preferred Qualifications:

• Effective interpersonal skills with customers, collaborators, and managers

• Ability to work with minimal direction and having a focus on meeting commitments.

• Show a willingness to learn, seek feedback, adapt quickly, and build technical capability through hands-on experience.
