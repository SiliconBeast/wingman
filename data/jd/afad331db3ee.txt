Role details

We are currently hiring for a 4-month paid internship/co-op in Analytics & Insights from May 3, 2027 – Aug 20, 2027 at our Toronto headquarters.

What does the work look like?

Have you ever wondered how companies decide which products to launch, how to connect with consumers, or where to invest for growth? At P&G, Analytics & Insights is at the heart of business decision-making. We are experts on consumer and marketing understanding and insights. We combine analytics, consumer understanding, and market insights to influence business strategy, product innovation, pricing, media investments, retail execution, and more. As Champions of the Consumer voice , we help shape the future of some of P&G's most recognized brands.

What You'll Do

• Work as an internal business strategy partner, ensuring consumer and shopper insights are at the foundation of business decisions.

• Analyze consumer, customer, market, and business data to uncover insights and opportunities.

• Develop clear, compelling stories that connect data to business action.

• Help shape strategies by integrating analytics, market trends, and consumer understanding.

• Lead consumer research and analytics projects to understand consumer needs, behaviors, and motivations.

• Partner with Brand Management, Sales, Finance, R&D, and other functions to solve business challenges and drive growth.

• Contribute to identifying growth opportunities and influencing business strategy through consumer and shopper insights.

• Use state-of-the-start analytics tools to dig deep into the data, identifying key business drivers and assessing the impact of investments like advertising and in-store promotions.

No two days are the same. One day you may be exploring consumer behavior and emerging trends; the next, analyzing data to identify growth opportunities for a leading P&G brand or customer. From Day 1, you'll do meaningful work with real business impact.

You are a great fit if you:

• Have strong analytical skills, using multiple data sources. Comfort with data analysis is essential.

• Demonstrate curiosity, initiative, and a passion for learning.

• Have strong communication and storytelling skills. Can simplify complex information into clear insights and recommendations.

• Thrive in collaborative, team-based environments.
