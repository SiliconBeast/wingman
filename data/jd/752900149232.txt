What You Will Do

As a Systems Engineering Intern at Collins Aerospace (RTX), you will support a multidisciplinary engineering team by contributing to verification activities, structured analysis, and documentation efforts within a complex aerospace systems environment.

• Assist with requirements interpretation, structured testing activities, and analysis of system behavior.

• Document engineering observations, compile evidence, and communicate results to mentors and teammates.

• Contribute to process‑focused improvements such as standardizing documentation formats and strengthening traceability between requirements, evaluations, and results.

• Collaborate with engineers across multiple disciplines, gaining hands‑on exposure to real‑world systems engineering workflows.

• Participate in regular team meetings to review progress, plan weekly objectives, and share findings.

• Demonstrate strong ownership of assigned tasks and follow through to completion.

• Ask clear, well‑formed technical questions and seek guidance appropriately.

• Maintain professionalism, reliability, and strong communication across all interactions.

• Work independently when appropriate while knowing when to involve teammates or mentors.

Qualifications You Must Have

• Must be pursuing a Bachelor’s or Advanced degree in Aerospace Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Systems Engineering, or a closely related STEM discipline, and actively enrolled through the completion of the intern session.

• Must have a minimum of 60 credit hours by the start of the internship.

• Experience with structured analytical or engineering workflows (may include coursework, research, projects, or prior work).

• Experience with technical documentation or communicating technical findings in a clear written format.

• Experience may be obtained through on‑the‑job experience, research, coursework, or projects.

Qualifications We Prefer

• Prior RTX internship, co‑op, or aerospace-related experience

• Familiarity with structured requirements interpretation or verification concepts

• Experience working independently in unfamiliar technical domains.

• Strong interpersonal communication and teamwork skills.
