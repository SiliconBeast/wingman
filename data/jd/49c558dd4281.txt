Job Description:

As an Applications Engineering Intern in the Data Center Solutions Business Unit of Microchip, you will be assisting the Applications team in developing tools and demos, board bring-up and debugging, setting up lab equipment to analyze and recreate customer system problems, assisting in customer collaterals, embedded firmware debug/development and assisting in technical support. You must be able to work independently, and in a team environment.

Responsibilities

• Setting up lab equipment including installation of OS, Drivers, tools, debuggers, analyzers etc.

• Bring-up and debug customer evaluation boards

• Develop and maintain customer collaterals, custom firmware and diagnostic tools

• Recreate, collect, analyze, debug and root cause customer reported problems in a lab environment

• Develop and provide product training to customers and internal teams

• Demonstrate new product features, capabilities in industry events and trade shows

• Performance and power measurement and analysis

Requirements/Qualifications:

• Currently enrolled in BS or MS Electrical/Computer/Computer Science Engineering program.

• Basic understanding of computer architecture

• Programming experience in any one of the following: C, C++, TCL/Tk, Python, Java

• Good troubleshooting and debugging skills

• Good writing and communication skills

Desired Qualifications

• Experience with installing OSes (Linux and Windows), setting up hardware and drivers

• Strong knowledge or experience in any one of the protocols or technologies (e.g. NVMe, PCI-Express, SAS, SATA, Fibre Channel, Ethernet, DDR, NAND Flash, UART, SPI etc.)

• Experience with board-level debug & bring-up.

• Experience with HW design, schematics capture, and board layout.

• Experience with driver development / embedded FW development.
