Job Description:

Microchip Technology Inc. is a global leader in the design, manufacture, and marketing of Frequency Control, Sensor, and Hybrid Product solutions using advanced, cutting-edge technologies. For more than 70 years, our Mount Holly Springs facility has supported critical applications including Satellite Constellations, Deep Space Exploration Missions, Military Systems, GPS, and Communication Networks.

We are seeking motivated students currently pursuing an Associate’s Degree in Electrical Engineering or a related technical field to join our team as an Electronics Technician Intern . This is a hands-on opportunity to gain real-world experience in electronic testing and high-precision manufacturing within an industry-leading organization.

On-the-job training is provided. We are committed to developing future technical talent and will equip you with the tools, mentorship, and support needed to succeed.

Essential Responsibilities:

•
Read and interpret process documentation to perform automated and manual electronic testing

•
Interpret basic test data and determine pass/fail results

•
Operate standard electronic test equipment (e.g., multimeter) and automated test systems

•
Perform basic soldering of surface-mount components

•
Maintain quality and safety standards in all tasks

•
Support team objectives and perform other duties as assigned

Requirements/Qualifications:

• Currently pursuing an Associate’s Degree in Electrical Engineering or related field

• Basic knowledge of electronic and electrical principles

• Quality- and safety-minded

• Detail-oriented with strong organizational skills

• Ability to work independently and in a team environment

• Basic PC and math skills

• Good written and verbal communication skills

• High School Diploma or GED required

Schedule:

• M-F, 7am-3:30pm
