Job Overview:

The Product Management Internship at LPL Financial is designed for analytically driven students who are passionate about solving complex problems at the intersection of finance, technology, and client experience. This role offers hands-on exposure to the product lifecycle, enabling interns to contribute to initiatives that enhance advisor workflows, improve platform capabilities, and drive meaningful business outcomes.

Successful candidates bring a structured, user-centric approach to understanding advisor needs, identifying process inefficiencies, and supporting the development of solutions that create value for both advisors and their clients. They are strong communicators who can translate complex concepts into clear, actionable insights for technical and non-technical stakeholders alike. With a foundation in data analysis and problem-solving, they are comfortable leveraging data to inform recommendations and support product decision- making.

Responsibilities:

•
Analyze and map advisor workflows to identify opportunities for process improvement and enhanced user experiences (e.g., client onboarding, account management, portfolio rebalancing).

•
Evaluate product and feature adoption metrics, uncover insights, and recommend enhancements that improve advisor engagement and efficiency.

•
Create, update, and refine product documentation, user guides, and training materials to support advisor understanding and adoption.

•
Assist with product delivery activities, including backlog refinement, user story development, acceptance criteria creation, and defect triage.

•
Collaborate with product managers, technology teams, designers, and business stakeholders to support product initiatives and ongoing enhancements.

•
Participate in product demos, testing activities, feedback sessions, and continuous improvement efforts.

•
Conduct research and analysis to support product strategy, roadmap planning, and operational decision-making.

Company Overview:

LPL Financial Holdings Inc. (Nasdaq: LPLA) was founded on the principle that the firm should work for advisors and institutions, and not the other way around. Today, LPL is a leader in the markets we serve, serving more than 23,000 financial advisors, including advisors at approximately 1,000 institutions and at approximately 580 registered investment advisor ("RIA") firms nationwide. We are steadfast in our commitment to the advisor-mediated model and the belief that Americans deserve access to personalized guidance from a financial professional.

At LPL, independence means that advisors and institution leaders have the freedom they deserve to choose the business model, services, and technology resources that allow them to run a thriving business. They have the flexibility to do business their way. And they have the freedom to manage their client relationships, because they know their clients best. Simply put, we take care of our advisors and institutions, so they can take care of their clients.
