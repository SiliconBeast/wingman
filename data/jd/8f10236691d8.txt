This is an existing vacancy.

Your Team, Your Impact
As an analog IC designer, you’ll be a member of the Data Center Networking business group, focusing on the Photonic Fabric. You’ll be part of an analog team working on silicon photonics for scale-up networking. Additionally, Marvell has the perfect size and scale for you to learn several aspects of engineering that will be new to you, but also have the time and freedom to dive deep into the details of your specialization on most projects.
You will gain exposure to a variety of IC design engineering skills and tools during your contract period.

What You Can Expect

• Use the most advanced technology nodes (3nm FinFET)

• Design integrated circuits for electrical and electro-optical interfaces at 100+ Gb/s.

• Learn a variety of CAD tools, primarily Cadence, to design and simulate high performance analog circuits.

• Learn advanced techniques in analog and high-speed layout.

• Work in a team and collaborate with people for effective outcomes.

What We're Looking For

• Currently working on your Master or PhD thesis

• Must have completed or be in the process of a circuit design or chip tape out

• Academic knowledge of CMOS analog design. Basics of RF design techniques are a plus

• Understanding of CMOS processes and fabrication.

• Basic knowledge of IC layout.

• Self-motivated personality, willing to learn exciting new technologies, and able to work effectively within a talented group of individuals.

• Strong written and verbal communication skills.
