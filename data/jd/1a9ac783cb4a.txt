Job Responsibilities:

About the Role:

As an Engineering Systems Intern, you will have the opportunity to work closely with our Engineering Systems Manager and team to develop and improve manufacturing systems.

Your responsibilities :

•
Assist in managing Engineering Systems initiatives.

•
Support Engineering system operational metrics and related systems.

•
Aid in ensuring compliance with customer engineering system requirements across facilities.

•
Assist Program Managers with aligning timing of engineering system deliverables.

•
Contribute to the development of internal expertise within Engineering teams.

•
Support technical reviews and sales activities through the quoting process.

•
Assist in developing and maintaining engineering change systems.

•
Help analyze and adapt standards to market feedback and customer needs.

•
Support team in capturing labor and capital increases in engineering changes.

•
All other duties assigned.
