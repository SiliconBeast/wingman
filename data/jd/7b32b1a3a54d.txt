Job Details

Summary:

Cencora's leading Internship Program is a 10-week opportunity, spanning multiple functions throughout the organization. The program is designed to help you take your leadership abilities, academic achievement, and business skills to the next level. As interns, students lead innovation by working on impactful projects that influence the future of healthcare support and delivery. This program aligns with Cencora’s commitment to fostering talent and contributing to the growth of the pharmaceutical industry.

Position Overview:

We are seeking motivated and enthusiastic interns to join our Team. This internship is designed for individuals who are passionate about learning functional and information technology aspects of the pharmaceutical industry and eager to gain hands-on experience in a dynamic work environment.

Primary Responsibilities:

• Work on technical projects with various Information Technology teams.

• Complete coding and other technical assignments given by team.

• Learn and use various software development tools.

• Edit, modify and debug software modules in accordance with software architecture specifications and internal procedures.

• Attend daily stand-up meetings to discuss assigned tasks and collaborate with team.

• Build dashboard and/or reports to provide insight and encourage self-service for business stakeholders.

• Assist with documentation and testing as assigned.

Qualifications:

The requirements for this position are as follows:

• Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering or similar major fields of study.

• Currently enrolled in undergraduate school with an upcoming completion of a bachelor’s degree.

• Basic database\SQL experience or any similar database general experience required.

• Knowledge or experience in one or more of the following technologies:

Experience in Angular or similar UI Web technologies:

o Knowledge of Angular.

o Experience with Angular CLI for project setup and management.

o Understanding of Angular components, services, directives, and modules.

o Knowledge of Angular forms and validation.

o Familiarity with Angular routing and navigation.

Bootstrap Expertise:

o Knowledge on HTML, CSS, and JavaScript/jQuery for Bootstrap integration.

o Experience with Bootstrap 3 or 4.

o Ability to create responsive and mobile-first web designs using Bootstrap.

o Knowledge of Bootstrap components such as grids, forms, modals, and navigation bars.

Front-end Development Skills:

o Understanding of front-end development principles.

o Knowledge of HTML5 and CSS3 for creating modern and visually appealing user interfaces.

o Knowledge of CSS preprocessors like Sass or Less.

JavaScript and TypeScript:

o Experience with JavaScript and TypeScript for client-side scripting.

o Understanding of asynchronous programming and Promises.

o Experience with AJAX, RESTful API integration, and handling HTTP requests.

Minimum skills, knowledge and ability requirements:

• Proficiency in clear and articulate communication, both in spoken and written forms.

• Proficient project management capabilities.

• Demonstrated interpersonal, teamwork, and leadership aptitude.

• Effective analytical, conceptual, and problem-solving abilities for assessing business challenges and implementing suitable solutions.

• A strong inclination towards learning, self-motivation, adaptability, accountability, and adept multitasking.

• Exceptional organizational and time-management proficiencies.

• Competency in using computer applications such as PowerPoint, Excel, and Microsoft Outlook for efficient operations.

What We Offer:

• Hands-on Experience: Interns engage in real-world projects that allow them to apply their academic knowledge and learn about the pharmaceutical industry.

• Mentorship: Participants receive guidance from experienced mentors who provide support, feedback, and insights into the industry.

• Networking Opportunities: Interns have the chance to connect with professionals across various departments, helping them expand their professional network. Network and gain Insights from business leaders through speaker series and professional development sessions.

• Skill Development: The program focuses on developing both technical and soft skills, preparing interns for future careers in the industry.

• Diversity of Roles: Internships may be offered in various areas, including marketing, operations, finance, research and development, and more, catering to a wide range of academic backgrounds.

• Potential for Full-Time Opportunities: Successful interns may have the chance to be considered for full-time positions after completing their studies.
