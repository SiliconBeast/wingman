ABOUT THE ROLE & TEAM

Getting an autonomy stack onto a 40-ton excavator is a hardware problem long before it's a software one — brackets, harnesses, enclosures, and interfaces that survive vibration, dust, and weather. As our Machine Integration intern, you'll design and build the physical interfaces between our system and the machine, plus the test stands we use to prove them out before they ever hit a jobsite.




WHAT YOU'LL DO

 - Integrate sensors, compute, and wiring onto machines alongside the hardware team

 - Design brackets, enclosures, and interface hardware in CAD, then help fabricate and install them

 - Build test stands for validating subsystems on the bench instead of in the field

 - Run lab tests, capture data, and iterate on designs based on what breaks

 - Document build and install procedures so the next machine goes faster than the last

 - Close the loop with the field team on what's working and what isn't


WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR


REQUIRED

 - Currently pursuing a BS or MS in mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, robotics, or a related field — or bringing equivalent hands-on experience

 - CAD proficiency (SolidWorks, Onshape, or similar)

 - Genuine hands-on shop skills — you've built physical things, not just modeled them

 - Basic electrical competence: harnessing, connectors, multimeter, reading a schematic

 - Willingness to work on and around heavy equipment in PPE


PREFERRED

 - Wiring harness design or manufacturing experience

 - Exposure to off-highway, automotive, or industrial equipment

 - Machining, welding, or 3D printing experience
