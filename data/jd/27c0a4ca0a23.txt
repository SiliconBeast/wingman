Le rôle que nous vous proposons :

En intégrant notre équipe, vous aurez l’occasion de de travailler dans l’équipe de générateur d’image. • Apprendre et évoluer dans le monde hautement technologique des systèmes visuels et simulateurs de vol.

• Travailler dans un environnement de simulation complètement intégré pour valider et intégrer différentes technologies.

• Utiliser C ++ dans un environnement Windows avec Visual Studio pour le développement.

• Contribuer à l'amélioration continue des logiciels CAE et des processus de développement.

• Utilisez les technologies de pointes en matériel « PC » (GPU& CPU).

• Travailler en collaboration dans une équipe avec un mixte d’expertise donc certains experts du domaine des générateurs d'images.

Le candidat idéal que nous recherchons possède:

• Bonne connaissance du développement orienté objet, en C ++, sous Windows.

• Connaissance de la programmation système (bas niveau) sous Windows.

• Connaissance d'OpenGL, des concepts de visualisation 3D, du shader GLSL.

• Connaissance de l'optique, des systèmes d'affichage, des interfaces vidéo, de l'architecture et des capacités graphiques des GPU modernes.

C-GE-925 - C++ Backend Developer

Are you looking for a work environment where you can innovate, reach greater heights and collaborate with experts from a wide range of sectors? You want to join a world-class company and industry leader with projects to which you would be proud to contribute? Come share your passion with us!

The role we are offering you:

By joining our team, you will have the opportunity to to in the Image generator team. You will have the opportunity to participate in the development and integration of software components that offer pilots a simulation environment of unmatched realism. Our department is responsible for display solutions, visual content and rendering, sound effects and motion generation, as well as the development of artificial intelligence for other "players" in military simulation scenarios. The software you will be contributing to will be deployed on flight simulators that will help train several pilots around the world.

Are you ready to:

• Learn and evolve in the highly technological world of visual systems and flight simulator’s.

• Work in a fully integrated simulation environment to validate and integrate different technologies.

• Use C++ in a Windows environment with Visual Studio for the development.

• Contribute to the continuous improvement of CAE software and development processes.

• Work in collaboration with a team containing mixed expertise such as Image Generator industry experts.

Our ideal candidate has:

• Good knowledge of object-oriented development, in C ++, under Windows.

• Knowledge of system programming (low level) under Windows.

• Knowledge of OpenGL, 3D visualization concepts, GLSL shader.

• Knowledge of optics, display systems, video interfaces, architecture and graphics capabilities of modern GPUs.

• Bilingual (French and English).

Preferred Education

• Pursuing a bachelor's degree in software engineering or computer engineering

Location of the internship is CAE Montréal.

Internship will be carried out in hybrid mode.
