The Value You Deliver

•
Collecting and preparing data necessary to analyze benefit programs

•
Maintaining organized storage of client documentation (e.g., Summary Plan Documents ( SPDs ) , contracts, employee communication)

•
Assisting in the development of health and welfare program pricing, reserving, budgets, financial dashboard reporting, employee contributions and premium equivalent rates for self- funded plans

•
Assisting in the negotiation of rates, contractual provisions and performance guarantees with current benefit vendors

•
Performing marketing analysis including RFP creation, proposal collection, and vendor comparisons; Assisting with vendor selection, performance criteria, pricing negotiations and contractual provisions
