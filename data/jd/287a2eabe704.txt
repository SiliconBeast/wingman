The Finance and Strategy team partners across Robinhood to drive long-term shareholder value by helping business leaders make strategic, financially sound decisions. We provide insights through analytics, build scalable frameworks for financial management, and help align the company’s operations and capital allocation to our corporate priorities.

As a Derivatives Finance & Strategy (F&S) summer intern, you will collaborate with finance leads to provide financial insights into two of Robinhood’s newest business lines - Futures and Prediction Markets. This position will assist in linking business and operational decisions with financial outcomes, playing a direct role in setting and tracking revenue targets and keeping leadership advised of key trends. A successful intern will be able to build insightful financial models, dashboards and presentations that are ready for executive-level consumption to drive strategic decisions across the Derivatives business.

This role is based in our Menlo Park, CA office with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

What you’ll do

• Synthesize and distill financial data and provide important insights for leadership

• Partner closely with business partners from product management, engineering, legal, partnerships, and accounting to evaluate new products, communicate performance vs. budgets and forecasts, and identify action items

• Prepare key financial decks weekly, monthly, and quarterly; highlight actionable trends and insights for Brokerage product team leads

• Construct P&L, unit economic, and cash flow models and summarize finance recommendations for business partners

• Assist in improving and simplifying processes, drive standardization, and build scalable solutions to support the next wave of Robinhood's growth

What you bring

• Currently enrolled in a full-time, degree-seeking program in business, finance, economics or a related field with an expected graduation date in Winter 2027/Spring 2028

• Ability to influence decisions through effective verbal and written communications, logical reasoning, and the presentation of alternatives

• Excellent analytical skills, a passion for metrics, and the business judgment to balance the details without losing sight of the big picture

• Advanced proficiency in spreadsheet and presentation software, including Google Sheets, Google Slides, Excel, and PowerPoint

• A positive attitude — a desire to work hard, be creative, stay curious, and maintain a can-do approach
