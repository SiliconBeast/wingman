Your Team, Your Impact

Marvell’s Design Verification teams span across multiple domains, giving you the opportunity to work on technologies that power next-generation cloud, AI, storage, and networking solutions. Some teams focus on pre-silicon emulation and verification for ODSP-related chips. Other teams focus on ASIC Design, where they testbench with UVM methodology and test specific SOC designs at block/cluster/full chip levels. Other teams focus on powering the full range of network connectivity from the optics of the AI GPU boards to top of the rack optical fibers and active copper cables of the data centers and their fabrics. Across all groups, you’ll collaborate with your direct team and cross-functional partners (across software, physical design, digital design, and more) to deliver robust, scalable solutions that enable Marvell’s leadership in data infrastructure.

What You Can Expect
