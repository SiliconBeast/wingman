The Opportunity

We are seeking a Student Intern to support the development, implementation, and continuous improvement of business solutions using Microsoft Power Platform technologies.

This role provides hands-on experience in application development, workflow automation, business intelligence, artificial intelligence, and low-code solution design. The successful candidate will work closely with business stakeholders and technical teams to develop solutions that improve efficiency, automate processes, and enhance business decision-making.

At TC Energy, connection is at the heart of everything we do. As a Student Intern, you will contribute to meaningful work that helps connect people, communities and industries to the energy they rely on every day. You will gain hands-on experience working alongside experienced professionals, applying your academic knowledge to real-world projects and helping support the safe and reliable delivery of energy across North America.

As a valued member of our team, you will take on challenging and rewarding work, build lasting professional relationships and develop skills that will help shape your future career. Through collaboration, mentorship and practical experience, you'll gain insight into our business while contributing to work that matters. Every day presents an opportunity to learn, grow and make an impact while helping power progress for the communities we serve.

• What you'll do

• Assist in designing and developing solutions using Power Apps, including canvas and model-driven applications

• Support workflow automation initiatives using Power Automate to streamline business processes

• Create and maintain dashboards, reports, and visualizations using Power BI

• Explore and support the use of Power Platform Copilot, AI Builder, and other AI capabilities to enhance business solutions

• Collaborate with business stakeholders to gather requirements and translate them into technical solutions

• Participate in testing, debugging, and enhancement activities for existing Power Platform solutions

• Document business processes, applications, technical designs, and solution requirements

• Identify opportunities for automation and contribute to continuous improvement initiatives
