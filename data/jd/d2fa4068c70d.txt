About the Role
Join a dynamic and forward-thinking investment team as an intern and gain hands-on experience in one of several specialized areas: Exchange-Traded Funds (ETFs), Structured Solutions, Alternative Investments, Core Active Investments or Client Portfolio Management. This internship offers a unique opportunity to work alongside experienced portfolio managers and researchers, contributing directly to the development and execution of data-driven investment strategies.

Key Responsibilities

• Assist with recurring and ad hoc reporting for the investment team.

• Help build, update, and maintain reports, dashboards, and data files used to track investment, market, or business activity.

• Prepare charts, tables, and summaries that communicate insights clearly for different audiences.

• Review reports for accuracy, consistency, and completeness before distribution.

• Support the team in improving the structure and usability of existing reporting processes.

• Gather, organize, and summarize data from internal and external sources.

• Assist in preparing investment research summaries, briefing notes, and materials for portfolio managers, analysts, or broader team discussions.

• Support ad hoc analysis related to market trends, product themes, competitor activity, or portfolio positioning.

• Help identify key insights, risks, and considerations from research findings.

• Develop and enhance Excel-based tools, templates, and macros to improve team efficiency.

• Support the development of practical workflow improvements that reduce duplication, improve accuracy, and save time.

• Broader work or accountabilities may be assigned as needed.

•

Qualifications

• Currently enrolled in an undergraduate program, preferably in business, finance, economics, mathematics, data analytics, computer science, engineering, or a related field.

• Strong interest in investments, financial markets, and asset management.

• Strong Excel skills, with experience using formulas, pivot tables, and data organization.

• Experience with VBA, macros, Power Query, Python, Power BI, or other automation and analytics tools would be considered an asset.

• Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

• High attention to detail and a commitment to accuracy.

• Ability to work independently while also collaborating effectively with team members.

• Strong written and verbal communication skills.

• Curious, proactive, and comfortable asking thoughtful questions.

Why BMO? At BMO, we’re committed to fostering innovation and developing future leaders. As a coop student, you’ll gain exposure to real-world investment challenges, receive mentorship from experienced professionals, and contribute meaningfully to our strategic initiatives.
