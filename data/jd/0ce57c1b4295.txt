Charter Manufacturing is a fourth-generation family-owned business where our will to grow drives us to do it better. Join the team and become part of our family!

Charter Manufacturing Company, Inc. is a family-owned group of differentiated metals manufacturing businesses founded in 1936. Our will to grow is our success. It is the result of Charter’s respected team of employees, each person’s dedication to continuous improvement and an entrepreneurial spirit that thrives within the Charter family. Our businesses include Charter Steel, Charter Casting, Charter Wire, and Niles Iron & Metal.

About the Digital Integration Internship :

Gain hands-on experience building and supporting backend software solutions while working with technologies like containers, Kubernetes, Azure web services, Oracle SOA, and Informatica. You’ll develop practical skills in cloud integration, service security, and managing modern digital platforms.

Program Structure:

• Schedule: Part-time (15-20 hours/week) during the academic year and full-time (40 hours/week) during the summer

• Location: Mequon, WI

• Start Date: May 2027

• Duration: Year-round internship

What we're looking for:

• Enrollment in a four-year degree program in Computer Science, Information Systems or IT Management, or a related field with current status of at least a Sophomore level.

• Cumulative GPA of 2.75 or higher.

• Proficient in Microsoft Office software: Excel, Word, and PowerPoint.

• Strong verbal and written communication skills.

• Strong interpersonal and relationship building skills.

• Ability to succeed in a team environment.

• Ability to handle multiple tasks.

What you'll focus on:

• Support and develop application and data integrations across digital systems.

• Build and maintain APIs, backend applications, and integrations using Azure tools.

• Support managed file transfers and data movement between systems.

• Monitor application errors and troubleshoot integration issues to improve reliability and performance.

What Sets Our Internship Program Apart: Work on meaningful projects while receiving mentorship, professional development, and networking opportunities that help launch your career. Our paid internship program also includes signature experiences like Charter's Intern Kickoff Day, volunteer opportunities, professional development workshops, and the Intern Summer Showcase.
