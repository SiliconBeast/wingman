Immuta is the Data Authorization Company, helping the world’s largest organizations control who, and what, can access their data.
The way enterprises access data is fundamentally changing. Organizations are racing to put data to work across analytics and AI, while AI copilots and agents are emerging as an entirely new class of data consumer. Traditional approaches built around tickets, static permissions, and manual approvals were not designed for this speed, scale, or complexity.
Immuta provides the authorization layer for modern data and AI. Our platform enables organizations to provision governed access to data by policy, by request, and dynamically for AI agents, so people and AI can access the data they need while enterprises maintain security, compliance, and control.
For Chief Data Officers, that means making enterprise data dramatically easier to access and use without giving up governance. For AI leaders, it means giving AI agents secure, governed access to enterprise data so they can move from experimentation into production.
Founded in 2015, Immuta is trusted by Fortune 500 companies and government agencies worldwide.

ABOUT THE TEAM
UX Design at Immuta helps make complex data access and governance workflows easier to understand and use. The team brings together design, research, and engineering to understand user problems, explore solutions, and turn them into clear, usable product experiences.
As a UX Design Intern, you’ll work with UX designers and collaborate with product and engineering teammates. You’ll help connect customer needs to what the product actually does and learn how a product team moves from understanding a problem to designing and prototyping a solution.



YOUR INTERNSHIP

At Immuta, interns are treated like members of the team. You’ll join the team’s regular workflow, contribute to real work, and take on responsibilities that reflect your skills and the team’s priorities.

The exact projects will depend on what the team is working on next summer, but you can expect to:

• Explore user and customer problems through research, conversations, workshops, and other discovery activities.

• Turn research and product insights into concepts, user flows, mockups, and prototypes.

• Use design tools to communicate ideas, explain design decisions, and gather feedback.

• Work with engineers and product teammates to understand constraints and shape solutions that can work in the real product.

• Participate in activities such as storyboarding, journey mapping, user interviews, and portfolio or design critiques.

• Use front-end skills, where relevant, to prototype, test, or communicate how an experience could work across responsive and mobile interfaces.

• Share your work, explain your thinking, incorporate feedback, and build confidence working across design, research, and engineering.


TOOLS & TECHNOLOGIES

Depending on your role, you may work with:

• Figma and similar design tools

• Wireframing, mockups, and interactive prototyping

• Storyboarding, journey mapping, user interviews, and other research methods

• HTML and CSS

• Front-end frameworks such as Angular or React

• Responsive and mobile design concepts

• AI-enabled tools and workflows used by your team

You do not need to know every tool listed above. We’re looking for a strong foundation, curiosity, and the ability to learn.


WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR

• A portfolio that demonstrates your design work, prototypes, research, product thinking, or other relevant projects.

• Strong ability in at least one of three areas: visual design, user research, or engineering/front-end development. Experience across two or more is a plus.

• You do not need to be equally strong in all three areas. We’re interested in candidates who can demonstrate clear strength in at least one and curiosity about the others.

• Curiosity about users, products, and how technology can solve real problems.

• Strong communication and collaboration skills.

• Initiative, willingness to learn, and comfort receiving and applying feedback.
