WHAT IS BOX?

Box (NYSE:BOX) is the leader in Intelligent Content Management. Our platform enables organizations to fuel collaboration, manage the entire content lifecycle, secure critical content, and transform business workflows with enterprise AI. We help companies thrive in the new AI-first era of business. Founded in 2005, Box simplifies work for leading global organizations, including JLL, Morgan Stanley, and Nationwide. Box is headquartered in Redwood City, CA, with offices across the United States, Europe, and Asia.

By joining Box, you will have the unique opportunity to continue driving our platform forward. Content powers how we work. It’s the billions of files and information flowing across teams, departments, and key business processes every single day: contracts, invoices, employee records, financials, product specs, marketing assets, and more. Our mission is to bring intelligence to the world of content management and empower our customers to completely transform workflows across their organizations. With the combination of AI and enterprise content, the opportunity has never been greater to transform how the world works together and at Box you will be on the front lines of this massive shift.

WHY BOX NEEDS YOU

As a Cloud & AI Finance Intern, you will play a key role in analyzing and forecasting Box's AI and cloud infrastructure spend. You will partner with Engineering, Product, and Finance teams to model token economics, evaluate model utilization, connect to large telemetry datasets in BigQuery, and deliver actionable insights that optimize Box's investments in cutting-edge AI and cloud technologies.

WHAT YOU'LL DO

•

Token & AI Usage Modeling: Build, maintain, and refine quantitative models tracking AI token consumption, prompt/completion ratios, caching efficiencies, and model inference costs across multiple AI model providers and workloads.

•

Cloud & Infrastructure Spend Analytics: Analyze compute, storage, and infrastructure spend trends across cloud platforms (e.g., GCP/AWS), identifying key cost drivers and variances against forecast.

•

Data Integration & BigQuery Pipelines: Query and connect to Google BigQuery data warehouses to extract large-scale usage telemetry, automate recurring data pipelines, and streamline financial reporting workflows.

•

Model Utilization & Capacity Analysis: Track model usage patterns, agentic workflows, and API call volumes to evaluate infrastructure utilization, capacity planning, and unit economics (e.g., cost per query/workflow).

•

AI Trends & Benchmarking: Research emerging AI industry trends, agent architectures, and pricing/token economics to benchmark Box’s AI cost structures against industry standards.

•

Dashboarding & Reporting: Design and build automated dashboards (e.g., Tableau, Google Looker Studio, Mode) to provide self-serve visibility into AI and Cloud spend for key stakeholders.
