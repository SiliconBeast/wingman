Are you ready to explore the world of aerospace and defense? Do you want to learn from and collaborate with some of the greatest minds in the industry? At RTX, our internships, co-ops and full-time careers provide an exceptional foundation to work on complex problems, advance your skills and create a safer, more connected world. Discover opportunities to make a difference at RTX.

What You Will Do:

• Support requirements capture from aircraft Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEM)

• Decompose and allocate requirements to appropriate domains within Collins Aerospace

• Support the creation of test procedures to verify and validate requirements

• Support the creation and completion of certification artifacts

• Support customer questions and investigate issues related to software and hardware delivered to the OEM

• Support investigations of root causes of issues discovered during flight test and in-service aircraft operations

What You Will Learn:

• Systems Engineering development process
Interaction with internal and external customers with opportunity to talk to aircraft manufacturers engineers during design meetings

• Managing scope, schedule, and cost in real life work

• Agile Program Increment and Sprint Planning

• Preparation of certification packages

Qualifications You Must Have:

• Must be pursuing a Bachelor’s degree or higher in a Science, Technology, Engineering or Math (STEM) discipline and actively enrolled through the completion of the internship/co-op session.

• An educational background covering requirements capture & definition, integration, verification & validation testing.

Qualifications We Prefer:

• Basic experience working within an aerospace related engineering development environment

• Basic experience with DOORS, JIRA, JAMA, and data capture tools or any other tools that achieve the same functions.

• Experience or knowledge using data bus tools such as A429 and A664 readers for data analysis in design, prototyping, testing, and troubleshooting

• Basic experience with the AGILE framework for workflow planning and development

• Basic working knowledge of a software language such as Python, C++, is an advantage
