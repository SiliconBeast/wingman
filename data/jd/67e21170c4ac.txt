Are you ready to explore the world of aerospace and defense? Do you want to learn from and collaborate with some of the greatest minds in the industry? At RTX, our internships, co-ops and full-time careers provide an exceptional foundation to work on complex problems, advance your skills and create a safer, more connected world. Discover opportunities to make a difference at RTX.

What You Will Do:

• Design, develop, and test applications to simulate the behavior of avionics sensors to support the development and testing of Collins Avionics products.

• Aid the development team in the debugging of laboratory issues to determine root cause and propose potential solutions to the discovered problems.

• Effectively interacts and networks with teammates, management, and customers.

• Support the Program Lead(s) with budget and schedule data for our work on a given program to help ensure the successful completion of a Collins Avionics program.

• Work with a team of dedicated engineers to help shape the future of the avionics simulation platform.

What You Will Learn:

• As a member of the Common Simulation Environment team, you'll be an active member of the development effort on multiple Avionics programs. You'll be a member of a scrum team, continually looking to improve, that values the input of all members. You'll get hands on experience troubleshooting on the System Integration Laboratory, gaining real-world experience.

Qualifications You Must Have:

• Must be pursuing Bachelor’s or Advanced degree in Mechanical or Aerospace
Engineering and actively enrolled through the completion of the internship/co-op session.

• Requires a High School Diploma or equivalent.

• Must have a minimum of 60 hours of credit by the start of the internship / co-op session.

• Experience with C/C++

• Experience with Python scripting

Qualifications We Prefer:

• Experience with Graphical User Interface (GUI) design/implementation

• Experience with C# programming language

• Experience with MS Visual Studio

• Experience with MS SQL Server
