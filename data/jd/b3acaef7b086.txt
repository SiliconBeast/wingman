Interested in helping design and improve the systems that keep manufacturing facilities running efficiently? At Foth, our internship program provides hands-on experience that allows you to apply what you've learned in the classroom while developing valuable technical and professional skills. As a Process Engineering Intern in our Green Bay, WI office, you'll support process and utility engineering projects for a variety of industrial clients, working alongside experienced engineers. During this Summer 2027 internship, you'll build your understanding of engineering analysis, design, and project execution.


Primary Responsibilities:
• Assist with field data gathering under the direction of a process engineer

• Create and maintain clear and accurate design documents (P&IDs, equipment plans, piping drawings, photo drawings)

• Assist with CADD in the preparation of drawings for process and utility system related projects

• Understand, interpret, and develop design and construction specifications

• Perform standard process system calculations (pump sizing, material and energy balance, fluid flow analysis, valve and instrument sizing)

• Develop equipment lists

Required Qualifications: 
• Pursuing a Bachelor's Degree in Mechanical Engineering or Chemical Engineering from an ABET-accredited program

• GPA of 3.0 or greater

Preferred Qualifications: 
• Previous experience with and/or coursework involving P&IDs

• Previous experience with and/or coursework involving standard process calculations (pump sizing, valve and instrument sizing, fluid flow analysis)

• Previous experience with equipment specification and procurement support (obtaining quotes, vendor contact/follow-up)
