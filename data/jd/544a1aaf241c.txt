What you'll be doing

Join CIBC’s Technology Operations team as a Technical Systems Analyst Co-op, where you’ll gain valuable hands-on experience in application provisioning and system administration. As part of your co-op term, you’ll be trained on key processes and tools, and you’ll be responsible for handling provisioning requests and completing a set of assigned tasks. You’ll have opportunities to implement process improvements, collaborate on presentations, and develop documentation to support team initiatives. Be part of an innovation-focused team that creates easy, flexible, and personalized banking solutions to enhance client experience and change the way that people bank.

At CIBC we enable the work environment most optimal for you to thrive in your role. Details on your work arrangement (proportion of on-site and remote work) will be discussed at the time of your interview.

How you'll succeed

• Application Provisioning and Support – Complete provisioning requests for various applications and systems, ensuring accuracy and adherence to established procedures.

• Process Improvement – Identify opportunities to streamline workflows and propose innovative solutions to enhance team efficiency.

• Documentation and Artifacts – Develop and maintain clear documentation and presentation materials to support operational processes and knowledge sharing.

• Collaboration and Communication – Work closely with team members to achieve shared goals, participate in meetings, and contribute to group discussions.

• Technical Skill Development – Gain hands-on experience with tools such as ServiceNow, Active Directory, Microsoft Azure, Microsoft Exchange, and scripting languages, with guidance and training provided throughout your term.

Who you are

• You’re eager to learn. You have a passion for technology and are excited to develop new technical skills in a collaborative environment.

• You have relevant technical experience. You’re comfortable working with ServiceNow, Active Directory, Microsoft Azure, Microsoft Exchange, and scripting languages, or you’re keen to learn these tools.

• You’re detail-oriented. You approach tasks with care and accuracy, ensuring that all provisioning and documentation is completed to a high standard.

• You communicate effectively. You can clearly explain ideas and contribute to presentations and documentation for a variety of audiences.

• You’re pursuing a related education. You’re working toward a degree or diploma in computer science or a related field.

• Values matter to you. You bring your real self to work, and you live our values - trust, teamwork, and accountability.
