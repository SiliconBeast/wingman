Are you an experienced professional or veteran looking for a purposeful step back into the workforce after an extended career break? Or are you ready to return to full‑time work but need a flexible bridge to re‑launch your career?

TI’s Encore Program helps you put your skills to work, sharpen your capabilities, and relaunch your career.

About the Encore program

• Overview: A paid, 12‑week internship designed for experienced talent who have taken a career break of 2 years or more (over a continuous period)

• Your role: You’ll be a contributor, not a trainee, from day 1—solving real problems, inventing solutions, and collaborating with TI teams.

• Support: Personalized mentorship, coaching, and networking opportunities are built into the program.

• Outcome: High‑performing participants will be considered for full‑time employment at the end of the program.

Qualifications, skills and requirements

• A career break of at least 2 years

• Intent to return to a full‑time role at TI after the program.

• No sponsorship required now or in the future (U.S. work‑authorization required).

Change the world. Love your job.

The Product Operations Analyst is a highly analytical and strategically impactful role focused on driving business performance through data-driven decision making. In this role, you will translate inventory strategy into measurable revenue outcomes while optimizing operational efficiency across the business.

As an Operations Analyst, you will develop strong cross-functional expertise by collaborating with strategic business unit stakeholders, manufacturing partners, and sales leaders. This role requires critical thinking and proactive problem solving to deliver measurable business results while maintaining high levels of customer satisfaction and strong financial performance.

Key responsibilities and opportunities in this role include:

• Owning portfolio management responsibilities within high-growth markets, demonstrating strong business stewardship and accountability for performance.

• Developing systems expertise that can be applied across multiple business environments to improve operational visibility and decision making.

• Supporting product lifecycle management, contributing insights from early development through successful market introduction.

• Driving collaborative problem solving with cross-functional partners to deliver measurable business impact.

• Playing an active role in shaping and implementing new Operations processes, helping build the operational foundation that will support how we deliver parts and serve customers for years to come .

This role offers a unique opportunity to build strategic, analytical and operational leadership skills while making a meaningful contribution to the company’s continued growth.

Texas Instruments will not sponsor job applicants for visas or work authorization for this position.

About Texas Instruments

Texas Instruments Incorporated (Nasdaq: TXN) is a global semiconductor company that designs, manufactures and sells analog and embedded processing chips for markets such as industrial, automotive, data center, personal electronics and communications equipment. At our core, we have a passion to create a better world by making electronics more affordable through semiconductors. This passion is alive today as each generation of innovation builds upon the last to make our technology more reliable, more affordable and lower power, making it possible for semiconductors to go into electronics everywhere.
