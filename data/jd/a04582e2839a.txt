WHO WE’RE LOOKING FOR

Megazone Cloud is looking for a Software Engineer Co-op who is a "builder" at heart and eager to kickstart their career in a high-growth, global environment. You are passionate about deep-diving into software development, cloud architecture, and solving ambiguous, high-stakes technical problems.

As a Co-op, you won't just be watching from the sidelines. You will be a key part of our internal and client-facing teams, contributing to technical discovery, designing full-stack solutions, and collaborating with our senior experts to execute a clear vision. You are a fast learner with a thirst for knowledge and a desire to see your code make a real-world impact.


WHAT YOU’LL DO

 - Collaborate and Build: Work directly with senior engineers and our U.S. tech team to translate complex business requirements into functional software features and services.

 - Own the Full Stack: Design, develop, test, and deploy software solutions, gaining hands-on experience with Python, Java, JavaScript, or Go.

 - Be a Client-Facing Contributor: Participate in technical discussions and client meetings, effectively communicating progress on tickets, improvements, and deliverables.

 - Solve the Unsolvable: Assist in troubleshooting and resolving complex issues related to cloud infrastructure, applications, and services.

 - Drive Modern Practices: Help implement monitoring solutions and lean into DevOps best practices, including CI/CD and Infrastructure as Code.

 - Learn and Multiply: Actively participate in design reviews and knowledge-sharing sessions, leveraging our "force multiplier" culture to level up your skills and the team’s output.


WHAT YOU BRING (REQUIREMENTS)

 - Educational Foundation: Currently pursuing a degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related technical field.

 - Technical Curiosity: Proficiency in at least one programming language (e.g., Python, Java, JavaScript, Go) and a strong grasp of programming/scripting fundamentals.

 - Cloud Ambition: Exposure to or a deep interest in major cloud platforms (e.g., AWS, Azure) and modern data ecosystems.

 - DevOps Mindset: Foundational knowledge of DevOps practices, including version control, CI/CD pipelines, and automation.

 - Communication Excellence: Ability to translate technical concepts for different audiences and a desire to engage in a professional services/consulting environment.

 - Startup Mentality: A strategic "builder" mindset, a sense of ownership, and the agility to manage multiple workstreams in a fast-paced environment.


BONUS POINTS (NICE-TO-HAVES)

 - Previous internship or project experience in a Professional Services or consulting environment.

 - Experience with GenAI, MLOps, or specialized data tools (e.g., Databricks, Snowflake).
