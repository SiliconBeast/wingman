HPR is the leading provider of high-performance and ultra-low latency electronic trading and capital markets infrastructure solutions offered as a managed service. Our cutting-edge technology is used by tier-1 financial institutions to monitor and execute trades rapidly and efficiently. As we continue to innovate and grow, we’re searching for forward-thinking FPGA Interns to help us build the future of capital markets infrastructure.

As an FPGA Engineering Intern at HPR, you will:

• Build on your strong academic background as you help us advance our high-performance computing and electronic trading systems

• Collaborate with mentors to solve real-world problems

• Own a project through the entire FPGA development life cycle

• Develop tools to automate RTL and testbench generation

Education Requirements

• Pursuing a BS/MS in Computer Engineering , Electrical Engineering , Computer Science , or related field and have attained Sophomore standing or higher

• Successful completion or enrollment in relevant coursework such as Digital Logic Design, Computer Organization, or Embedded Systems

• Must have and maintain at least a 3.0 cumulative GPA , if on a 4.0 scale

Required Qualifications

• Internship or academic experience writing Verilog/SystemVerilog or VHDL

• Familiarity working in a Linux environment

• Proficiency with a scripting and/or high-level software language (e.g., Python, Perl, or similar)

• Excellent interpersonal, verbal, and written communication skills

Preferred Qualifications

• Experience working with FPGAs

• Familiarity with FPGA tools (Vivado and/or Quartus)
