Role Overview 
Create flexible framework dashboards that can accommodate multiple KPI elements. Incorporate new KPI's as needed and continuously seek to improve dashboards

Responsibilities
Support of Vehicle Engineering Dashboard project, including:

• Interviewing managers to understand what metrics they need to track

• Gap Analysis: Work with Zoox internal data team to identify existing dashboard platforms and data sources. Develop plans to collect data to track items not currently covered.

• Collaborate with IT: To establish mechanisms to push standard and custom dashboards to targeted people and to public screens

• Document process, findings and future plans
