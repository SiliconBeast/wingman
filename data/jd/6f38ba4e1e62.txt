Your Impact

As a Store Operations Corporate Facilities Intern, you'll gain valuable experience supporting the day-to-day operations that ensure Lowe’s stores remain safe, functional, and customer-ready. Working alongside our Retail Facilities and Store Operations teams, you’ll assist in coordinating maintenance requests, tracking vendor activity, and analyzing store-level data to help improve facilities support across our 1,700+ locations. This role is ideal for someone interested in operations, facilities coordination, and gaining insight into how Lowe’s keeps its stores running efficiently behind the scenes.

What You Will Learn

• Develop dashboards and reports that support internal business teams, with a focus on creating clear, actionable insights for non-technical users.

• Query and transform data from multiple sources, and gain experience building efficient ETL pipelines that prepare datasets for reporting and analysis.

• Explore automation tools to streamline repetitive reporting or data prep tasks, building small-scale solutions that reduce manual effort.

• Understand the operational areas supported and practice translating technical findings into recommendations that business stakeholders can act on.

• Gain introductory experience with advanced analytics techniques to see how they can add value beyond standard reporting.

• Serve as a connector between business and IT, including facilitation of requests, proposals of alternatives, and navigating organizational processes.

Minimum Qualifications

• Pursuing a Bachelor’s Degree in Data Analytics, Computer Science, Statistics or a related field.

• Expected graduation dates of December 2027 or May 2028.

Preferred Skills/Experience

• 3.0 cumulative GPA or higher.

• Proficiency with BI tools (PowerBI, Tableau, etc) and data analytics.

• Foundational knowledge of SQL and databases, and ability to write simple queries.

• Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with curiosity to explore data.

• Comfort working with data and learning internal platforms.

• Experience in a leadership position at work or within a campus organization is a plus.

About Lowe’s

Lowe’s Companies, Inc. (NYSE: LOW) is a FORTUNE® 100 home improvement company serving approximately 16 million customer transactions a week in the United States.
