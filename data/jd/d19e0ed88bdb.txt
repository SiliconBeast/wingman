Job Description:

Ignite your Career Journey with Pentair’s Leadership Development Internship Program!

Are you ready to gain hands-on experience, develop your leadership skills, and make a meaningful impact? Pentair is seeking ambitious, innovative, and driven students to join our Leadership Development Internship Program.

🚀 What Awaits You

Embark on a 12-week experience where you'll contribute to meaningful work, build lasting connections, and grow both personally and professionally. Through hands-on projects, mentorship, networking opportunities, and intern-focused programming, you'll gain valuable experience and develop skills that will help prepare you for the next step in your career.

🌎 Make a Difference

Each intern will be assigned to a real-world, strategic business project that you will be immersed in for the summer. Your work will directly help Pentair achieve our mission of a more sustainable future. Interns will present their project objectives, processes, and overall results and impact to leadership at the internship program's end.

🌐 Professional Cohort Development

As a Pentair intern, you'll join a cohort of talented students from across the organization and participate in meaningful development experiences throughout the summer. From mentorship and networking opportunities to executive speaker sessions, a team case competition, and our signature Intern Summit, you'll build valuable skills, expand your network, and gain exposure to potential career opportunities. Along the way, you'll collaborate with experienced professionals, contribute to meaningful work, and grow both personally and professionally.

💦 About Pentair

At Pentair, we believe the health of our world depends on reliable access to clean water. We design, manufacture, and deliver a comprehensive range of smart, sustainable water solutions to homes, business, and industries around the world. Whether it is improving, moving, or enjoying water, we help manage the world’s most precious resource.

👍 Pentair Will:

• Offer meaningful, hands-on work through a strategic business project that will allow you to make a real impact while developing valuable professional skills.

• Provide on-the-job training, mentorship, and coaching from experienced professionals invested in your growth and success.

• Pay competitively.

• Provide opportunities to connect with leaders across the organization through executive speaker sessions, coffee chats, networking events, and mentorship experiences.

• Offer a variety of professional development, social, volunteer, and cohort experiences designed to help you build your network and accelerate your growth.

• Give you the opportunity to collaborate with fellow interns through a team case competition and attend Pentair's Intern Summit, bringing together interns from across the company for learning, development, and connection.

• Provide the opportunity to showcase your accomplishments through a final presentation to Pentair leaders, with the potential to be considered for future full-time opportunities upon graduation.

📃 Minimum Qualifications:

• Be pursuing a Bachelor’s Degree in Business (Finance, Marketing, Business Administration, Economics, Supply Chain, Management), Bachelor’s Degree in Engineering, or related field from an accredited university

• Be enrolled as a junior undergraduate (Preferred Graduation Date: Spring 2028)

• Have a strong interest in pursuing Pentair’s full-time Product Management Leadership Development Program upon graduation

• Willing to relocate and work in locations throughout the United States while in Pentair's Leadership Development Program

• Be proficient in Microsoft Word, Excel, and PowerPoint

• Must be legally authorized to work in the United States without sponsorship now or in the future

• Session 2: Early June – Mid-August 2027
