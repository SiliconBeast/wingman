Role Overview:

As a Software Developer Intern on the Genesys Cloud Predictions Data & Decision team, you will work with a highly collaborative and diverse team of software engineers, data engineers, and operations research engineers to build intelligent solutions for our workforce management products.

Our team tackles complex, real-world problems involving forecasting, resource planning, optimization, and intelligent decision-making . You will have the opportunity to work across the full lifecycle of a problem—from understanding the problem and exploring data, to designing algorithms, building software, running experiments, and evaluating results.

We are especially interested in people who are intellectually curious, enjoy solving ambiguous problems, and are excited about the intersection of software engineering, data, optimization, and AI .

You don't need to be an expert in Operations Research or AI. We are looking for strong problem solvers who are excited to learn and apply new techniques to challenging problems.
