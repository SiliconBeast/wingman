What You Will Do:

You are the kind of engineering student who can’t help pulling things apart to see exactly how they work. You're probably involved in cybersecurity and coding clubs, understand the term “white hat” does not refer to "black-tie events", and you definitely have a pile of dismantled electronic devices in the corner dedicated to personal projects. If that sounds like you, welcome to Secure System Engineering; the intersection of Systems, Software, and Safety Engineering.

The Embedded Product Cybersecurity Engineering team is seeking a Secure System Engineer Intern for Summer 2027. Our shared services team supports product development across the company wherever a customer has a security requirement, or when a system will be exposed to cyber threats through connectivity or operational use over public networks. Members of our team help develop cyber-resilient systems by supporting design and implementation teams to ensure a Secure System Development Lifecycle (SSDLC). We also develop tools and testing techniques for integration and verification, helping identify residual defects in design or implementation that could negatively impact the confidentiality, availability, or integrity of a system.

This is an onsite position. Located in Cedar Rapids.

• Must be a U.S. Person/Permanent Resident

• Assist with developing and executing fuzzing and penetration tests to evaluate attack surface robustness.

• Develop threat models and data flow diagrams to ensure data is properly isolated in transit and at rest.

• Implement mandatory access controls (MAC) through the development of SELinux policies.

• Participate in code reviews to identify weaknesses in the implementation of security functions.

• Conduct vulnerability assessments on proposed and in-service systems.

• Develop and improve tools and scripts that enhance the overall efficacy of the Embedded Product Cybersecurity team.

What You Will Learn:

• Practical experience in security testing methodologies, emphasizing fuzzing techniques.
