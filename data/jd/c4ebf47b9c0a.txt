Project Description
This internship will be embedded within Xcel Energy’s Renewable Operations organization, supporting performance improvement initiatives across Solar, Energy Storage (BESS), and Wind assets spanning all three operating regions. The intern will contribute to identifying performance gaps, improving operational consistency, and supporting reliability initiatives across a diverse renewable fleet. The role is designed to provide hands-on experience through meaningful, business-impact work aligned with operational priorities and strategic objectives.

Team Description
The intern will work within a cross-functional Renewable Operations team that includes field operations, performance optimization, and engineering functions. This environment provides exposure to multiple disciplines required to support renewable generation, reflecting the collaborative and multi-functional structure used to manage solar, wind, and storage assets across the company. The intern will engage with subject matter experts across technologies and regions, gaining insight into fleet-wide operations and strategic initiatives. The role will support operations across all Xcel Energy renewable operating regions and may involve coordination with remote teams across Solar, Wind, and Energy Storage.

Typical intern responsibilities may include but are not limited to:

•
Analyze operational performance data across Solar, Storage, and Wind assets

•
Identify trends, performance gaps, and regional inconsistencies

•
Support development of standardized processes and best practices across sites

•
Assist with reporting, dashboards, and performance tracking tools

•
Collaborate with site teams, reliability engineering, and performance optimization groups

•
Provide recommendations to improve reliability, availability, and generation performance

This position is for a May 24, 2027 start date. This position may have the possibility to extend beyond the internship’s initial term based on the candidate’s successful performance and Xcel Energy’s business needs.
