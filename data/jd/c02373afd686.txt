At Oshkosh, we build, serve and protect people and communities around the world by designing and manufacturing some of the toughest specialty trucks and access equipment. We employ over 18,000 team members all united by a common purpose. Our engineering and product innovation help keep soldiers and firefighters safe, is critical in building and keeping communities clean and helps people do their jobs every day.

As an onsite year-round Treasury intern, you will support daily treasury management activities to include running reports, reconciling cash, inputting transactions, and benchmarking treasury activity.

For this year-round internship, you will be required to work on-site in Oshkosh, Wisconsin throughout the year.

YOUR IMPACT

• Assist in daily treasury operations.

• Perform daily reconciliation of banking activities.

• Perform reconciliation of cash applied.

• Assist with month end close process and journal entries.

• Assist with SARBOX and quarterly compliance requirements.

• Monitor and track key treasury operational benchmarks.

• Assist in the implementation of new treasury systems and procedures.

• Maintain Treasury Department files.

• Handle special assignments as assigned by supervisor.

• Plan and prioritize work activities.

• All other duties or projects as assigned.

MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS

• Student in a Sophomore standing or greater working toward a bachelor’s degree in Finance, Economics, Accounting or related field.

• Graduation date on or after May 2029.

• Available to work onsite year-round; 20 hours per week during school and full-time during breaks.

• Knowledge of Microsoft Word and Excel.

STANDOUT QUALIFICATIONS

• Treasury, Finance or Accounting experience.

• Excellent written and communication skills.

• Cumulative GPA of 3.0 or above.
