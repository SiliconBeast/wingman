Job Description

The Robotics Research Co-op is apart of the Robotics Centre of Excellence (RCoE). The RCoE is the "innovation engine" of Rockwell Automation robotics. We are a dynamic, research group dedicated pre-programmed automation and ushering in the new era of Autonomous Operations. Here, you'll work with world-class experts in a culture that prizes rapid prototyping and the desire to see "impossible" ideas working on the factory floor.

We are looking for a passionate Robotics Research Intern who is at the intersection of AI theory and hands-on implementation. You will work with senior researchers, gaining invaluable hands-on experience with modern embodied intelligence. Your work will centre on building data tooling and pipelines. These pipelines move, curate, and integrate data into our platform. They directly help develop perception and world models. We achieve this support through the use of machine learning frameworks.

*Relocation assistance is not available or provided for this role.

Your Responsibilities:

•
Build data tooling and pipelines to integrate robot and perception data into our platform. These pipelines support dataset curation and ensure model evaluation workflows, including dataset slicing and active learning loops for continuous improvement.

•
Collect robot and perception datasets using LiDAR, cameras, and other sensors across a range of scenarios, including both structured experiments and real-world deployments.

•
Organise perception datasets, contributing to the creation of high-quality datasets for model training and evaluation.

•
Write clean, scalable, and code applying modern system programming languages and following software engineering best practices.

•
Document your best practices and research through technical reports, white papers, and publications.
