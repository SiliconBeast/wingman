Job Details

Position Summary:

We are looking for passionate and engaged Information Security Interns to join our team for the Summer of 2027! This internship provides an exciting opportunity to gain hands-on experience with real-world security practices, tools, and techniques. Collaborate with seasoned professionals and make a real impact by contributing to strategic projects that are pivotal to our organization's success. Embark on a journey to enhance your skills, expand your knowledge, and help safeguard our digital landscape!

Key Responsibilities:

•
Contribute to strategic security projects and tasks to gain practical experience in the security landscape

•
Partner closely with seasoned professionals on assigned projects

•
Collaborate with team members to meet project goals and deadlines

•
Perform other security-related tasks as needed to support the team

•
Pro-active vs. Re-active mindset to engage in Information Security developmental workshops and intern-led activities

•
Contribute to the creation and maintenance of documents such as spreadsheets that track, measure, and facilitate improvement of internal processes.

•
Review and assist in the implementation of security policy, standards, procedures, and guidelines.

•
Assist with ad hoc projects and initiatives that require analysis and keen desire to learn various Information Security tools.

•
Support on special projects and other tasks/duties as needed

Qualifications:

•
Currently pursuing a degree in Cybersecurity, Information Technology, Computer Science, or a related field

•
Interest and desire to learn about information security practices and technologies

•
Basic understanding of information security tools and technologies (e.g., SIEM, firewalls, GRC platforms, etc.)

•
Ability to work in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment.

•
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills

•
Excellent written & verbal communication skillsets

•
Ability to work well both independently as well as within a team oriented environment

•
Attention to detail and ability to work with sensitive information

•
Eagerness to learn and apply cybersecurity principles in a professional environment

•
Microsoft Operating System concepts and system administration knowledge

•
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

•
Ability to work on-site at either our corporate headquarters in Conshohocken, PA or in Carrollton, TX
