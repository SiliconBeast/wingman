Your Team, Your Impact

Marvell Product Security team is driving Security Development Lifecycle (SDL) process and exploring best known methods/tools for HW and SW security implementation & testing, as part of SDL charter. They are evaluating tools from expert suppliers in the industry and investigating scope for using AI to facilitate existing capabilities. The team works with Business Units to evaluate effectivity of the tools while testing security implementation of the product. By learning architecture/design and conventional verification methodologies of the product, the team also proposes how it can be evolved to catch security vulnerabilities earlier in the product development life cycle.

What You Can Expect

• Work with team to evaluate specialized security verification tools from external suppliers.

• Work with team to explore scope for using AI assistance for facilitating security verification.

• Support Business Units to conduct product security testing on selected products at RTL design phase using specialized methods/tools.

• Learn architecture/design and functional testing methodologies of the product and provide input on evolving existing strategy for expanding security specific coverage.

What We're Looking For

• Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s Degree (BS) in CS/EE or related technical field(s) with an expected graduation date between Fall 2027 – Summer 2028.

• Strong understanding of basic principles for product security assurance.

• Strong understanding of validation/problem solving principles with experience in testing and debug in pre-silicon environment.

• Good Verilog, System Verilog programming skills to understand RTL design.

• Good scripting skills in Unix environment.

• Good understanding of system architecture with general understanding of security requirements.

• Good prompting skills with general experience in using Artificial Intelligence (AI).

• Good communication skills in English (written and verbal) with great collaboration skills.
