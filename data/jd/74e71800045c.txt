The Opportunity

Our internship program lets you showcase your skills, while gaining real-life experience that’ll help you jump-start your career. Throughout the internship, you'll participate in professional development opportunities, networking events, mentorship programs, and team-based projects designed to help prepare you for a successful career in technology.

Our interns deliver innovative solutions to real business challenges, while building enduring relationships in a collaborative culture with their manager and an assigned mentor.

This position is based in San Antonio, TX.

What you'll do:

As a Infrastructure Engineer Intern you will have the opportunity to explore how large-scale technology environments are monitored, maintained, secured, and optimized to deliver reliable experiences for our members and employees. You'll contribute to meaningful projects that enhance operational efficiency, strengthen platform reliability, and support modernization efforts across the organization. Under direct supervision and with guidance from senior engineers and your mentor, your day will focus on supporting infrastructure operations. You'll attend team meetings and assist in analyzing potential threats or vulnerabilities across systems. You will learn about designing scalable infrastructure, implementing system changes, and automating service delivery and maintenance tasks. You may assist in building monitoring and tooling activities, and help troubleshoot production issues or system problems. You'll learn how to analyze outages to identify opportunities for improvement and experiment with new patterns and technologies. Your work will support USAA's technology strategy and may involve leveraging AI tools and automation.

Primary Responsibilities:

• Learn how to design scalable infrastructure and implement system changes.

• Assist in automating service delivery and maintenance tasks.

• Help build monitoring and tooling activities for infrastructure systems.

• Learn to independently resolve production issues through system problem-solving under guidance.

• Start to analyze outages to identify opportunities for improvement.

• Experiment with new infrastructure patterns and technologies.

• Contribute to projects aligned with USAA's Technology Strategy.

• Assist in developing and implementing innovative IT solutions, potentially using AI tools.

• Create and maintain documentation related to technical solutions.

• Gain exposure to risk and compliance procedures within IT operations.
