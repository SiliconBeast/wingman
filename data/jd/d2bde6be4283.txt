Responsibilities Join a team at the forefront of semiconductor innovation, enabling the breakthrough technologies that differentiate Keysight's industry-leading design enablement solutions. As a Characterization Intern you will work hands-on with some of the world's most advanced compound semiconductor ICs that power next-generation RF, microwave, millimeter-wave, and high-speed digital products. In this role, you will play a critical part in evaluating cutting-edge technologies to the meet the demanding performance, quality, and lifetime expectations of Keysight customers. You will collaborate closely with device engineers to evaluate, qualify, and continuously improve the technologies that form the foundation of Keysight's product leadership. If you are excited by advanced semiconductor technology, data-driven problem solving, and the challenge of ensuring reliability in products operating at the leading edge of performance, this internship offers an opportunity to make a visible and lasting impact. What You'll Do Collaborate with device engineers in the electrical characterization and analysis of advanced semiconductor devices used in high-performance RF, microwave, and millimeter-wave applications. Perform transistor and diode measurements using laboratory test equipment and established characterization procedures. Apply modern software and AI-assisted coding tools to generate compact models and accelerate test automation, data processing, and engineering productivity. Assist with collecting, organizing, and analyzing large electrical measurement datasets to support technology development activities. Document measurement procedures, summarize findings, and communicate results in a clear and organized manner. Support continuous improvement efforts related to device characterization methodologies, test infrastructure, and data analysis processes. Qualifications Qualifications & What Will Help You Succeed We're looking for a highly motivated student who is excited about semiconductor technology and interested in gaining hands-on experience with advanced device characterization and analysis. This role is ideal for students pursuing careers in semiconductor devices, microelectronics, electrical engineering, applied physics, or related technical fields. Successful candidates will enjoy combining laboratory work, data analysis, and software tools to better understand semiconductor device behavior. Coursework in semiconductor devices or semiconductor physics. Familiarity with modern AI tools and an interest in applying them to automation, testing, or engineering workflows. Familiarity with laboratory instrumentation such as source-measure units (SMUs), parameter analyzers, oscilloscopes, network analyzers, probe stations, or related equipment.
