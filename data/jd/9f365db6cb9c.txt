Are you ready to explore the world of aerospace and defense? Do you want to learn from and collaborate with some of the greatest minds in the industry? At RTX, our internships, co-ops and full-time careers provide an exceptional foundation to work on complex problems, advance your skills and create a safer, more connected world. Discover opportunities to make a difference at RTX.

What You Will Do

• Provide technical interpretation of drawings and specifications.

• Analyze and resolve design tolerance issues.

• Evaluate, select, and order capital test equipment, including performing cost/payback analysis.

• Support capacity planning and utilization analysis to ensure proper staffing and test equipment needs.

• Identify and address routine test defects and improve test yields.

• Perform root cause analysis and implement corrective actions to resolve test failures.

What You Will Learn

• Gain hands-on experience in manufacturing electrical engineering, supporting high-frequency communication radios and circuit board manufacturing processes.

• Learn to use engineering tools such as:

• SAP, engineering drawings, change control boards, SolidWorks, AutoCAD, eView, and PDM.

• Apply standard work tools and processes, including CORE certification.

• Develop essential communication, collaboration, and leadership skills.

• Take advantage of growth opportunities within the role and develop expertise for future career paths.

• Participate in on-the-job training and professional development programs.

Qualifications You Must Have

• Actively pursuing a Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Science Engineering, or a related STEM major through the completion of the internship/co-op session.

• Background in Root Cause Analysis.

• Relevant experience gained through on-the-job training, research, coursework, or projects.

Qualifications We Prefer

• Prior experience in manufacturing environments.

• Prior Experience in RF Technology

• Demonstrated interest or experience in electronics.

• Proven ability to drive continuous improvement in manufacturing operations.

• Prior RTX internship, co-op, or work experience
