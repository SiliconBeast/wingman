Job Description:

• Product application engineering intern for the mixed signal IC product line.

• Support customer design-in activities including answering customer technical questions, schematic & layout reviews, and customer board debug.

• Develop technical collateral including product datasheets, reference designs, user guides, application notes, etc.

• Perform bench characterization for new products.

• Create go-to-market technical collateral including evaluation boards, reference design schematics, application notes and design tools for new products.

• Work cross-functionally with product marketing, product management, and chip design teams in supporting customers

• Support product marketing and management to collect product and application requirements in the related area, by communicating with customers and internal teams.

Requirements/Qualifications:

• Pursuing a BS in Electrical Engineering, Electronics Technology, or Electrical and Computer Engineering.

• Prefer academic experience in the field of power electronics.

• Familiar with discrete semiconductor characteristics and operations. (MOSFET, BJT, diodes).

• Lab experience with using digital oscilloscopes, function generators, curve tracers, and network analyzers for testing, debugging, and characterizing electronic components.

• Experience in using schematic capture, circuit simulation, and PCB layout tools.

• Effectively multi-tasking, managing priorities, solving technical problems independently and completing project deliverables on time.

• Well-organized, self-driven, good written and verbal communication skills, and work effectively in a team environment.
