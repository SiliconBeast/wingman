The Gas & Electric Design Contracting team is seeking a motivated intern to support data analysis, reporting, process improvement initiatives, and operational activities across our design organization. This internship provides an opportunity to work with leaders and subject matter experts responsible for gas and electric design, contracting, governance, compliance, and business process improvement.

The intern will assist with analyzing business performance metrics, identifying trends within operational data, developing dashboards and reports, documenting processes, and supporting continuous improvement efforts. The role will provide exposure to utility operations, project management, engineering design processes, contractor management, and business analytics while offering hands-on experience with real-world projects that directly support business goals.

The position will be based in Minneapolis, Minnesota, with opportunities to collaborate across multiple design offices and operational teams throughout the service territory.

T ypical intern responsibilities may include but are not limited to:

•
Analyze operational and project data to identify trends, risks, and improvement opportunities

•
Develop and maintain dashboards, reports, and performance metrics using Excel, Power BI, and other reporting tools

•
Support continuous improvement initiatives through process mapping, documentation, and workflow analysis

•
Assist with data validation, quality reviews, and reporting related to design, contracting, and project delivery activities

•
Create presentations and recommendations for leadership based on data findings and business insights

•
Collaborate with cross-functional teams including Design, Project Management, Engineering, Operations, and Business Support

This position is for a May 24, 2027 start date. This position may have the possibility to extend beyond the internship’s initial term based on the candidate’s successful performance and Xcel Energy’s business needs.
