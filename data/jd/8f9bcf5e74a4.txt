Solidigm is seeking talented and motivated undergraduate students for our Software Development & Firmware Engineering Internships in the United States. This evergreen posting supports multiple internship opportunities across software development, firmware engineering, back-end engineering, AI-assisted programming, and data analysis for Solidigm's data center SSD products.

As an intern, you will work on real engineering challenges supporting software tools, firmware features, and data-driven workflows that improve engineering productivity and product development.

This internship is ideal for undergraduate students who enjoy solving complex technical problems, writing clean and efficient code, working close to hardware, and applying software, firmware, and data analysis skills to real-world engineering systems.

Key Responsibilities

Interns may be assigned one or more of the following responsibilities based on project needs, team priorities, and individual strengths:

• Design, develop, test, and maintain software tools, applications, or back-end systems that support engineering workflows and product development.

• Contribute to firmware features, tools, test environments, or debug workflows for SSD and storage-related technologies.

• Apply Python, C/C++, or other programming languages, including AI-assisted programming tools, to build scripts, services, or automation that improve engineering efficiency.

• Support data analysis efforts, including analyzing engineering data, logs, or performance metrics to identify trends and improvement opportunities.

• Troubleshoot and debug software or firmware issues; reproduce problems and assist with root-cause analysis.

• Work in Linux-based development environments to build, test, and deploy software or firmware.

• Work closely with cross-functional teams including software, firmware, data science, and product engineering.

• Create clear documentation for tools, code, workflows, and project deliverables.

Minimum Qualifications

• Currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering, Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Data Science, or a related technical discipline.

• Experience with Python, C, C++, or another programming language.

• Familiarity with Linux-based development environments.

• Strong analytical, debugging, and problem-solving skills.

• Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a technical team environment.

• Strong communication skills and eagerness to learn.

• This role is not eligible for candidates requiring VISA sponsorship.

Preferred Qualifications

• Experience with firmware development, embedded software, device drivers, or low-level systems programming.

• Familiarity with SSDs, NAND flash, NVMe, PCIe, or storage systems.

• Experience with back-end development, APIs, or service-oriented architecture.

• Exposure to AI-assisted programming tools, AI/ML concepts, or applied machine learning workflows.

• Experience with data analysis, scripting, or automation.

• Familiarity with version control systems such as Git.

• Strong interest in data center storage, software engineering, firmware, or AI-enabled systems.

What You'll Gain

As a Solidigm intern, you will have the opportunity to:

• Work on meaningful projects tied to real engineering and business needs.

• Gain hands-on experience with software development, back-end systems, AI-assisted programming, and data analysis.

• Collaborate with experienced engineers and cross-functional teams.

• Develop practical skills in coding, debugging, and technical communication.

• Present your work, receive feedback, and contribute to solutions that may influence future products.

• Grow your professional network through mentorship, team engagement, and intern program activities.

Additional Information

Work locations may include Rancho Cordova, CA; Sacramento, CA; Longmont, CO; or other U.S. Solidigm locations based on business need and internship assignment.

Internship availability, location and project scope may vary by team, degree level, and business requirements.

All your information will be kept confidential according to EEO guidelines.
