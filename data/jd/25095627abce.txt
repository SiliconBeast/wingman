About Vercel:

Vercel is the agentic infrastructure company, freeing people and agents to ship what's next. For more than a decade we've helped builders move from idea to production with speed, security, and exceptional developer experience.

Now we're scaling our products for both agents and people to ship and run software, built in the open and trusted by OpenAI, PayPal, Ramp, Supreme, and millions of developers worldwide.

About the Role:

As an Engineering Intern at Vercel, you will be an important part of our engineering team, working on real projects that will impact our products and customers. You will be mentored by experienced engineers and will be given opportunities to grow and learn.

What You Will Do:

Work will vary by team - but your general scope will include, but not be limited to:

• Own an end-to-end project: scope it with your manager, build it, ship it, and see it in customers' hands.

• Write and review production code alongside the engineers who maintain it.

• Debug real systems under real conditions - trace an issue through the stack and fix the root cause.

• Communicate your progress clearly, including when you're blocked.

About You:

• Currently pursuing a degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, graduating December 2027 or May 2028.

• Strong knowledge and capability with JavaScript and/or TypeScript. Experience with Go or Rust is a bonus.

• You dig into problems rather than around them - you want to know why something broke.

• You move work forward on your own. You surface blockers early instead of waiting to be asked.

• You communicate well in writing. Much of how we work is asynchronous and written down.

• Fluent with AI tooling and clear-eyed about it. You use it to move faster, and you can still explain, defend, and debug everything you ship.

• Based in San Francisco, willing and able to commute Mon/Tues/Fri.
