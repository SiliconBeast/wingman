The Experience

As a Finance Intern at Salesforce, you'll be embedded with one of the Finance functions — Controllership, Finance and Strategy, Treasury, Compliance, Procurement, Internal Audit, or Data and AI. You'll work directly with global teams on financial statement review and analytics, compliance audits, accounting, operational process improvement, and AI implementation, helping drive data-driven insights and Agentic innovation for the Finance organization.

What You'll Actually Be Doing

• Build AI automations for finance processes and tasks, and demonstrate how these capabilities drive measurable impact for the team

• Design analytical models (descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, or prescriptive) to support financial decision-making

• Design, build, or maintain dashboards in business intelligence software (e.g., Tableau, Power BI) to automate financial reporting and track key performance indicators (KPIs)

• Communicate data-driven insights through visualizations and storytelling to stakeholders across Finance
