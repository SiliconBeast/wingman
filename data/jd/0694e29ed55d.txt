NiSource Internship Program Introduction

NiSource is currently seeking a Summer People Analytics Intern for its Human Resources Organization. Successful applicants will become integral members of the department while learning within a team environment, completing meaningful tasks and collaborating with team members and leaders on important projects. This position will be a great opportunity for the candidate desiring to launch their career in this field. Successful applicants will build professional relationships with other interns and all levels of NiSource personnel while participating in rewarding professional development exercises and fun, team-building activities. Interns will obtain a broad overview of NiSource's operations and current events in the utilities industry.

This is a 12-week summer internship program starting in May 2027, for students entering their junior or senior year in fall 2027. The role is based in Columbus, OH. Candidates must reside within 50 miles of the reporting location to be eligible.

Essential Functions

• Successful applicants will become integral members of the Human Resources department, while learning within a team environment, completing meaningful tasks and collaborating with managers on important projects. This position will be a great opportunity for the candidate desiring to launch their career in Human Resources.

• The intern will have the opportunity to collaborate with our Organizational Development, Talent Acquisition, Talent Strategy, Leadership Development, Change Management, Human Resource Consultants and Inclusion and Diversity teams. Successful applicants will build professional relationships with other interns and all levels of NiSource personnel while participating in rewarding professional development exercises and fun, team-building activities. Interns will obtain a broad overview of NiSource's operations and current events in the utilities industry.
