What You Will Do

As an Optical Engineering Intern at Collins Aerospace Optronics, you will have the opportunity to work with Mechanical, Optical, Systems and Electrical Engineers on the design, analysis, development, and testing of complex optical and visual display systems. At Collins Aerospace Optronics, we develop and build unique optical systems for space, simulator, airborne and body-worn environments.

• Apply knowledge of the characteristics and behaviors of light to help design high-precision optical and visual display systems.

• Aid in the design of optical components, and help determine methods of optical system performance testing

• Prepare technical documentation including optical models, drawings, procedures and methods

• Support project lifecycle from design through testing

• Support trade studies and contribute to continuous process improvement activities

What You Will Learn

• Gain knowledge in design, analysis and tolerancing of complex optical assemblies.

• Hands on experience of assembly and testing of precision optical, and visual display systems

• Increase your understanding of design for manufacturability

Qualifications You Must Have

• Must be pursuing a Bachelor’s or Advanced degree in Optical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, or Aerospace Engineering and actively enrolled through the completion of the internship/co-op session.

Experience may be obtained through on-the-job experience, research, coursework, or projects.

Qualifications We Prefer

• Experience using optical design tools such as Zemax, or Code V

• Experience with Microsoft Office (Excel, Word, PowerPoint, and/or Outlook)

• Coursework or project experience involving optical system setup, alignment, or characterization (lasers, telescopes, visual display systems, and/or spectrometers)

• Experience with optical metrology equipment such as interferometers, profilometers, MTF test equipment

• Experience with basic optical engineering principles, including optical alignment methods, techniques, and tools used for setting up and verifying optical systems.
