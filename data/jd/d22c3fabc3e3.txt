Job Description

As a Software Engineering Intern, you will work alongside experienced software engineers to design, develop, test, deploy, and support applications that drive Medline's Warehouse Management Systems and E-Commerce platforms. This role provides exposure to both front-end and back-end development, allowing interns to contribute across the software development lifecycle while collaborating in an agile, team-oriented environment.

Examples of Projects and Responsibilities

• Develop and implement new features for business and e-commerce applications.

• Design, code, test, deploy, and support software solutions.

• Analyze, debug, and resolve software defects and production issues.

• Participate in code reviews and follow established coding standards and best practices.

• Develop and execute unit tests and support automated testing efforts.

• Collaborate with team members to improve existing applications and software development processes.

• Write technical documentation to support software maintenance and usability.

• Analyze business requirements and contribute to solution design discussions.

• Work closely with fellow interns and Medline team members in a collaborative, professional environment.

Qualifications

• Rising senior or junior pursuing a bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, or a related technical field.

• Minimum 3.0 GPA preferred.

• Experience with Java and/or JavaScript through coursework, projects, or internships.

• Understanding of object-oriented programming principles and software design concepts.

• Exposure to modern front-end technologies such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Angular, or Next.js.

• Familiarity with databases and SQL.

• Experience with Git or other version control systems.

• Knowledge of frameworks and tools such as Spring, Hibernate, Maven, Selenium, Jasmine, or Karma is a plus.

• Exposure to cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud is a plus.

• Strong analytical, problem-solving, organizational, and communication skills.

• Ability to work effectively in a collaborative team environment and adapt quickly to new technologies.

• Unrestricted permanent U.S. work authorization.
