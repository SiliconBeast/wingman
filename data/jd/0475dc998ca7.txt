Job Details

At Cencora, we are united in our responsibility to create healthier futures. As a Summer 2027 Intern, you will have the opportunity to apply your academic knowledge in a professional business environment while gaining meaningful, hands-on experience within our IT team.

Throughout the internship, you will work on real business initiatives, collaborate with team members and leaders, develop professional skills, and contribute to projects that support Cencora's purpose of creating healthier futures.

Start Date: June 7, 2027

End Date: August 13, 2027

Location: Carrolton, Dallas

This internship is designed for students who are curious, motivated, and eager to learn while making a meaningful impact in a fast-paced, collaborative environment.

The intern will gain exposure to regulated quality operations and work cross-functionally with Quality and business stakeholders to improve documentation practices, reporting, and process consistency.

Responsibilities

• Assist with document control activities for policies, SOPs, work instructions, forms, and related quality records

• Support document review for consistency, completeness, formatting, and version control

• Help maintain document trackers, status reports, and audit-ready files

• Assist with preparation for client audits, including organizing documentation and tracking action items

• Support tracking and trending of audit findings, deviations, and related quality metrics

• Contribute to continuous improvement initiatives related to quality systems and compliance processes

• Assist with eQMS implementation readiness activities, such as document preparation, workflow mapping, training support materials, and data organization

• Prepare summaries, presentations, and reports for the Quality team as needed

• Perform other related duties or special projects as assigned

• Support ongoing projects, reporting, analysis, or process improvement initiatives.

• Collaborate with cross-functional teams and business partners.

• Participate in team meetings, professional development opportunities, and internship program activities.

• Present project outcomes, recommendations, or key learnings at the conclusion of the internship.

Education and Experience

• Current student pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in quality, engineering, business, healthcare, regulatory affairs, or a related discipline

• Rising sophomore, junior, or senior at the time of the internship preferred

• Must not require sponsorship to work in the United States now or in the future.

Skills and Knowledge

• Strong organizational skills and attention to detail

• Interest in quality systems, compliance, auditing, or regulated operations

• Ability to manage multiple assignments and work independently in a professional environment

• Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a team environment

• Excellent verbal and written communication skills.

• Highly motivated self-starter with a strong desire to learn.

• Strong interpersonal, teamwork, and relationship-building skills.

• Demonstrated leadership through academic, extracurricular, volunteer, or work experiences.

• Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.

• Effective organizational and time-management skills.

• Ability to receive feedback and adapt in a dynamic work environment.

• Proficiency with Microsoft Office applications, including Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, and Teams.
