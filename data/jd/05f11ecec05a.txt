• Provide technical expertise and support to colleagues, assisting with the resolution of lab- and equipment-related issues and implementing corrective actions as necessary

• Develop, refine, and document photonic designs and experimental setups

• Analyze and present results to teammates and across the company

• Programming experience with languages such as Python, C/C++ or similar
