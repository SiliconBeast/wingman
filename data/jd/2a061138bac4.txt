Quantitative Trading Internship – Summer 2027

Location:  Chicago, IL |  Duration:  10 Weeks |  Start Date:  June 2027

Launch Your Career in Quantitative Trading with Geneva Trading

Are you a problem-solver with a passion for markets, data, and technology? Geneva Trading is seeking exceptional students to join our  Summer 2027 Quantitative Trading Internship Program .
