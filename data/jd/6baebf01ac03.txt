What You'll Be Doing:

Dev interns will work on a dynamic AI-driven solution development in support of a client. This is an opportunity to contribute to improving outcomes for our client and to observe and participate in many aspects of solution development. This effort will be highly technical and use modern and leading AI and agentic tools.

• Participate in the development of solution(s) to solve real business problems.

• Engage with business users to understand problems and needs, and collaborate with them on prototyping and iteration

• Create all necessary documentation to support the technical, business, and user success of the delivery of the solution

• Communicate actively with team members, colleagues, project members, and others regarding the status of planning, work, challenges or difficulties, learning opportunities, and lessons learned.
