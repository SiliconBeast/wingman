What you'll be doing:

•
Drive physical design, power, and timing closure for high-frequency and low-power designs.

•
Help drive frontend and backend implementation in select areas, from RTL to GDSII.

•
Run physical design experiments and help develop flows and methodologies.

What we need to see:

•
Pursuing an MS or Ph.D. in Electrical or Computer Engineering.

•
Familiarity with applying AI/ML techniques to physical design workflows to accelerate design, analysis, or automation tasks.

•
Knowledge of or experience with Static Timing Analysis (STA), power analysis, and power reduction.

•
Some experience in logic synthesis and equivalence checking.

•
Experience with physical optimization, e.g., placement, routing, clock tree, etc.

•
Some background in industry-standard EDA tools (e.g., Cadence, Synopsys, Siemens, etc.).

•
Proficiency in programming and scripting languages such as Perl, Tcl, Make, Python, etc.

Ways to stand out from the crowd:

•
Hands-on experience applying AI/ML models or LLM-based tools to solve EDA, design automation, or physical design problems.

•
Previous work experience in physical design.

•
Experience in methodology and/or flow development/automation.
