Responsibilities Job Description Looking for a challenging position in software development? Come join Keysight for a summer internship in Colorado Springs and help us build the AI tooling our own engineers use to design and ship measurement software. You'll learn how to apply your engineering coursework and knowledge in software development to solve real-world problems. About the Job You will be part of an Agile software development team and will work on the design, implementation, and testing of internal tooling that helps Keysight R&D engineers develop software faster and better with AI. You will be assigned a self-contained project at the start of your internship, chosen from the team's active work so that it delivers something the team keeps using after you leave. Projects span the range of what this side of the team builds: MCP servers and agent workflows for engineering tasks, AI assistance integrated into our IDEs and CI/CD pipelines, prompt and context management for internal tools, evaluation of AI-assisted development output, and the measurement that tells us whether any of it made the team faster. This internship is a deliberate pipeline into our full-time R&D Engineer openings. About the Team We are not a research group: everything we build is aimed at getting used. Your users are the engineers sitting around you and on neighboring teams, which makes this a different kind of software job than most internships - you will spend real time finding out what people actually need, and then getting them to adopt what you built. The team has a mix of new and experienced developers with a broad range of skills, and is eager to help you succeed. You will be paired with a mentor on the team and will report to the engineering manager, with regular guidance from the Solutions Architect.
