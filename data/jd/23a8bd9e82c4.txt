ABOUT THE ROLE & TEAM

Putting an autonomous 40-ton excavator on a live jobsite means being able to say, precisely, what we know about its safety and what that knowledge rests on. Today that argument lives in hazard analyses, requirements, tests and logs, linked by hand.

As a Safety Engineering intern, you’ll explore how formal methods and LLM-assisted proving can strengthen that argument. Working at the intersection of formal methods, statistics, and field robotics, you’ll formalize a narrowly scoped safety claim, verify a checker in Lean, and evaluate it on real fleet data, with explicit assumptions and auditable results.


WHAT YOU'LL DO

 - Become fluent in Bedrock's safety case and trace safety claims through the hazards, requirements, mitigations, and evidence that support them

 - Build an end-to-end demonstration for one claim: define the property and assumptions, implement a checker in Lean, prove its correctness against the specification, and run it on fleet data

 - Develop checks for assumptions that can be monitored in fleet data, and document those that require other evidence

 - Build audit tooling for LLM-assisted proving: record proof dependencies, document toolchain trust assumptions, and reject proof placeholders or unapproved axioms in automated builds

 - Work with Safety, Systems, and Autonomy teams to review formal specifications and turn findings into safety case improvements

 - Document the results, limitations, and recommendations for adoption in our review process


WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR
