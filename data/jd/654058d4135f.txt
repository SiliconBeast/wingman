The Opportunity

Marvell Central Engineering, FW team. The Central Engineering AMS-IP team provides leading-edge SerDes PHY solutions and other Analog Mixed-Signal IPs to support all Marvell products.
Our innovative approaches have resulted in the company’s products being first to market in many key areas, including optoelectronics and DSP-based transceivers for high-speed data transport. We provide the most advanced chip and subsystem solutions to address today’s and future’s multi-100Gig interconnect requirements to address the ever-increasing demand of higher data rates in numerous data center and AI-based applications. We are seeking talented individuals to work on solving technical challenges with the most outstanding group of collaborators in the industry. Join our team of world-class experts and make a difference in an exciting new career opportunity

What You Can Expect

• FW development of Marvell Serdes IP

• Be responsible for the development and verification of new firmware modules

• Work on developing CI/CD process

• Deliver module documents, module design specs, firmware source code, and build scripts

• Perform tasks including debugging, bring-up, and production firmware development

• Collaborate with firmware, design, application, and SoC team
