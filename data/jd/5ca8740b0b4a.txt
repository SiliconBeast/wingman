Position Overview:

This position reports to an AI Development Team Lead and will collaborate with software developers, business teams, and technical stakeholders across the organization. This position is responsible for assisting with the research, development, testing, and implementation of AI-driven solutions that support NISC's software development lifecycle and business operations. Interns will gain hands-on experience exploring emerging AI technologies, analyzing business processes, prototyping innovative solutions, and evaluating opportunities to improve efficiency through intelligent automation.

Based on intern interest and business needs, opportunities may exist to contribute to projects involving generative AI, machine learning, natural language processing, intelligent automation, software development, and data analysis. Adaptability, curiosity, and a passion for learning new technologies are essential.
