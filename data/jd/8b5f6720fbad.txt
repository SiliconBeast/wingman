Job Responsibilities:

This opportunity is from Spring-Summer (January - August) Monday-Friday Full time

Mission of the Position:

Works within the systems engineering team with the Advanced Systems Engineering team and the Noise, Vibration and Harmonics team, assisting with projects on all different levels throughout the co-op program. Tasks include supporting mechanical/electrical/controls design, industry research, prototype acquisition/assembly, and testing. Position is expected to include hands on learning experience in the lab.

Team Summary:

The Systems Engineering Team is responsible for providing engineering solutions in powertrain systems. This group is responsible for the design, development and realization of innovative, robust and leading-edge solutions for high volume powertrain applications.

Key Responsibilities:

• Help create new and advanced powertrain and driveline technology through supporting lead engineers in their projects.

• Work with other design and testing departments, guiding and collaborating with functional organization for technology/product concept development.

• Maintains working knowledge of safety policies and regulations to ensure duties of self and others are performed in a safe manner.

• Additional duties and responsibilities as assigned.

• Supports Systems Engineering by assessing and analyzing NVH performance of MPT driveline products.

• Assist with the use of NVH instrumentation, hardware and software.

• Work with the team to improve NVH development methods and the integration into existing/emerging engineering development processes.

Expected Learning Outcomes:

• Exposure to systems engineering executed at a high level on powertrain systems

• Test setup and execution

• Problem-solving skills and experience working with a team

• Fundamental understanding of powertrain products noise and vibration performance (traditional and electrified).

• Practical experience in setting up, measuring, and analyzing data in noise and vibrations tests.

• Understanding of how product noise and vibration performance affects vehicle quality.

• Experience reporting technical data in written reports, presentations, electronic media and talking in front of an engineering audience.

Requirements and Qualifications:

• Knowledge in computer-based software, including 3D CAD, strong organizational/communication skills, ability to work with others in a team environment

• Minimum 3.0 GPA strongly preferred.

• Enrolled in an engineering program with an accredited school.

• Understand the basics of engineering theory and principles of design
