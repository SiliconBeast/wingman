About Our Company

We’re a diversified financial services leader with more than $1.5 trillion in assets under management, administration and advisement as of year-end 2024. Our team of 22,000 people across 19 countries, serves more than 3.5 million individual, small business and institutional clients. We are a longstanding leader in financial planning and advice, a global asset manager and an insurer. Our unwavering focus on our clients and strong financial foundation connects each of our unique businesses – Ameriprise Financial, Columbia Threadneedle Investments and RiverSource Insurance and Annuities. Here, we foster meaningful careers, invest in the future, and make a difference for clients, institutions and communities around the world.

About the Role:

Ameriprise Financial has a global Technology team delivering technology solutions to support the evolving needs of our clients, advisors and employees. As a Technology Intern, you are assigned to a specific Technology team for the 10-week summer internship. Technology internship teams and responsibilities vary each year.

For the 2027 summer internship program, we are seeking one intern to join the Asset Management Technology Data Enablement team in our Columbia Threadneedle Investments team in our Boston office.

How you’ll spend your time…

As a Technology intern, you will gain hands-on experience to Ameriprise tools, applications, and technologies while contributing to solutions that support the team’s goals and priorities. During the summer, you’ll connect with other Technology interns and LDPs on a regular basis to foster community and build your professional network. At the end of your internship, you will present an overview of your internship and accomplishments to our Technology leadership team.

As an intern, responsibilities vary depending on assigned team and may include:

•
Define business requirements

•
Develop, document, test, and modify new or existing code

•
Develop working knowledge of Ameriprise systems, processes, data, and vendor tools

•
Learn and apply standard techniques, procedures, and criteria to the software development life cycle

•
Learn new programming languages

•
Identify opportunities for process improvement or automation

•
Participate in Agile team ceremonies
