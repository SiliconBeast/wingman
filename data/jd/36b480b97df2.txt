About NISC

NISC develops and implements enterprise-level and customer-facing software solutions for over 1,200+ energy cooperatives and communication organizations across North America. Our mission is to deliver technology solutions and services that are Member-focused, quality-driven and valued-priced. We exist to serve our Members and help them serve their communities through our innovative software products, services and outstanding customer support. We are an AI-forward company committed to being a technology leader in our industry. NISC has been ranked in ComputerWorld’s Best Places to Work for 23 years, and we are looking for qualified individuals to join our team.

Are you ready for an internship that not only lets you work on real projects and gain authentic job experience but also has an impact on a cooperative that is helping to provide power and communications to millions of homes across the country? Join our team as a Quality Engineer and use your eye for detail, your hunger to learn, and your problem-solving skills to help NISC remain at the forefront of technology!

Essential Functions

• Assist and perform in basic level testing tasks to verify software changes meet design specification and are functioning within total quality management standards.

• Research, test and report results of testing scenarios.

• Write release notes and new system enhancement documentation.

• Participate on design teams to better understand enhancements to products.

• Test system documentation as necessary and suggest edits/enhancements.

• Other duties as assigned.

Desired Education and/or Certification(s)
