If you are a Finance Operations Intern looking for an opportunity to grow your career, Emerson has an exciting opportunity for you! Based in Round Rock, Texas, this Summer 2027 internship offers the opportunity to gain hands-on experience in finance operations within a collaborative and innovative environment. You will work alongside experienced professionals, contribute to meaningful business initiatives, and develop valuable skills supporting financial planning, reporting, analysis, and process improvement activities that help drive business success.

In This Role, Your Responsibilities Will Be:

• Develop and maintain models used to calculate fully burdened cost rates.

• Assist with the preparation and analysis of budgets across a variety of functional areas.

• Support finance team activities related to external audits.

• Contribute to special projects involving deferred revenue recognition and financial reporting.

• Learn and support monthly financial close and reporting processes.

• Identify opportunities for process improvements and assist in developing reports, dashboards, and analytical tools.

• Collaborate with cross-functional teams to support business objectives and enhance operational efficiency.

• Apply analytical thinking and problem-solving skills to real-world business challenges.
