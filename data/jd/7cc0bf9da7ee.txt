Product Management Practice

BNY’s Product Management Practice plays a key role in delivering products, platforms and experiences that meet client needs, support business goals and drive innovation across the firm. The Product Management Internship Program offers driven students hands-on exposure to the practice through strategic projects aligned to product discovery, client-focused design, product development, delivery execution, and continuous improvement. In a collaborative, fast-paced environment, interns will gain insight into the product lifecycle, support analysis and documentation, and work across product, design, business, operations and technology teams to help bring thoughtful, effective solutions to market.

What you will do

•

Research user behavior, client needs, market trends, and competitor activity to inform product priorities and positioning.

•

Participate in discovery activities by gathering feedback from users, clients, and stakeholders and helping identify unmet needs and opportunities.

•

Support roadmap development by organizing requirements, synthesizing research and stakeholder feedback, and helping teams evaluate trade-offs.

•

Help evaluate early product concepts and prototypes by gathering feedback, documenting observations, and identifying opportunities for improvement.

•

Assist in documenting product requirements, user journeys, process flows, and other artifacts that help teams align on product direction.

•

Track product adoption, usage, and performance metrics to identify opportunities for growth and improvement.

•

Collaborate with product, design, engineering, and business teams to help move ideas from discovery through delivery and launch.

Skills you will build

•

Product thinking and strategic prioritization

•

Market, competitor, and user research

•

Requirements gathering and product documentation

•

Feedback synthesis and data-driven decision making

•

Cross-functional collaboration and stakeholder management

Impact you will have at BNY

•

Help teams design products and experiences that better match what clients need now and next.

•

Contribute to product decisions grounded in evidence, feedback, and measurable outcomes.

•

Gain visibility into how innovation, strategy, and execution come together inside BNY.

Why this could be a fit for you

This role resonates if you are curious, entrepreneurial, and energized by building solutions that combine business, technology, and user experience.

Summer Internship Program
The BNY Summer Internship Program offers high-potential students a dynamic 10-week experience designed to accelerate development, build professional networks, and provide meaningful exposure to one of the world's leading financial services institutions. Through a skills-driven talent model, interns gain insight into the capabilities, platforms, and functions that power BNY's global franchise while contributing to impactful business initiatives.

From day one, interns are immersed in BNY's collaborative and innovative culture through:

•

Enterprise-wide onboarding and development programming, including an in person immersive induction experience, executive speaker series, skill development, and networking opportunities.

•

Meaningful project work aligned to key business practices and platforms, providing hands-on experience, skill development, and real-world business impact.

•

Structured mentorship and career support from managers, senior leaders, peer mentors, business stakeholders, and dedicated program managers.

•

Exposure across practices, platforms, and teams, building a broad understanding of BNY's integrated business model.

•

Professional, technical, and financial services training designed to strengthen business acumen and prepare participants for long-term career success.

•

A premier early-career experience, backed by senior leadership and designed to develop the next generation of talent for BNY's global workforce.

Program eligibility:

•

Enrollment in a 4-year undergraduate degree program with a strong focus on business-related and/or technology-related majors preferred

•

Graduating in Dec 2027 or May 2028 (U.S.)

•

Minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0 or better

•

Does not require sponsorship for employment visa status (now or in the future) in the country where applying.
