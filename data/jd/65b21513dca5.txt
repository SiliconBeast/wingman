Your Opportunity

You have the opportunity to join our dynamic team of engineering professionals that are working on projects throughout North America. By joining Stantec, you join a global engineering firm that enables you to work on leading-edge projects with water and wastewater experts.

Stantec Calgary’s Water group is seeking a four-month summer term expected to commence in May 2027 and conclude in August 2028. At Stantec, we’re committed to empowering our employees and providing a dynamic work environment that fosters growth and collaboration. When you join our team, you’ll benefit from:

• Opportunities to work on-site at various locations within Calgary, ensuring exposure to different environments and projects.

• Work directly with a larger, multidisciplinary team, fostering collaboration and innovative problem-solving.

• Connection with experienced senior leaders who provide mentorship and guidance to support your professional development and with junior/intermediate peers who provide a strong team network.

• Engagement in a wide variety of work projects, offering the chance to expand your skills and experience across different industries and disciplines. These tasks include research for up-to-date techniques, calculations, field work, project coordination, reporting, and meeting attendance and recording.

Your Key Responsibilities

As part of the Calgary Water team, you will contribute to the planning, design, and delivery of municipal water and wastewater infrastructure projects. Your responsibilities will include:

• Provide general support to engineers and technologists within the Process Water group.

• Support the field construction and commissioning team on ongoing projects. This may involve:

• Monitoring and reporting on current construction activities

• Assisting the commissioning lead with startup and commissioning activities.

• Onsite document management including deficiency reviews, asset management, document review, and quality assurance.

• Utilize knowledge of the general concepts of relevant basic engineering elements.

• Generate preliminary calculations and reports.

• Review drawings and complete audits on relevant project design elements.

• Perform due diligence and research on projects as required to provide the background information to start new projects.

• Perform other duties of a similar nature and level as assigned.
