Position Overview :

As part of the talented Mercury Marine Parts & Accessories business team, you will be responsible for conducting competitive market research, identifying market gaps, developing dealer catalogs, and creating compelling product descriptions and content. This position will also collaborate with cross-functional teams to support daily operations of the parts and oils categories.

Strong communication skills are essential, as the role involves regular interaction with various departments, divisions, suppliers, and customers. This internship offers an excellent opportunity to gain hands-on experience in the dynamic marine industry, with exposure to marine engines, parts, and oils.

At Brunswick, we have passion for our work and a distinct ability to deliver.

Essential Functions:

•
Support daily product management activities, including the creation of product collateral such as website content and sell sheets, SKU rationalization, sales reporting, and product assortment analysis.

•
Conduct competitive analysis across various product lines by evaluating the 5Ps: pricing, product, promotion, positioning, and placement in online markets.

•
Perform market assessments to identify new growth opportunities and assist in business development initiatives.

•
Maintain and update cross-reference lists, pricing data, and competitive positioning insights.

•
Assist in updating product images and descriptions to help dealers and retail customers accurately identify and purchase Mercury and Quicksilver parts.

•
Collaborate with cross-functional teams, suppliers, customers, and dealers to support product-related initiatives.

•
Analyze industry trends and competitor data to identify opportunities for product line enhancements.

•
Review current product offerings and present recommendations for improvement in a final presentation.

About Mercury Marine

Headquartered in Fond du Lac, Wisconsin, Mercury Marine® is the world’s leading manufacturer of recreational marine propulsion engines. A division of Brunswick Corporation (NYSE: BC), Mercury provides engines, boats, services and parts for recreational, commercial and government marine applications. Mercury empowers boaters with products that are easy to use, extremely reliable and backed by the most dedicated customer support in the world. The company’s industry-leading brand portfolio includes Mercury outboard engines, Mercury MerCruiser® sterndrive and inboard packages, Mercury propellers, Mercury inflatable boats, Mercury SmartCraft® electronics, Land 'N' Sea marine parts distribution and Mercury and Quicksilver® parts and oils. More information is available at MercuryMarine.com.
