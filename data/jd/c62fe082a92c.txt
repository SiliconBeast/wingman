Role Overview:

Help build and validate the next generation of cloud-based software that empowers employees to develop their skills and reach their full potential. As a Software Developer Intern/Co-op in Test on the WEM team, you will gain hands-on experience across the software development lifecycle, contributing to design, development, testing, automation, debugging, and validation of production-quality services.

You will work with technologies such as Java, Kotlin, Python, Selenium, Playwright, and AWS, while learning how engineering teams solve real-world challenges related to scalability, security, quality, reliability, and usability. Working alongside experienced engineers in an agile environment, you will contribute meaningful code and test automation while building a strong foundation in software engineering and quality practices.

At Genesys, you will be part of a collaborative global team building technology that enables organizations to deliver better experiences for both employees and customers.

Key Responsibilities:

•
Contribute to the design, development, and verification of high-quality full-stack software throughout the development lifecycle, from implementation and testing through deployment and continuous improvement.

•
Develop and maintain automated tests that improve software quality, expand test coverage, and increase confidence in production releases.

•
Build and enhance automated test pipelines that enable reliable, efficient, and repeatable software delivery.

•
Test and validate resilient, scalable, secure, and maintainable cloud applications running on AWS.

•
Investigate and debug software issues, collaborating with developers and quality engineering partners to identify root causes and effective solutions.

•
Explore and apply modern technologies, tools, and testing approaches to strengthen automation, engineering efficiency, and continuous improvement.

•
Develop a strong sense of ownership for software quality by participating in design discussions, identifying potential risks, and helping ensure features meet functional and quality expectations.
