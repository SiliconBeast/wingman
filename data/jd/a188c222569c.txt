Group Summary:

The Magna Exteriors portfolio of products includes access systems such as liftgates, exterior trim, modular systems, front-end modules including fascia, active aerodynamic systems and other lightweight structural components for automotive, commercial truck and other industrial markets.

Recognized globally as an innovator in all aspects of vehicle exteriors, Magna provides everything needed, from materials development and design through manufacturing and assembly, to help automakers create sleek, state-of-the-art vehicles across the world.

Job Responsibilities:

Specific Job Duties

Manufacturing Automation Support

· Support Automation Engineers and Technicians with day-to-day production support activities.

· Assist in troubleshooting equipment-related issues.

· Learn the operation and support of PLC-controlled equipment, robotics, vision systems, HMIs, industrial networks, and automated manufacturing equipment.

· Assist with equipment monitoring and performance analysis

Robotics & Controls Exposure

· Gain experience with industrial robotics systems.

· Learn basic PLC troubleshooting and diagnostics.

· Assist with controls system verification and testing activities.

· Support backup management and equipment documentation efforts.

· Participate in automation upgrades and improvement projects.

Equipment Launch & Factory of the Future Support

· Support new equipment installations and commissioning activities.

· Assist with factory acceptance testing (FAT), site acceptance testing (SAT), runoff, and startup activities.

· Participate in Factory of the Future projects and technology deployments.

· Observe and support equipment handoff activities between project teams and production operations.

· Gain exposure to advanced manufacturing technologies and Industry 4.0 initiatives.

Continuous Improvement

· Participate in problem-solving and root cause analysis activities.

· Support data collection and improvement projects.

· Assist with identifying opportunities to improve safety, quality, productivity, and equipment performance.

· Participate in kaizen and continuous improvement initiatives.

Documentation & Standards

· Assist with maintaining equipment documentation, controls backups, troubleshooting procedures, network documentation, and engineering records.

· Support standardization efforts throughout the facility.
