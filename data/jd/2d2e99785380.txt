About the Role

As a Test Engineering Intern, you will gain hands-on experience supporting the development, validation, and optimization of test solutions for semiconductor products. Working alongside experienced engineers, you will contribute to test hardware and software development, product characterization, data analysis, and debug activities while building a strong foundation in semiconductor test engineering.

Key Responsibilities

• Assist in developing, validating, and debugging test hardware and software solutions.

• Support product characterization, qualification, and production test activities.

• Collect, analyze, and interpret test data to support engineering decisions and troubleshooting.

• Assist in the development of test documentation, reports, and technical presentations.

• Support circuit simulation, test setup, and laboratory measurements using standard engineering tools.

• Collaborate with Design, Product, Quality, and Test Engineering teams to resolve technical issues and improve test processes.

Must Have Skills

• Basic understanding of electronics, circuit analysis, and engineering fundamentals.

• Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

• Familiarity with laboratory test equipment and measurement techniques.

• Basic programming or scripting knowledge (Python, C/C++, or similar).

• Ability to analyze and organize technical data using tools such as Excel.

• Strong written and verbal communication skills.

• Ability to work effectively in a collaborative team environment.

Preferred Education and Experience

• Currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related field.

• Experience through coursework, projects, laboratory work, or internships involving electronics, testing, or hardware/software development.

• Familiarity with circuit simulation, debugging, and test methodologies.

• Exposure to automated test systems, data analysis, or engineering software tools is a plus.

• Interest in semiconductor product development, validation, and manufacturing test.
