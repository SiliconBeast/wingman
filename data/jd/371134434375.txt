Position Summary

We are seeking a motivated and detail-oriented Summer Intern to join our Oracle CPQ (Configure, Price, Quote) team. This internship offers hands-on experience with enterprise quoting and pricing technology, providing the opportunity to contribute to real-world projects that directly impact business efficiency and revenue operations. The ideal candidate is eager to learn, comfortable working in a fast-paced environment, and interested in the intersection of technology and business processes.

Key Responsibilities

• Assist in the configuration and maintenance of product rules, pricing models, and quote workflows within Oracle CPQ Cloud

• Support the development and testing of quote document templates and layouts

• Help identify and implement process improvements to streamline the quote-to-cash lifecycle

• Participate in data quality initiatives, including product catalog cleanup and validation

• Create and maintain technical documentation for system configurations and business processes

• Assist with user acceptance testing (UAT) for new features and enhancements

• Collaborate with cross-functional teams including Sales, Finance, Product, and IT to gather requirements and troubleshoot issues

• Support reporting and analytics efforts related to quoting activity and system performance

Qualifications

Required:

• Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Business Administration, or a related field

• Strong analytical and problem-solving skills

• Excellent written and verbal communication skills

• Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and comfort working with data

• Ability to manage multiple tasks and meet deadlines in a collaborative team environment

• Self-starter with a willingness to learn new technologies

Preferred:

• Coursework or experience in business process analysis, sales operations, or systems administration

What You'll Gain

• Hands-on experience with a leading enterprise CPQ platform used by Fortune 500 companies

• Mentorship from experienced CPQ professionals and solution architects

• Exposure to cross-functional business operations and enterprise software lifecycle

• A portfolio of real project work to showcase in future career opportunities

About Vertiv

Vertiv (NYSE: VRT) brings together hardware, software, analytics and ongoing services to enable its customers’ vital applications to run continuously, perform optimally and grow with their business needs. Vertiv solves the most important challenges facing today’s data centers, communication networks, and commercial and industrial facilities with a portfolio of power, cooling and IT infrastructure solutions and services that extend from the cloud to the edge of the network. Headquartered in Westerville, Ohio, USA, Vertiv employs around 34,000 people and does business in more than 130 countries. Visit Vertiv.com to learn more.
