Essential Duties & Responsibilities:

Our Summer Internship is an intensive, exciting opportunity to join our organization.  Interns will take on challenging projects and solve complex problems to help drive the company forward. Roles in this program vary from Business Finance, Corporate FP&A, Operations Finance, Investor Relations, Treasury, and Accounting. Throughout the 12-week summer, interns will also participate in:  

• 1:1 Mentorship  

• Development trainings  

• Executive exposure  

• Social and networking events 

The work location will be based at our US Office in Milpitas, CA.

Required:

• Current student pursuing a Bachelors Degree with concentration in Finance, Accounting, Business, or Economics with an expected graduation date of May/June 2028

• Previous internship(s) in a corporate environment is highly preferred

• Available to work full-time during the summer on a collaborative/hybrid schedule starting June 2027

• Willing to relocate to Milpitas,CA for the duration of the internship

Preferred:

• Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to create effective presentations for management

• Ability to work both independently and in large, cross-functional team settings

• Proficient in PowerBI, Excel, AI Tools and other Microsoft Office applications
