Your Role: R&D Laboratory Intern

As an R&D Laboratory Intern at Elanco’s global headquarters in Indianapolis, you will contribute directly to our Research and Breakthrough Innovation and Technical Development organizations by performing hands-on laboratory work in support of new and existing animal health products. You will own the progress of an innovative project and work alongside scientists and engineers to design and execute experiments, generate and interpret data, and contribute to robust processes and products for companion and/or farm animals.

Interns may be placed in one of several teams, including but not limited to:

• Synthetic Technical Development – Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient, Formulation, or Analytical

• Biopharma Technical Development – Bioprocess Development, Formulation, or Analytical

• Early Research Antibody Discovery

Your Responsibilities:

• Design and execute hands-on experiments in an R&D laboratory ( e.g. , synthetic chemistry, formulation development, analytical development, upstream/downstream bioprocess development, cell culture, immunoassays, molecular biology, or protein sciences), following established safety protocols and guidelines.

• Prepare solutions, reagents, media, and test articles. Operate relevant instrumentation. Analyze and interpret experimental data, summarize findings, and propose next steps in collaboration with your supervisor and project team.

• Perform independent laboratory work under appropriate supervision, including troubleshooting and optimizing experimental procedures.

• Provide recommendations on experimental design, instrumentation, or process improvements that may enhance robustness, efficiency, or scalability.

• Participate in regular check-ins, presenting progress and results to your supervisor and cross-functional stakeholders.

• Rigorously document experimental plans, procedures, and results in laboratory notebooks, maintaining high standards and data integrity.

• Present final project results in an oral presentation summarizing your project objectives, methods, results, and key learnings to R&D colleagues and the intern community.

What You Need to Succeed (Minimum Qualifications):

Education: Currently enrolled in a STEM field such as chemistry, biology, biochemistry, chemical engineering, pharmaceutical science, molecular biology, microbiology, biomedical engineering, or other related fields

• Degree: Bachelor’s, Master’s, or PhD

• Academic Progress: Students must return to a degree seeking program for the fall semester following the completion of the internship.

• Technical Skills and Core Competencies:

• Experience with hands-on laboratory work grained through independent research, teaching labs, or prior internship experience.

• Comfort working at the bench ( e.g. , pipetting, solution preparation, aseptic technique, basic instrumentation) and following written protocols.

• Basic proficiency with Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint for data organization, analysis, and presentation.

• Scientific curiosity and a desire to innovate. Strong learning agility and willingness to acquire new techniques and concepts quickly.

• Demonstrated critical thinking and problem-solving skills.

• Ability to work effectively in team environments and communicate clearly with technical and non-technical colleagues

What Will Give You a Competitive Edge (Preferred Qualifications):

• Academic Progress: Expected graduation date between December 2027 and August 2028 with intention of entering industry following degree completion.

• Research Experience: Sustained performance (>1 semester) in an undergraduate or graduate STEM research laboratory (academic, industry, or prior internship/co-op) with demonstrated technical growth.

• Area-Specific Experience (depending on interest and placement):

• Synthetic/Analytical Development: Organic synthesis, route scouting, analytical method development, chromatography, spectroscopy, physicochemical characterization, or solid form characterization.

• Formulation Development: Experience preparing dosage forms (e.g., solutions, suspensions, tablets, injectables), working with excipients, or conducting stability and compatibility studies. Experience with synthetic molecules, peptides, or biologics.

• Bioprocess/Upstream Development: Mammalian or microbial cell culture, aseptic technique, bioreactor operation, media/feed preparation, process optimization, or scale up studies.

• Antibody/Biologics: Immunoassays (e.g., ELISA), protein expression and purification, molecular cloning, flow cytometry, or related techniques.

• Familiarity with statistical or data analysis tools (e.g., R, JMP, Python, Minitab, or similar) and/or data management systems.

• Ability to work exceptionally well both independently and as part of a multidisciplinary team, with appropriate levels of supervision.

• Demonstrated interest in animal health, sustainability, veterinary medicine, or data driven approaches to R&D.

• Strong written and oral communication skills, including the ability to clearly present scientific results.

• Strong organizational and time management skills, with the ability to manage multiple tasks in a fast-paced research environment.
