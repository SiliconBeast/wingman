Firmware Engineering Intern Position Overview

We are looking for a graduate-level intern to support the development, simulation, and testing of embedded firmware and control algorithms for Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) systems.

The ideal candidate is currently pursuing a Master’s or PhD in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Control Systems, Power Electronics, or a related field. A background in digital signal processing, control systems, power conversion, or model-based development is preferred.

Responsibilities

• Support the development and testing of embedded firmware for UPS power conversion systems.

• Model and simulate power converters and control systems using MATLAB and Simulink.

• Assist with the implementation of digital control algorithms on DSPs or microcontrollers.

• Support Model-in-the-Loop, Software-in-the-Loop, and Hardware-in-the-Loop testing activities.

• Develop and execute test cases to evaluate control performance, firmware behavior, and fault conditions.

• Compare simulation results with embedded software and hardware test results.

• Support laboratory testing, debugging, and analysis of power electronic hardware.

• Work with power conversion stages such as PFC converters, DC/DC converters, and inverters.

• Document simulation results, test data, and technical findings.

• Collaborate with firmware, hardware, controls, and test engineers.

Basic Qualifications

• Currently pursuing a Master’s or PhD in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related field.

• Coursework, research, or project experience in control systems, DSP, embedded systems, or power electronics.

• Experience with MATLAB or Simulink.

• Basic programming experience in C or C++.

• Basic understanding of feedback control and power conversion concepts.

• Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

Preferred Qualifications

• Familiarity with Model-in-the-Loop, Software-in-the-Loop, or Hardware-in-the-Loop validation.

• Experience creating simulation models, test cases, or automated test scripts.

• Familiarity with DSPs or embedded microcontrollers.

• Exposure to PFC, DC/DC converter, or inverter topologies.

• Experience working with oscilloscopes and laboratory equipment.

• Knowledge of PWM, digital control loops, or real-time embedded systems.

• Experience with Python, Git, or automated testing is a plus.
