Becoming Vernova

Looking to make a global impact and solve problems?

Through relentless innovation and continuous collaboration with our customers, GE Onshore Wind , part of GE Vernova, is providing more advanced, cleaner, and efficient power that people depend on today and building the energy technologies of the future.

What impact you’ll make

As a student of the Top Issues Services Team, you will be an active contributor to the improvement of Fleet wind turbine's reliability and safety.

What you’ll do (Job Responsibilities)

• Own the implementation of retrofits into the Wind Turbine fleet
• Early engagement with engineering team to understand the engineering fix and the validation of the process
• Ordering of parts and following up with global supply chain to ensure no delays
• Establish of execution strategy and supplier selection
• Ensure safety and quality of execution teams
• Facilitate feedback to engineering as program matures with any issues

What you’ll bring (Basic Qualifications)

• Good understanding and familiarity with excel, ppt and other MS Office teals

• Basic Understanding of engineering principles, electrical, mechanical and controls

• Good understanding of program management, from parts ordering to installation in Wind Turbine

• Current enrollment in undergrad or graduate degree.

What will make you stand out (Desired Qualifications)

• You have a passion for learning and understanding how business processes work.
• You are someone who brings a sense of ownership.

• Excellent written and verbal communication skills and ability to articulate clearly.
• Demonstrated creative problem solving and proactive learning.
