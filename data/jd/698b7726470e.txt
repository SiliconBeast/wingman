Who Are We?

The Toro Company (TTC) has delivered market-leading, innovative products and outstanding customer care for more than 100 years. With over 11,000 employees worldwide, TTC is headquartered in Bloomington, Minnesota, USA.

Our company designs and manufactures turf maintenance equipment; turf irrigation systems; landscape and lighting products; snow and ice management solutions; agricultural irrigation systems; rental, specialty and underground construction equipment; and residential turf and snow equipment. We market and sell TTC products worldwide through a network of distributors, dealers, mass retailers, hardware retailers, equipment rental center and home centers—as well as online direct to end users.

What Will You Do?

In order to grow and build a successful career with The Toro Company, you will be responsible for:

• Create a specification for the product.

• Schematic capture and circuit design.

• PCB layout with the help of one of Toro’s PCB designers.

• Test the finished product in the lab using tools like oscilloscopes, multimeters, etc.

The work will be guided by other electrical engineers to provide mentoring and keep the project on task. Other summer activities will include some job shadowing and supporting a current team project, possibly a vendor visit or trip to Toro manufacturing plant(s), and depending upon the candidate, some scripting in Python or similar language.

What Do You Need?

To be considered for this role, an individual should meet the following minimal requirements:

• Majoring in Electrical or Electronic Engineering from an ABET accredited university.

• Completed junior-level courses preferred.

• Strong desire to learn, collaborate, and effectively communicate in a team setting while achieving a common goal.

• Ability to stay organized and able to use Microsoft Office programs without much direction.

• Bonus skills include: Analog circuit design, digital circuit design, ARM microcontrollers, PCB design, C programming, Python or similar Scripting Language.
