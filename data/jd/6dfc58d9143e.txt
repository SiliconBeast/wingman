Job Description

What impact you’ll make

• As a member of the Critical Infrastructure Communication (CIC) Engineering Team you will:

• Learn our product portfolio and become a developer on one of our product lines.

• Be part of an active development team working on a next generation product line.

• Secondary duties may include assisting with designing/developing sub-systems and features in Software Development.

What you’ll do

• Use your course related skills in Java, C or C++ to design, develop and test software.

• Successfully integration of the GE Cloud DevOps tools into the existing GE MDS CI/CD process (ex: python robotframework supporting GE end-to-end functional Software System Regression Automation).

• As part of the development process, follows engineering processes, practices, and procedures.

• Report progress and status to project management.

• Perform rough estimates of time required to implement features and resolve issues, helping with task prioritization process.

• Work on an assignment / project within the engineering & technology; to stretch your learning and skillset.

• Must be highly self-motivated and be able to work under minimal supervision

• Use your academic knowledge to complete assigned project(s), deliver customer outcomes and improve the operation of our business.

• Learn and utilize software development techniques for disciplined design rigor.

What you’ll bring (Basic Qualifications)

• Must be enrolled in a full-time undergraduate or graduate program in a technical field of study in Engineering, Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or IT.

What will make you stand out

• Must maintain a minimum 3.0 cumulative GPA (without rounding).

• Experience in Computer and Networking administration (ex: Linux, Windows).

• Experience in software development and revision control, such as git/GitHub.

• Ability to follow formal software design principles and development processes.

• Experience in python script development.

• Experience in Linux bash and Windows Batch scripts.

• Some knowledge of networking protocols such as TCP/IP, SSH, HTTP, DHCP

• Working understanding of JIRA, Confluence (or equivalent tools).

• Working understanding of physical network topologies and Ethernet bridging.

• Some knowledge of Git.

• Proficient in programming, Java, C++, C.

• Experience creating Web UIs.

• Working understanding of physical network topologies and Ethernet bridging

• An analytical approach to problem solving with a high degree of curiosity.

• Willingness and ability to learn from others.

• Demonstrated leadership and team player.

• Excellent interpersonal skills.

• One who strives for academic excellence and shows great initiative.

• Desire to work in a fast paced and agile environment.
