This position supports the Ankura Disputes & Economics Business Group - one of five practices focused on client delivery services across the Firm.

Practice Overview:  The Financial Services Advisory practice assists clients in Banking, Insurance, Capital Markets, Wealth Management, FinTech and related industries. Our primary services are Business Strategy and Transformation, Mergers & Acquisitions, Risk, Compliance, and Controls, and Data & AI.

Role Overview: Interns, working as part of a project team, perform critical, value-added work that can directly impact a client’s bottom line.  A sample of diverse consulting engagements that you could be involved in include:

• Identifying, prioritizing, and sequencing AI implementation for a Wealth Management firm

• Reducing a retirement organization’s operating costs by $80m/year

• Executing a firmwide program to enhance a bank’s processes and control

• Optimizing and redesigning an insurers claims function

• Documenting a bank’s obligations that arose from laws, rules, and regulatory requirements

• Redesigning a corporate trust’s operating model

Essential Duties and Responsibilities:

• Identifying gaps and opportunities for improvement and automation in processes and controls and related risks

• Reviewing client documentation to understand current-state processes

• Documenting findings through crafting stories in PowerPoint

• Preparing client reports and exhibits

• Presenting findings to the consulting team and clients
