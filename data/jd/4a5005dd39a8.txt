Change the world. Love your job.

At TI, Systems Marketing Engineering Interns within the Aerospace and Defense (A&D) team push technological boundaries through system innovations in the demanding A&D industry. Through direct interaction with A&D customers, product development teams, and sales, you will enable current and future system advancements in end equipments like space, radar, and communications.

In this system marketing engineering internship, you’ll have the chance to work on projects that involve:

• Work with leading A&D customers to provide innovative, high performance solutions to their most challenging problems

• Utilize TI’s broad portfolio of commercial, automotive and high reliability devices to create whole system level solutions to accelerate our customers’ product life cycle

• Combine hardware and software capabilities to further integrate complete TI solutions

• Develop system level reference designs including: block diagram, schematic capture, PCB layout, fabrication, assembly and lab testing.

• Interface directly with TI business units and customers to influence product roadmaps

• Gather user requirements, translate them to functional specifications, and implement solutions and enhancements to broader end equipments.

• Provide complete documentation for system level solution

• Perform troubleshooting and debugging on issues related to the system working directly with customers

As a Systems Marketing Engineering intern, you will also get exposure to other roles such as: Applications Engineering, Product Marketing Engineering, Systems Engineering, and Sales that will help you broaden your knowledge and accelerate your growth and success at TI.

Put your talent to work with us as a systems marketing engineer!

About Texas Instruments
