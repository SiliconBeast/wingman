About the team
Annapurna Labs designs the custom silicon at Amazon - including Graviton (our server processors), Trainium and Inferentia (our machine learning training and inference accelerators), and the Nitro system that powers modern EC2. We own the full stack, from silicon through the software that makes it work (compilers, runtimes, drivers, and the frameworks that let customers run workloads on our hardware). As a Software Development Engineer Intern, you'll write the software that turns custom silicon into products used by millions.

Where you might land:
Annapurna has software teams spanning the full stack, and we work to place you on a technical team aligned with your interests and strengths. Example teams and focus areas include:

- Compiler development for ML accelerators (Neuron SDK, MLIR, LLVM)
- ML runtimes and frameworks (integration with PyTorch, JAX, TensorFlow)
- Low-level systems software, firmware, and device drivers
- Performance optimization and kernel development for custom silicon
- Distributed training and inference infrastructure
Once you join, we partner with you and our hiring managers to match you to a team where your background and interests fit best, so your project is something you're genuinely excited to work on.

As an SDE Intern, you'll contribute to real production software, which may include:

- Designing, implementing, and testing software across the stack - from low-level systems code to compilers, runtimes, and tooling
- Writing efficient, maintainable code in languages such as C, C++, or Python
- Optimizing software performance on custom hardware (Graviton, Trainium, Inferentia)
- Debugging complex issues across the hardware-software boundary
- Collaborating with hardware, compiler, and systems teams to deliver features end-to-end
- Documenting your work and presenting results to the team
You'll be paired with a mentor and manager who will scope your project, support your growth, and set you up to deliver meaningful, measurable impact by the end of your internship.
