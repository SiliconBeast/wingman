About the Hiring Team

What the Role Entails
We are looking for a proactive and detail-oriented IT Operations Intern to join Tencent’s IT team in the PA office, United States. You will work closely with our IT Operations, Infrastructure, and Service Support teams to support daily IT operations, project delivery, data analysis, coordination, and continuous improvement initiatives.
This role is suitable for someone with an IT background who is interested in enterprise IT operations, service management, project execution, operational planning, and cross-team collaboration. You will gain hands-on experience in real business scenarios and support the team in improving IT service quality, delivery efficiency, and operational visibility.

Responsibilities

• Support day-to-day IT operations, including issue follow-up, service tracking, operational reporting, and documentation.

• Assist in coordinating IT projects and operational initiatives, including task tracking, meeting notes, progress updates, risk follow-up, and stakeholder communication.

• Work with IT engineers and operations teams to support project delivery, ensuring actions are clearly assigned, followed up, and closed on time.

• Collect, organize, and analyze IT operational data to identify trends, issues, risks, and improvement opportunities.

• Prepare dashboards, reports, trackers, and management summaries to improve visibility of IT operations and project progress.

• Support the planning and coordination of team activities, operational reviews, training sessions, workshops, and cross-team meetings.

• Help maintain internal knowledge bases, process documents, service guides, and project-related materials.

• Communicate with internal users and stakeholders to understand requests, collect feedback, clarify requirements, and support issue resolution.

• Support complex and multi-threaded tasks that require coordination across different teams, systems, or regions.

• Identify opportunities to improve operational processes, including workflow optimization, better use of existing tools, and light automation where applicable.
