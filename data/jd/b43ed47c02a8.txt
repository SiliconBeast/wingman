It will also include the following:

• Excellent digital project management skills supporting large scale Transformation Implementations.

• Help draft, update, and standardize global SOPs and training documentation for MSI Supply Chain

• Identify operational bottlenecks, lead time variances, or process hand-off gaps from forecasting to receiving.

• Strong analytical skills

• Participate in and lead continuous improvement projects.

• Strong orientation to leadership, teamwork, and positive communication; a flexible, dynamic, self‐demanding and proactive individual, able to motivate him/herself and others

• Basic AI Literacy: Familiarity with Generative AI platforms (ChatGPT, Copilot, Claude) and practical experience with prompt engineering techniques.
