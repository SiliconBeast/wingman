About the Role

As a Technical Support Engineering Intern, you won't be grabbing coffee or shadowing others. Our interns are at the forefront of our team, tackling real-world challenges alongside our full-time engineers. Based at our San Mateo HQ, you'll work with cutting-edge, cloud-managed IoT technology, helping our enterprise clients with everything from troubleshooting to system design.

This internship is your chance to make a real impact, build valuable technical skills, and jumpstart your career in a dynamic, high-tech environment.

What You'll Do

• Hands-on Troubleshooting: Dive into complex network, hardware, and software problems on Verkada's platform

• Level Up Fast: Become the go-to expert customers trust for smart system design and integration advice

• Communicate with Impact: Translate heavy technical concepts into clear, sharp advice on phone calls, live chat, and emails

• Own It, End to End: Take full ownership of customer issues from "we have a problem" to "problem solved"

• Team Up with Tech Leaders: Partner directly with Engineering and Product teams to test new gear and hunt down bugs before anyone else

• Share Knowledge: Write Knowledge Base content that helps thousands of users

What You Bring

• Available for a full-time (40 hours per week) in-person internship for Spring 2027 in San Mateo, CA

• A third or fourth-year student in a Bachelor's degree program from an accredited university, with a focus on a related technical field like Networking, Telecommunications, Systems Administration, Information Technology, Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or Cybersecurity

• You have coursework experience with computer networking, Linux, and computer systems

• You're a problem-solver with a keen eye for detail

• You can clearly and confidently communicate complex technical information

• You thrive in a team environment

• Industry certifications (like CCNP, CCNA, or Network+) are a bonus, but not required!
