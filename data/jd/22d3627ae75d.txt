Role Overview
As a Software Developer Intern on the Genesys Cloud team, you will embed directly into an agile engineering team delivering high-impact features across our cloud platform. In this role, you will specifically contribute to projects focused on modern reporting and data insights, leveraging cutting-edge AI tools to accelerate development, improve software quality, and enhance engineering workflows.

You’ll experience a high-energy development environment where quality, automation, and scalability matter—all supported by a collaborative, friendly, and laid-back team culture. Alongside hands-on technical experience, you can expect dedicated mentorship, exposure to leadership across our Cloud business, and flexible work arrangements (in-office, hybrid, or fully virtual depending on location).

Responsibilities / Job Duties

•
Build & Deliver: Collaborate with team members to design, develop, and test scalable, reliable features powering our reporting and analytics solutions.

•
AI-Augmented Engineering: Actively leverage modern AI coding assistants and development tools to explore, prototype, write, and refactor code, accelerating time-to-market.

•
Engineering Quality & Testing: Write and maintain comprehensive unit, integration, and acceptance tests to ensure enterprise-grade stability and performance.

•
Collaborative Development: Actively participate in peer code reviews, architectural discussions, and agile team ceremonies.

•
Security & Compliance: Understand and uphold Genesys' strict standards for security, data privacy (including HIPAA compliance guidelines), and our Code of Business Conduct and Ethics.

•
Have Fun: Bring your enthusiasm, engage with teammates, and enjoy learning and building great software together.
