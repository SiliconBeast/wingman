About This Role:

Electrical Test Engineer Co-Ops at iRhythm work passionately to deliver innovative solutions that improve the quality of healthcare and the patient experience. In this role you will contribute directly to near and long-term commercialization efforts for our next-generation wearable bio-sensors, collaborating alongside a small team of multi-disciplinary engineers experienced in bringing medical devices to market. You will be treated as a regular member of the engineering team, inclusive of the associated projects, deliverables and accountability.

Assignment Detail s

This is a full-time, six-month Engineering Internship / Cooperative Education (Co-op) assignment for a current student. It offers internship-style learning, mentorship, and meaningful project work, but it is not a traditional summer internship. Candidates must be available for the full January through June 2027 assignment in San Francisco.

About You:

We are seeking an enthusiastic electrical engineering student or new graduate to be a part of the iRhythm Electrical Engineering team. You will gain exposure to the testing approach required to release Class 2 compliant hardware in the medical device industry. Your tasks will be to test, analyze, design, evaluate and debug circuits to support exciting additions to our product line. You have excellent technical and documentation skills, approach prototyping and debug with curiosity, interested in automation, appreciate feedback and feel satisfied once you collected data to back up your ideas.

Desired Qualifications

• Pursuing a PhD or Master’s or Bachelor's degree in biomedical, mechanical, electrical, or a similar engineering discipline

• Strong EE fundamentals

• Detail-oriented with excellent troubleshooting and critical thinking skills

• Strong understand of analog and digital circuits, with underlying strength in math and physics

• Hands-on lab experience including soldering, data acquisition, lab test equipment: multi-meters, oscilloscopes, power supplies, etc

• Hands-on experience in prototype bring-up, debugging and functional verification

• Familiarity with schematic and layout tools (Altium a bonus) as well as circuit analysis and simulation tools (SPICE)

• Experience with a scripting language for analyzing and plotting data (Python a plus)

• Ability to use Office tools: spreadsheets, word processor, etc

• Strong communication and interpersonal skills

• Comfortable working with minimal supervision
