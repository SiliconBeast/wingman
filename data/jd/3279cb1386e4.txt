ABOUT THE ROLE & TEAM

Simulation is how we move fast, but a sim is only as good as the machine model underneath it. The V&V team measures what real actuators and real environments actually do, and feeds that back until sim and reality agree. As our V&V intern, you'll own black-box testing of hardware actuators and help quantify and then close the sim-to-real gap.




WHAT YOU'LL DO

 - Develop and execute black-box test plans that characterize actuator behavior on machines and test stands

 - Instrument hardware, set up data acquisition, and collect clean, trustworthy datasets

 - Characterize response behavior — latency, deadband, hysteresis, saturation — and how it shifts with temperature, load, and wear

 - Compare real machine data against simulation output and quantify where they diverge

 - Feed your findings to the controls and simulation teams as concrete model improvements

 - Automate test execution and reporting so these runs become routine


WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR
