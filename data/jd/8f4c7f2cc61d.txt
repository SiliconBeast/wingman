Position Summary

The Product Management MBA Intern will support Vertiv's Power Solutions & Distribution (PSD) business by assisting with product strategy, portfolio management, market analysis, and growth initiatives. The intern will work closely with the Product Management team on strategic projects and recommendations that support business growth, profitability, and new product development efforts. The internship provides hands-on experience in managing a global technology product portfolio serving the data center and critical infrastructure markets.

Key Responsibilities

•

Conduct market, competitive, and customer research to identify growth opportunities and product strategy recommendations.

•

Analyze product portfolio performance, revenue trends, and profitability drivers.

•

Support evaluation of emerging technologies and new market opportunities.

•

Develop business cases and strategic recommendations for new product or feature investments.

•

Assist Product Managers with product roadmap development and portfolio planning activities.

•

Gather and analyze customer feedback to identify unmet needs and product improvement opportunities.

•

Create executive-level presentations and recommendations for business leaders.

•

Contribute to strategic initiatives focused on portfolio expansion, market share growth, and profitability improvement.

•

Support analysis and recommendations related to product positioning, pricing, and competitive differentiation.

About Vertiv

Vertiv (NYSE: VRT) brings together hardware, software, analytics and ongoing services to enable its customers’ vital applications to run continuously, perform optimally and grow with their business needs. Vertiv solves the most important challenges facing today’s data centers, communication networks, and commercial and industrial facilities with a portfolio of power, cooling and IT infrastructure solutions and services that extend from the cloud to the edge of the network. Headquartered in Westerville, Ohio, USA, Vertiv employs around 34,000 people and does business in more than 130 countries. Visit Vertiv.com to learn more.
