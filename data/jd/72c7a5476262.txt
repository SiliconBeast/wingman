Labcorp is seeking a Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) Intern to join our Finance Team located in Indianapolis, Indiana , working a primarily remote work schedule and occasional in-person collaboration in Indianapolis.

Work Schedule:

Days/Hours: Monday–Friday / 40 hours per week

Job Responsibilities:

• Support financial planning, forecasting, reporting, and business analysis activities across the organization.

• Assist with financial analysis, budgeting, and forecasting processes.

• Support the preparation of management reporting and financial analyses used to inform business decisions.

• Analyze financial and business information to identify trends and provide insights.

• Partner with finance professionals and business leaders on financial planning and analysis activities.

• Support process improvement initiatives that enhance financial reporting, planning, and analysis.

• Contribute to projects that support strategic decision-making across the organization.

Minimum Qualifications:

• Current enrollment in a Bachelor’s degree program.

• Working towards a degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business Administration, or a related business discipline.

Additional Job Standards:

• Basic understanding of financial statements, budgeting, forecasting, and financial analysis concepts.

• Proficiency in Microsoft Excel, including formulas, pivot tables, and data analysis.

• Strong analytical, problem-solving, and organizational skills.

• Effective written and verbal communication skills.

• Ability to manage multiple priorities and work independently in a remote environment while collaborating with team members as needed.
