The Allied Solutions internship program is a robust and consistent internship program for juniors and seniors. The program will focus on learning and development as well as hands-on experience. The program will immerse the intern into a broad range of responsibilities, allowing them to identify their professional interests.

Interested applicants must be available during the 12-week program (May 24th – Aug. 13th, 2027). This is a full-time internship (40-hours per week) based in St. Paul, Minnesota on the Securian campus.

The Risk Management Product Intern will help the Risk Management Product Department with various projects related to managing products like vehicle insurance tracking, real estate insurance tracking, financial recovery solutions offered to Lenders and more. Product management projects and tasks may include the design and implementation of a Product Management knowledge hub/sharepoint site, development of monthly product summary report and identification of key inputs, identification and assessment of current Service Level Agreements (SLAs) and tracking/reporting capabilities, or standard product packaging alignment and change management. In this position, the intern will gain a better understanding of the role and gain hands-on experience for them to be better prepared to work in Financial Services and Lending.
