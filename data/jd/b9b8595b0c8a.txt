Job Description:

TD Securities offers a wide range of capital markets products and services to corporate, government, and institutional clients who choose us for our innovation, execution, and experience. With more than 6,500 professionals operating out of 40 cities across the globe, we help clients meet their needs today and prepare for tomorrow.

Institutional Equities (the Equity Division of TD Securities) is a client-driven business which provides equity sales, trading, research, underwriting and distribution support with offices in the United States, Canada, UK, Europe and Asia. Institutional Equities delivers investment ideas, research product and new issues to buy-side clients and earns commissions on agency trades generated or fees on new issue deals distributed to clients.

We are growth-oriented, people-focused, and community-minded. As a team, we work to deliver value for our clients every day.

Job Responsibilities:

• Working closely with Equities Traders and Salespeople to assist with client requests, conduct day-to-day activities which help facilitate client trading

• Produce and distribute recurring market and trading reports used by Traders throughout the day

• Analyze and validate market and trading data, while identifying opportunities to improve efficiency through automation and process enhancements.

• Support the desk through ad-hoc projects and assignments

• Conduct research and obtain market data through Bloomberg, trading systems, and internal databases

• Assist in design and implementation of Front Office solutions for trading analytics and operations
