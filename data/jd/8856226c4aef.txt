Position Overview

Are you excited about the potential for AI to transform not only what we build, but how we build it? Areyou curious about how Product Managers work, how they make decisions, and where AI can help them work more effectively?

We are seeking a curious, resourceful, and hands-on Product Management Intern to join Autodesk for a unique 16-week internship focused on exploring how AI can improve the way our Product Management organization works.

During this internship, you will own a real Product Management problem from beginning to end. Rather than being handed a predefined feature to implement, you will operate as the Product Manager for the project, with Autodesk Product Managers as your customers.

You will work directly with Product Managers to understand their workflows, identify recurring pain points, and determine where AI can automate or augment their work. You will then prioritize the highest-value opportunities, rapidly build and test AI-powered solutions, measure their impact, and drive adoption across the Product Management organization.

Responsibilities

•
Work closely with Autodesk Product Managers to understand their workflows, challenges, and needs.

•
Conduct customer interviews and observations to identify pain points and opportunities for AI.

•
Identify and prioritize AI opportunities based on customer value, frequency, feasibility, and adoption potential.

•
Define the product vision, requirements, hypotheses, and success metrics for selected opportunities.

•
Use AI tools and rapid prototyping to develop solutions that Product Managers can test early.

•
Follow a Build → Measure → Learn → Iterate approach using customer feedback and data.

•
Measure product impact through time saved, adoption, task completion, quality, and user satisfaction.

•
Develop rollout and adoption strategies for successful AI solutions.

•
Create onboarding, documentation, and training materials to help Product Managers use the solutions effectively.

•
Communicate insights, decisions, experiments, results, and recommendations while collaborating with Product Managers and Autodesk partners.

Minimum Qualifications

•
Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Business, Product Management, Design, Data Science, or a related field.

•
Strong interest in Product Management and a desire to learn the discipline through hands-on experience.

•
Curiosity about artificial intelligence and enthusiasm for exploring how AI can improve knowledge-work and business workflows into actionable ideas.

•
Comfortable speaking with users, asking thoughtful questions, synthesizing feedback, and identifying patterns across qualitative information.

•
Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to clearly explain ideas, decisions,and findings.

•
Analytical mindset and comfort using qualitative and quantitative information to make decisions.

Preferred Qualifications

•
Experience with Product Management through coursework, internships, student organizations, entrepreneurial projects, hackathons, or personal projects.

•
Experience building prototypes, applications, automations, or other solutions using AI tools, large language models, APIs, low-code/no-code tools, or software development technologies.

•
Familiarity with concepts such as customer discovery, product experimentation, prototyping, product analytics, and user research.

•
Basic understanding of software development and the ability to collaborate effectively with technical teams.

•
Interest in developer tools, enterprise software, platforms, or the Design & Make industries Autodesk serves.
