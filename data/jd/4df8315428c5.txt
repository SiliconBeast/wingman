What You Will Do

Immerse yourself in a full-time, Monday-Friday co-op session, that will run May/June- December (including the Fall semester; it is expected students will take a semester off from coursework to fully engage with this working mentorship opportunity).​

• Gather field data, assist in root cause analysis, corrective action plans, and implementation of service bulletins.

• Address issues related to in-service aircraft and component repair.

• Document and communicate technical information to technical/non-technical stakeholders.

• Develop and verify training materials used in OEM/operator training classes.

• Validate and update technical service engineering documentation.

• Help ensure product enhancements/improvements are complete and accurate through review of engineering data and implementation instructions.
