Northrop Grumman is seeking Program Planning and Scheduling Analyst Intern for an internship opportunity. This position will be located at our Space sector in Dulles, VA

The qualified candidate will become part of Northrop Grumman’s East Region Space Superiority Division – Scheduling Management team.

What You Will Learn

• How integrated master plans and integrated master schedules are prepared, developed, and coordinated to support program objectives.

• How major projects and program schedules are horizontally and vertically integrated across functional groups such as finance, engineering, material, manufacturing, and quality.

• How to track plans and schedules, perform basic risk analysis, and support the identification and resolution of critical path and network logic conflicts.

• How Gantt charts, PERT charts, milestone charts, earned value management, and other project management techniques are used to measure progress and identify performance variances, enabling teams to focus on critical areas.
