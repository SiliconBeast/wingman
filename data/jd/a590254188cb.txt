SUMMARY

As an intern in the Fund Data Reporting and Analytics Team (“Fund Data Team)” within Grosvenor’s Finance Department, the Intern will focus primarily on preparing portfolio performance reports and analyses in support of GCM Grosvenor’s business development and marketing initiatives for our Private Equity, Infrastructure, and Real Estate investment verticals.

The Fund Data Team works closely with our Client Services, Marketing, Corporate, and Investment teams, which submit recurring and ad hoc requests for performance and benchmarking reports, pro forma portfolio analyses, and other portfolio metrics. Furthermore, the Fund Data Team works closely with our Fund Finance team and our administrators to obtain the information necessary to prepare the requested reports and analyses. The role operates in a fast-paced, dynamic, deadline-driven environment, so the ideal candidate must be a well-organized, thoughtful team player who operates well under pressure and has excellent analytical and communication skills.

RESPONSIBILITIES

The individual will be involved in the following critical activities:

• Analyze data related to fund and co-investments across all our private equity, infrastructure, credit, and real estate-focused portfolios.

• Prepare, analyze, and report performance information for business development and marketing purposes as well as for GCM Grosvenor’s investment team and senior management.

• Prepare historical and forward-looking analysis for management fees, carried interest, and expenses.

• Assist in Master Entity Data management across the GCM fund and investment universe.

• Perform various ad-hoc and medium-term projects as assigned. Manage multiple requests simultaneously, ensuring that deadlines are met.

• Regularly interact with disparate departments across the firm to obtain information required to prepare the requested reports and analyses.

• Manage analyses and requests through a multitude of applications and data visualization tools such as Microsoft PowerBI, Microsoft PowerQuery, Tableau, IHS Market iLevel, third party benchmarking services, and various Microsoft Excel Add-ins. Gain exposure to managing and querying transactional and reference data from enterprise data warehouses and accounting databases.

EDUCATION, SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE REQUIREMENTS

• Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, Economics, or Business Analytics with outstanding academic credentials is required.

• Strong analytical, critical thinking and problem-solving skills are required. The ideal candidate will have the ability to articulate complex thoughts in a clear and concise manner.

• Microsoft Excel skills and a demonstrated aptitude for quantitative and qualitative analysis are required.

• A strong attention to detail and concern for the quality and accuracy of final work products is critical.

• The highest degree of integrity and the ability to maintain confidential information is absolutely necessary.

• The ideal candidate will have a strong work ethic, a high level of energy and the flexibility to work extended hours at times to accommodate specific projects.

In terms of cultural fit, the successful candidate will be self-motivated and energized by working amongst a group of thoughtful, smart, and successful colleagues. They will enjoy being part of an organization focused on excellence and will be a naturally collaborative person who enjoys interacting with individuals at all levels. Additionally, they will be a strong team player with a proactive approach and the ability to exercise discretion and judgment.
