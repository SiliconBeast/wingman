The Opportunity

Our internship program lets you showcase your skills, while gaining real-life experience that’ll help you jump-start your career. Throughout the internship, you'll participate in professional development opportunities, networking events, mentorship programs, and team-based projects designed to help prepare you for a successful career in technology. Our interns deliver innovative solutions to real business challenges, while building enduring relationships in a collaborative culture with their manager and an assigned mentor.

This position is based in San Antonio, TX.

What you'll do:

Under direct supervision and with guidance from senior engineers and your mentor, your day will involve supporting your team in various tasks, including the design, development, and deployment of Large Learning Models for production environments. The work will be aligned with USAA's technology strategy, potentially involving the use of AI tools and automation to enhance efficiency.

Primary Responsibilities:

• Design, test, and optimize prompts for LLMs and generative AI applications

• Contribute to AI workflows, agentic AI solutions, and prompt orchestration to automate business processes

• Partner with business leaders to translate business problems into AI enabled solutions

• Evaluate AI outputs, measure quality, and continuously refine prompt performance

• Design and enforce prompt guardrails to ensure compliant solutions

• Research emerging AI models, prompting techniques, and agent frameworks to continuously improve AI capabilities

To qualify, you must be pursuing a degree from an accredited college or university, and plan to graduate after the final day of the internship. Most of our interns participate in the summer between their junior and senior years. Our 2027 internship will start on June 2nd, 2027, and last through August 13, 2027.

Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, or a related technical field.

Anticipated graduation date between September 2027 and May 2028 .
