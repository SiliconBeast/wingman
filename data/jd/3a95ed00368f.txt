Position Summary

The Data Analyst Intern will support data analytics initiatives spanning IT and Accounting/Finance, building the dashboards and reports that help Hays leadership make faster, better-informed decisions. This role offers rotational exposure across two departments, giving the intern a broad view of how data drives both operational and financial decision-making at a growing electrical contractor.

Key Responsibilities

Reporting & Dashboards

• Build and maintain Power BI dashboards covering recruiting, project cost, and financial KPIs.

• Support ad hoc data and reporting requests from leadership.

Data Quality & Analysis

• Clean and validate data across systems (ERP, ATS, HRIS).

• Identify trends and anomalies, and support root-cause analysis on data discrepancies.

Financial & Operational Analytics

• Support cost tracking and budget analysis alongside the Accounting team.

• Assist with reconciliation and reporting automation.

Cross-Department Collaboration

• Partner with IT, Accounting, and Recruiting to understand reporting needs and translate them into analytics deliverables.

Qualifications & Skills

• Currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Data Analytics or a related field

• Proficiency in Excel; exposure to Power BI, SQL, or similar tools preferred

• Strong quantitative and analytical skills

• Ability to communicate data insights clearly to non-technical audiences

Preferred

• Experience with Power BI, Tableau, or similar BI tools

• Basic SQL or database querying experience

• Coursework or experience in financial or construction-industry analytics

Internship Program Details

• Paid, 10–12 week summer program (early June – mid-August)

• Assigned supervisor for day-to-day work plus a separate mentor for informal guidance and support

• Company-wide orientation, weekly lunch-and-learns with department leaders, and a mid-summer check-in

• Capstone project presented to department and executive leadership in the final week

• Strong performers — particularly graduating seniors — will be considered for full-time offers
