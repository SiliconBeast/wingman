The Data, Systems and Special Projects division of Workplace Infrastructure and Logistics (WIL) is a team focused on laying the foundation for data governance and data analytics practices for our department. We support multiple WIL systems that support our operational divisions like facility maintenance and operations; space utilization and space reservations; facilities support; and asset management teams. We are focused on providing systems capabilities for these divisions to effectively manage and use their data, as well as research and recommend new or existing systems to more effectively run their business.

The Summer Associate would work both independently and collaboratively to support analysts on the team and our internal business partners by performing/supporting data collection, processing, and use; developing and refining systems and systems capabilities; and providing recommendations to support departmental objectives based on available technology.

The Summer Associate Program is a 12-week internship program beginning in May 2027 and ending in August 2027. Students will work on impactful projects and meaningful work during their internship. To qualify for this position, applicants must be currently pursuing a degree from an accredited college or university and have an anticipated graduation date of December 2027 or later.
