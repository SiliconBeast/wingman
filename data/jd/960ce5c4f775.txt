Job Description

Intern - Finance - Plymouth, MN – Summer 2027

Join Philips as a Finance Intern and gain hands-on experience supporting high-impact business decisions in a global MedTech company focused on improving people’s lives. In this paid, 3-month Summer 2027 internship, you will work onsite at our Plymouth, Minnesota location and gain meaningful exposure to corporate finance, business partnering, analytics, and strategic planning in the fast-growing MedTech industry.

Your role:

• Support strategic finance projects connected to the Annual Operating Plan and key business priorities

• Analyze revenue, profitability, spending, and business performance trends to identify actionable insights

• Partner with finance mentors and business stakeholders to develop decision-support materials and recommendations

• Use Excel, PowerPoint, and AI-enabled tools to improve reporting, analysis, and process efficiency

• You will work independently with guidance from a finance mentor and collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders.

You're the right fit if:

• You are currently pursuing an undergraduate degree in finance, accounting, economics, business analytics, or a related field

• You are curious, analytical, and eager to learn how finance supports business strategy and decision-making

• You are passionate about making a difference in patients’ lives and gaining valuable experience in a fast-growing MedTech environment

• You have strong attention to detail, clear communication skills, and experience using Excel and PowerPoint

• You are interested in using AI, automation, and data tools to improve analysis, reporting, and decision-making

• You must be able to successfully perform the following minimum Physical, Cognitive and Environmental job requirements with or without accommodation for this position .
