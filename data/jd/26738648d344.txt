What You Will Do

Collins Aerospace in Melbourne, FL is actively looking for students to join our Avionics Communication, Navigation and Surveillance (CNS) directorate. As part of our team, you will be involved in designing, verifying, and qualifying next generation avionics systems. Gain hands-on experience with our cutting-edge next generation avionics products. Join us and contribute to redefine the aerospace!

• You will participate in a collaborative integrated product team and support the design, integration, verification and validation of avionics CNS products.

• You will actively work in the full product development life-cycle activities and gain hands-on experience in requirements management, systems architecture, test planning and data analysis.

• You will assist in documentation of engineering work in compliance with company and regulatory standards.

What You Will Learn

• You will deepen your understanding of systems engineering, gaining insights into development and integration processes 

• You will gain exposure to Avionics regulatory and certification requirements and processes 

• You will expand your professional network, working alongside industry experts and global teams 

• You will learn why people enjoy and feel fulfilled by working in our industry 

Qualifications You Must Have

• Must be pursuing Bachelor’s or Advanced degree in Mechanical or Aerospace Engineering and actively enrolled through the completion of the internship/co-op session.

• Must have a minimum of 60 hours of credit by the start of the internship / co-op session.

• Experience may be obtained through on-the-job experience, research, coursework, or projects.

Qualifications We Prefer

• Understanding of systems engineering principles and requirements management.

• Displays experience in any of the scripting languages, like Python, Perl.

• Strong interest in the aerospace industry and related fields

• Excellent communication, analytical, and problem-solving skills

• Ability to work independently and as part of a team
