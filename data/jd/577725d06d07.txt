What you’ll be doing

Join our Global Markets team as a Student Analyst or Associate on our eTrading Quant team in this 12-month role and become part of a team focused on delivering innovative banking and investment solutions to CIBC’s retail, wealth and business clients including corporates, governments and institutional clients around the world. Our goal remains to be the leading Capital Markets franchise in Canada and to work as one team to build our brand across North America and the world - proudly living our values as we work toward our vision to be the leader in client relationships.

Our Global Markets business comprises our sales, trading, structuring and research teams spanning cash and derivative products in foreign exchange, commodities, rates and equities in addition to securitization, prime brokerage, money markets and more. We are widely recognized for our capabilities and leadership in these areas and remain committed to investing in technology and talent to drive innovation and scalable growth.

The FX eTrading Quant team develops quantitative models, pricing algorithms, and analytics for the electronic foreign exchange (FX) business. We move quickly on our ideas and test our hypotheses relentlessly. Working closely with both trading, sales, and business analysts, you will help drive the business forward by building out internal applications, infrastructure, analytics, and pricing/market making algorithms.

At CIBC we enable the work environment most optimal for you to thrive in your role. To successfully perform the work, you’ll be on-site full-time.
