Voloridge Investment Management was founded by David Vogel in 2009 and is based in Jupiter, FL. We incorporate advanced data science and mathematics into our systematic, market neutral investment strategies to exploit alpha opportunities we consider unique in financial markets. Our firm is comprised of award-winning predictive modelers, experienced data analysts, advanced technologists, and a group of dynamic business professionals.

We are seeking a motivated Quant Trading Intern to join our team. As an intern, you’ll gain hands-on exposure to systematic trading by assisting with daily trading operations, market research, portfolio analysis, and process improvement projects. Our automated quantitative systems are deeply dependent on vast quantities of data and a proprietary trading platform. This internship is designed to give you meaningful, real-world experience in quantitative trading within a supportive, fast-paced environment.
