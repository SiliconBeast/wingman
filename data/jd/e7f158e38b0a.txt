Job Description & Summary
The Opportunity

As a Tax Intern, you will immerse yourself in the dynamic world of tax consulting, gaining exposure to the complexities of the global tax landscape. Within our Tax practice, you will support teams in delivering tailored tax services that optimize strategies, minimize risks, and drive compliance. As an Intern, you will focus on learning and gaining exposure to PwC's methodologies, participating in projects, and performing basic tasks that contribute to the team's success. You will have the chance to observe professional work environments and develop your personal brand.

In this role at PwC, you will engage with our General Tax Consulting services, providing comprehensive advice and planning strategies to businesses. You will navigate complex tax regulations, optimize tax positions, and minimize risks, all while being part of a supportive and enthusiastic team. This opportunity allows you to apply a learning mindset, appreciate diverse perspectives, and actively listen to enhance your communication skills. Your journey will be enriched by the chance to gather information, analyze facts, and discern patterns, setting the foundation for a rewarding career in tax consulting.

Responsibilities

- Supporting tax teams in delivering comprehensive tax advice and planning strategies
- Participating in the preparation and review of corporate tax returns and compliance documents
- Assisting in the analysis of complex tax regulations to optimize tax positions and minimize risks
- Engaging in international tax planning and multistate tax compliance activities
- Conducting tax research to support consulting projects and provide innovative solutions
- Collaborating with team members to develop tax models and structures for clients
- Contributing to the preparation of tax provisions and audits to validate compliance
- Observing and learning from experienced tax professionals to enhance personal development
- Applying data analytics to identify opportunities for tax optimization and efficiency
- Supporting the development of regulatory compliance consulting strategies for diverse industries

What You Must Have

- Currently pursuing or have completed a Bachelor's degree in the following field of study: Accounting
- At least a 3.0 overall GPA
- Before starting full-time at PwC, meet the educational requirements to be eligible to sit for the CPA exam in your intended state of employment
- Client service intern positions are considered entry-level roles. Candidates are typically in their third year of a four-year degree program or fourth year of a five-year program at the time of application

What Sets You Apart

- Preference for a 3.5 overall GPA
- Leveraging AI to create efficiencies, innovate ways of working and deliver distinctive outcomes
- Demonstrating intellectual curiosity in tax research and planning
- Participating in international tax planning and compliance projects
- Utilizing data analytics to enhance tax strategy optimization
- Engaging in corporate tax consulting and regulatory compliance
- Supporting multistate tax compliance and partnership tax initiatives
