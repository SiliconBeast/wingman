MORSE is seeking a detail-oriented Business Operations Analyst Co-op to plan, track, analyze, report, and monitor the contractual and financial performance of our Department of Defense and national security research and development programs. Responsibilities

•

Monitoring, tracking, analyzing, and reporting on program status on multiple programs varying in size and complexity with attention to contractually deliverable reports, 75% spent metrics, and backlog reporting

•

Collaborating with chief engineers, program managers, portfolio leaders, contracts, and business leadership to understand and track program financial health

•

Developing and delivering reports to assess program contractual and financial performance and projections including the opportunity pipeline

•

Creating and maintaining Excel and ERP-based reports that will be custom-developed based on program requirements
