ABOUT THE ROLE:

As a Materials Engineering/Materials Science Intern here at Honeywell Technologies, you will have the opportunity to gain hands-on experience in the field of materials engineering and contribute to cutting-edge projects. You will work alongside our team of experts to develop and advance materials that are integral to our products. This internship will provide you with valuable insights into materials development, innovation, and the application of materials in real-world scenarios.

This internship will be conducted on-site, allowing you to collaborate closely with our team and gain practical experience in a professional setting.

In this role, you will have the opportunity to:

• Assist in the design, development, and testing of advanced materials

• Collaborate with cross-functional teams to integrate materials into our products

• Conduct research and analysis to identify new materials and technologies

• Support materials testing and characterization activities

• Stay updated with emerging trends and recommend their incorporation into product development

Location: This position is available in multiple locations across the United States.

The exact worksite location will be selected based on business requirements and the candidate’s relevant experience and qualifications.

The hourly base range for this position is $24.00/hr - $45.00/hr. Please note that this salary information serves as a general guideline. Honeywell considers various factors when extending an offer, including but not limited to the scope and responsibilities of the position, the candidate's work experience, education and training, key skills, as well as market and business considerations.

• Must graduate December 2027 or later

• Must continue enrollment in degree program upon completion of the internship

• Validated academic excellence (3.0 GPA and higher)

• Due to compliance with U.S. export control laws and regulations, candidate must be a U.S. citizen, U.S. permanent resident, or have asylum or refugee status in the U.S.

Honeywell Technologies is currently unable to consider applicants who would require current or future sponsorship for employment authorization for this position. Candidates must be authorized to legally work in the United States without current or future sponsorship requirements.
