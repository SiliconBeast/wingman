Overview :
Marathon Petroleum Corporation (MPC) offers internship and co-op opportunities to high-performing college students who want meaningful hands-on experiences in their fields of study. The following core values guide Marathon Petroleum’s approach to doing business: safety and environmental stewardship; integrity; inclusion; collaboration; and excellence. Interns/co-ops work side-by-side with experienced professionals as contributing members of diverse work teams who create value for the company’s shareholders. Marathon Petroleum believes that how we conduct our business is just as important as the bottom line.

As part of MPLX, a midstream company majority owned by MPC, the Natural Gas & Natural Gas Liquids (NGL) Services organization is committed to delivering innovative solutions using Geographic Information Systems (GIS) to support strategic decision-making. We are currently seeking a motivated and detail-oriented GIS Intern to join our team. This is an excellent opportunity for someone looking to gain hands-on experience and develop their skills in the rapidly evolving field of GIS.

As a GIS Intern, you will work alongside experienced GIS professionals and contribute to various projects involving data collection, analysis, and mapping. You will gain valuable experience working with GIS software, managing spatial data, and supporting key initiatives that leverage geospatial information.

Key Responsibilities :

• Assist in the collection, processing, and management of geospatial data using GIS software (e.g., ArcGIS).

• Support the creation and maintenance of maps, spatial data layers, and other GIS-related deliverables.

• Help perform spatial analysis and generate reports to support decision-making.

• Conduct data quality control and ensure data accuracy and consistency.

• Participate in field data collection activities (if applicable).

• Assist with data visualization and presenting geospatial data in accessible formats.

• Support GIS-related tasks for ongoing projects and collaborate with cross-functional teams.

• Provide technical support to team members and troubleshoot GIS-related issues.

• Stay up to date with emerging trends and technologies in GIS.

Qualifications :

• Candidates must be pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Geography, Geospatial Sciences, Urban Planning, Environmental Science, or a related field.

• Concurrent enrollment in a Bachelor's degree seeking program for the duration of the experience.

• Strong academic performance.

• Available to work on site 40 hours per week.

• Valid driver's license for work-related travel.

• Must be able to provide reliable transportation to and from place of work.

• Candidates must be authorized to work in the United States without the need for employer sponsorship now or in the future.

• Familiarity with GIS software (ArcGIS, QGIS, or similar) and geospatial data formats.

• Strong analytical skills and attention to detail.

• Ability to work independently and as part of a team.

• Excellent communication and organizational skills.

• Basic knowledge of spatial analysis, cartography, and data management practices is a plus.

• Previous experience in GIS (internships, coursework, or projects) is preferred.

Why apply ?

• Gain hands-on experience working with GIS tools and technologies.

• Opportunity to contribute to real-world midstream projects and impact decision-making.

• Mentorship and guidance from experienced GIS professionals.
