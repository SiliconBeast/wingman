Qualitrol, an operating company of Ralliant, develops monitoring and diagnostic technologies that help utilities and industrial customers keep critical electrical infrastructure running safely and reliably. Our technologies help customers detect issues early, reduce risk, and make more informed decisions about the assets they depend on most. We help power what matters.

Research & Development (R&D) Fall 2027 Co-Op

As an R&D Co-Op , you'll work alongside engineers and technical leaders to solve real-world challenges, build practical solutions, and contribute to technologies that help shape the future of the energy industry. During this six-month co-op beginning Fall 2027 , you'll gain hands-on experience with automation, software development, sensing technologies, and product innovation while contributing to projects that directly influence the products and technologies our customers rely on. You'll receive mentorship from experienced engineers, collaborate across functions, expand your professional network, and have opportunities to share your work and recommendations with leadership.

This is an opportunity to apply what you've learned in the classroom, strengthen your technical skillset, and see how your contributions can grow from concept to real-world impact.
