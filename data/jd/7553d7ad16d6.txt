POSITION SUMMARY

The IT Systems Product Management Intern will support the Product Management team in driving portfolio growth, market analysis, competitive intelligence, pricing activities, and sales enablement initiatives across the IT Systems product portfolio. This role provides hands-on exposure to product lifecycle management, data center infrastructure technologies, market strategy, and cross-functional business operations.

The ideal candidate is analytical, detail-oriented, and interested in technology, product management, and business strategy. This internship offers an opportunity to gain real-world experience working with product leaders, sales teams, engineering, marketing, and supply chain organizations

RESPONSIBILITIES

• Conduct competitive research on industry-leading IT infrastructure products and solutions.

• Analyze market trends, customer needs, and emerging technologies.

• Analyze sales trends, pricing data, and business performance metrics.

• Support development of pricing recommendations and promotional strategies.

• Track and report key performance indicators (KPIs) across product categories.

• Assist in creating customer-facing presentations, sales tools, and training materials.

• Help develop product positioning and value proposition messaging.

• Participate in customer, distributor, and channel research activities.

• Coordinate action items across Product Management, Engineering, Supply Chain, Operations, and Marketing teams.

• Assist with project tracking and status reporting.

• Support data gathering for strategic planning exercises and management business reviews.

About Vertiv

Vertiv (NYSE: VRT) brings together hardware, software, analytics and ongoing services to enable its customers’ vital applications to run continuously, perform optimally and grow with their business needs. Vertiv solves the most important challenges facing today’s data centers, communication networks, and commercial and industrial facilities with a portfolio of power, cooling and IT infrastructure solutions and services that extend from the cloud to the edge of the network. Headquartered in Westerville, Ohio, USA, Vertiv employs around 34,000 people and does business in more than 130 countries. Visit Vertiv.com to learn more.
