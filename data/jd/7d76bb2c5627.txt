Student Criteria:
• Education : Undergraduate or Graduate students. This program is designed and optimized for rising Junior or Senior Undergraduate students. Students must be actively enrolled in either an Undergraduate or Graduate degree program as of May 31st, 2027 to be eligible.

• Majors : Open to students pursuing degrees in Mathematics, Management Information Systems, Business, Computer Science, or related fields. We welcome applicants from other majors as well, provided they possess the technical skills and analytical mindset we’re looking for.

Skills & Experience:
• Proficiency in SQL and Excel ; experience with Power BI or Tableau is a plus.

• Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

• Ability to take initiative and ask thoughtful questions.

• Comfortable working with data sets and interpreting results.

• Exposure to upper-level mathematics and computer science coursework.

• Strong communication and collaboration skills.

• Eagerness to learn and adapt in a fast-paced environment.

Activities:
• Partner with senior analysts to work through specific projects.

• Assist in building dashboards, reports, and data visualizations.

• Support ongoing analytics projects and contribute to process improvements.

• Participate in team meetings and provide input on data-driven decisions.

• Learn and apply business intelligence tools and techniques.

• Document findings and share insights with manager.

Employee Benefits:  
We aim to protect the wellbeing of our employees and their families with a broad benefits offering. In addition to offering comprehensive health, dental and vision insurance, we support emotional wellbeing through mental health benefits and an employee assistance program. Work/life balance is important and Protective offers a variety of paid time away benefits (e.g., paid time off, paid parental leave, short-term disability, and a cultural observance day). The financial health of our employees is just as important as physical and emotional health.  Some of the financial wellbeing benefits include contributions to healthcare accounts, a pension plan, and a 401(k) plan with Company matching. All employees are encouraged to protect their overall wellbeing by engaging in ProHealth Rewards, Protective’s platform to improve wellbeing while earning cash rewards.   

Eligibility for certain benefits may vary by position in accordance with the terms of the Company’s benefit plans.
