Hermeus is a venture-backed defense aviation company reclaiming the lost art of rapid iterative prototyping to build the fastest aircraft in the world today. By prioritizing relentless hardware iteration, we deliver high-speed systems at the pace of the modern battlefield. We work with the Department of War to provide the high-speed capabilities our nation and its allies need to maintain a durable, asymmetric advantage.


About The Role: 
As a Build Reliability Engineering Intern at Hermeus, you will support the development, improvement, and standardization of manufacturing processes used to build our next-generation aircraft. You will work closely with engineers and technicians to identify manufacturing challenges, investigate root causes, and implement improvements across structural fabrication and assembly processes.
Spring: Around 16 weeks (January - April)
Summer: Around 12 weeks (May - August)


Responsibilities:
• Support the development and improvement of manufacturing processes for aircraft structures and hardware.

• Work hands-on with sheet metal, welded assemblies, tooling, and other manufacturing equipment on the factory floor.

• Investigate manufacturing defects, nonconformances, and process inefficiencies to identify root causes.

• Develop and implement corrective actions to improve quality, repeatability, and manufacturing efficiency.

• Help establish standardized processes and best practices for welding, fabrication, repair, preparation, and assembly.

• Create and improve work instructions, process documentation, and manufacturing standards.

• Partner with engineers and technicians to capture lessons learned and incorporate them into future builds.

• Support continuous improvement projects across structural manufacturing and aircraft assembly.

Requirements:
• Pursuing a degree in Mechanical Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, Materials Science & Engineering, or a related technical field (undergraduate student in your third or fourth year of study, or enrolled in a Master's or Ph.D. program).

• Hands-on experience building, fabricating, assembling, or testing hardware through internships, coursework, clubs, competition teams, or personal projects.

• Comfortable working directly on the factory floor and learning manufacturing processes hands-on.

• Strong interest in manufacturing, welding, materials, structures, or process improvement.

• Ability to approach problems using engineering first principles and root-cause analysis.

• Strong written and verbal communication skills with an ability to clearly document technical processes.

• Demonstrated ability to work effectively with engineers, technicians, and cross-functional teams.

• Enthusiasm for aviation and Hermeus’s mission

• GPA of 3.0 or above

The hourly rate listed for this internship is intended as a general guideline. Final compensation will be determined based on degree level.
Benefits:
Time Off: Interns are eligible for 5 days of sick leave and unpaid time off.
Office Perks: Weekly paid office lunches and fully stocked breakrooms.
