Your Team, Your Impact

Marvell's Central Engineering Foundational IP team is looking for a motivated SRAM Software Engineer Intern to join us in Burlington, VT. You'll work hands-on as part of a team designing, verifying, and characterizing high-performance memory used in cutting-edge semiconductor technologies. This role is an incredible opportunity to apply your software and circuit fundamentals while collaborating with experienced engineers on real-world products that are deployed at scale.

About the Position:
Are you ready to use your software engineering background to innovate and deliver the data infrastructure technology that enables the powerful AI tools that are revolutionizing global industry? Marvell’s Central Engineering Foundational IP team is seeking a motivated SRAM Software Engineer Intern to join our Burlington, VT Custom SRAM team. Our Custom SRAMs are made possible by a dedicated team of expert software engineers that develop infrastructure, tooling, and deliverables for memory IP, as well as create custom memory optimization solutions for chip designs. Do you have what it takes to deliver best-in-class memory projects to the most well-known technology companies in the world?

What You Can Expect

• Work closely with SRAM circuit designers to implement PPA (Power, Performance, Area) data to create design kits in leading-edge technologies.

• Create systems to optimize SRAM memory solutions for System on Chip (SoC) designers and streamline the memory selection process.

• Analyze, summarize, document, and present results to colleagues and local internal stakeholders.

What We're Looking For

• Currently pursuing a Bachelors degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or related technical field(s) and graduating between Fall 2027 and Summer 2028

• Scripting (Perl, Python, Bash) in a command line environment.

• Experience working in a Linux environment.

• Experience with version control systems (Git, GitHub).

• Ability to work effectively in a collaborative team environment.

• Strong verbal and written communication skills.

Preferred Qualifications:

• Experience with Functional programming (Clojure desirable), and Java programming.

• Familiarity with SRAM architecture.

• Basic circuit-design skills (digital logic, transistor fundamentals, etc.)

• Previous experience in the semiconductor industry.
