Job Details

At Cencora, we are united in our responsibility to create healthier futures. As a Summer 2027 Intern, you will have the opportunity to apply your academic knowledge in a professional business environment while gaining meaningful, hands-on experience within our Pharmaceutical Distribution Operations Warehouse Automation team.

Throughout the internship, you will work on real business initiatives, collaborate with team members and leaders, develop professional skills, and contribute to projects that support Cencora's purpose of creating healthier futures.

Start Date: June 7, 2027

End Date: August 13, 2027

Location: Remote but will likely do some DC visits

This internship is designed for students who are curious, motivated, and eager to learn while making a meaningful impact in a fast-paced, collaborative environment.

Under the direction of a Sr. Manager or Director, the Intern assists with inspection, integration, and testing of various Material Handling Controls implementations that form Cencora’s key business operations.

Responsibilities

•
Assists the Project Teams with implementation, functional specifications, interface specification, testing, issue tracking, and trend analysis.

•
Assists corporate and/or division MHE enhancement projects with evaluations, implementation plans, testing, documentation, delivery, deployment for project software/systems/hardware/equipment, and project closeout.

•
Assists with training and support of automated Material Handling installations.

•
Keep management updated with accurate project and task information.

•
Participate in requirements, design, documentation, and evaluations.

•
Support ongoing projects, reporting, analysis, or process improvement initiatives.

•
Collaborate with cross-functional teams and business partners.

•
Participate in team meetings, professional development opportunities, and internship program activities.

•
Present project outcomes, recommendations, or key learnings at the conclusion of the internship.
