As the Treasury Analyst Intern, you will assist the team with preparing, inputting and/or reviewing appropriate journal entries from treasury system. You will help ensure compliance with internal controls and assist with monthly and annual financial reporting and forecasting. A few additional responsibilities will include:

• Ensure departmental internal control policies.

• Assist with integration of new acquisitions into cash pool system and general ledger.

• Consolidate Bosch Global USD, CAD and MXN liquidity reporting.

• Administer security for cash pool banks.

• Review internal control compliance and quarterly reporting.

• Prepare and post financial and accounting entries to G/L for financial reports.

• Prepare and analyze monthly financial reporting packages.

• Reconcile and post accounting entries from treasury system to G/L.

• Assist in preparation of department operating budget for Business Plan.

With this position you will:

• Gain hands on experience on Accounting, Finance, Treasury and Cash Management

• Add valuable skills to the resume

• Build relationships and references

• Can lead to other opportunities within the organization

Minimum Qualifications:

• Currently pursing a Bachelors degree in a Finance or Accounting program

• Minimum of 3.0 cumulative GPA

• Knowledge with treasury software as well as online banking software.

• Proficiency in MS Excel and MS PowerPoint

• Problem solving, mathematical and formulation skills, reasoning and analyzing skills

• Strong written and verbal communication skills

• Able to handle multiple tasks/projects at the same time and meet deadlines

• Must be a team player, detail oriented, and very organized

• Must be able to successfully complete a drug and background screen pre-employment
