Position Overview:

This position reports to a Systems Engineering Team Lead and will collaborate with Systems Engineers and other technical teams. This position is responsible for assisting with the maintenance, support, automation, and continuous improvement of NISC's internal systems, DevOps workflows, deployment processes, and infrastructure. Interns will gain hands-on experience working with enterprise systems, Linux environments, automation tools, monitoring solutions, and application deployment processes. Based on intern interest and business needs, opportunities may exist to explore areas such as DevOps, system administration, scripting, cloud technologies, and infrastructure operations. Adaptability and a willingness to learn are essential.
