The High Frequency Technology Center (HFTC) of Keysight Technologies is seeking an intern to join our Factory Automation team. This role offers the opportunity to contribute to software and systems that support semiconductor manufacturing operations. Working alongside software engineers, automation engineers, process engineers, manufacturing personnel, and IT professionals, you will help develop solutions that improve factory efficiency, equipment utilization, operational visibility, and manufacturing processes. Projects may span software development, automation, data analysis, system integration, and manufacturing systems. This is an excellent opportunity for someone who enjoys learning, solving complex problems, and working on projects that have a direct impact on real-world operations. What We're Looking For We are seeking a motivated engineering student who enjoys solving problems, learning new technologies, and building practical solutions that create real value. The ideal candidate is curious, organized, and capable of working through complex technical challenges while collaborating effectively with engineers, technicians, and manufacturing teams. We value individuals who can balance technical excellence with practicality, focusing on solutions that are maintainable, reliable, and useful for the people who depend on them. Many of the technologies used within our manufacturing environment can be learned on the job. We are more interested in strong engineering fundamentals, a willingness to learn, and a thoughtful approach to problem solving than experience with any single technology. Responsibilities AI-Assisted Development Our team leverages modern AI-assisted engineering tools such as GitHub Copilot, Claude, and similar technologies to accelerate research, development, troubleshooting, and documentation. These tools are used as engineering aids, not replacements for engineering judgment. Candidates should be comfortable learning how to effectively use AI tools while maintaining responsibility for understanding, validating, testing, and supporting the solutions they create. Internship Experience This internship is designed to provide meaningful, hands-on engineering experience in a real manufacturing environment. Interns will be assigned a defined project with clear objectives, ownership, and measurable outcomes. Throughout the internship, participants will work closely with experienced engineers while gaining exposure to software development, automation, manufacturing systems, and cross-functional collaboration. By the conclusion of the internship, the goal is for each intern to have delivered a tangible project, feature, or system improvement that provides value to the organization. The experience should result in a meaningful accomplishment that can be showcased on a resume, discussed in future interviews, and serve as a foundation for continued professional growth. Why Join Us? We believe internships should be more than just a summer job. Our goal is to provide an environment that fosters learning, growth, mentorship, and professional development. You will have the opportunity to work alongside experienced engineers, contribute to real projects, and gain exposure to technologies and systems that directly impact semiconductor manufacturing operations. We encourage curiosity, welcome questions, and support continuous learning throughout the internship experience. Whether your interests are in software development, automation, manufacturing systems, data, or modern engineering practices, you will have the opportunity to build valuable technical and professional skills while making meaningful contributions to the team. If you're passionate about technology, enjoy solving challenging problems, and want to develop solutions that make a real difference, we'd love to hear from you.
