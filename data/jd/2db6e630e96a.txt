Job Description

Ready to make your mark in the world of asset management? Spend an unforgettable summer at Invesco alongside a select group of driven students. Our 9-week Summer Internship Program, kicking off in June 2027, offers you the opportunity to delve into the dynamic world of client services within a global investment firm. You’ll learn from seasoned professionals, gain hands-on experience, and build connections that will last a lifetime.

As part of our Summer Internship Program cohort, you’ll participate in orientation, networking events, development workshops, and volunteer activities. You’ll work side by side with talented teams to learn about the business and contribute to meaningful initiatives.

Here’s the best part: crush your internship, and you could be eligible for a full-time offer to join our Early Career Program after graduation!

Security Overview

At Invesco, Global Security interns will gain hands-on exposure to how cybersecurity teams identify risk, strengthen controls, support secure technology delivery, and manage cyber initiatives across the enterprise. Interns will work across Offensive Security & Testing, Cloud Security & Engineering, and the PMO delivery space, gaining both technical exposure and practical experience in how cyber programs are planned, executed, measured, and communicated. This experience is designed to help interns understand how security testing, cloud and SaaS security, data protection, vulnerability management, and project delivery come together to reduce risk and support business resilience.

This internship provides practical experience across cybersecurity operations, security engineering, and cyber program delivery. Interns will support active work in Offensive Security & Testing, Cloud Security & Engineering, and PMO-led cyber initiatives. The role includes exposure to technical security concepts such as vulnerability management, application security, cloud security, SaaS security, data protection, detection and response, remediation tracking, and security reporting. Interns will also learn how cyber work is organized through project plans, roadmaps, milestones, risks, dependencies, stakeholder updates, and executive-ready reporting.

What You'll Do

• Cybersecurity Program Support: Support active cyber initiatives across Offensive Security & Testing, Cloud Security & Engineering, and PMO delivery workstreams.

• Technical Exposure: Gain exposure to vulnerability management, application security, cloud security, SaaS security, data protection, detection and response, remediation tracking, and security control improvement.

• Project Coordination: Support project managers and cyber teams with meeting notes, action items, decisions, risks, dependencies, and follow-ups.

• Status Reporting: Help maintain project plans, status trackers, roadmaps, dashboards, and delivery materials.

• Security Documentation: Assist with operating models, process flows, testing documentation, remediation plans, playbooks, and stakeholder-ready materials.

• Data and Metrics Support: Help organize security metrics, project updates, risk information, and program data into clear summaries and reports.

• Process Improvement: Identify opportunities to simplify project tracking, improve communication, and make cybersecurity information easier to understand and use.
