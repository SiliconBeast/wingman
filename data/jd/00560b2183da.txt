At Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Nebraska, we are a mission-driven organization dedicated to championing the health and well-being of our members and the communities we serve.

Our team is the power behind that promise. And, as the industry rapidly evolves and we seek ways to optimize business processes and customer experiences, there’s no greater time for forward-thinking professionals like you to join us in delivering on it! As a member of Team Blue, you’ll find purpose, opportunities and the support you need to build a meaningful career and make a powerful impact in our community.

To millions of Americans, Blue Cross and Blue Shield means peace of mind when it's needed the most. That's because the Blue Cross and Blue Shield brand represents the nation's largest and most experienced health care benefit companies, providing insurance coverage to 1 out of every 3 Americans. As a licensee of the BCBS Association since 1939, BCBSNE has celebrated 80+ years of Nebraskans serving Nebraskans. Not only do we provide health insurance coverage to Nebraskans, we are also active members of the communities where our customers live and work by giving back through volunteering and our corporate social responsibility efforts. We would love for you to be a part of BCBSNE’s journey as an intern where you will gain hands-on experiences solving real-world problems.

Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Nebraska is a Partner of Choice for the Nebraska University System. Our Interns work a hybrid schedule and spend some days working on-site at our Omaha office located in Aksarben Village . Therefore, you will need to live within commuting distance from Wednesday, May 26 – Friday, July 30, 2027.

Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Nebraska is seeking enthusiastic students with a passion for technology, innovation, and problem solving. As a Digital Experience Intern, you’ll gain hands-on experience building and improving digital products and member experiences across web, mobile, data, and AI, supported by Agile engineering practices in a dynamic and rewarding environment.

Our Digital Experience teams work with a range of technologies including:

•
AI & Data: Microsoft Copilot, AI-assisted development, Python, SQL, analytics, experimentation, and data workflows

•
Digital Platforms & Delivery: Azure, APIs, DevOps Pipelines, API integration, cloud services, and continuous delivery

•
Web & Mobile Development: React, React Native , Angular, Python, C#, HTML/CSS, JavaScript, JSON/XML

•
Digital Quality & Reliability: Automated testing, accessibility, monitoring, performance tuning, and resiliency patterns

What you'll do:

•
Digital Product Development : Build and improve responsive web and mobile experiences, APIs, and supporting services that make it easier for members and customers to complete important tasks.

•
AI-Enhanced Experiences: Explore AI tools such as Microsoft Copilot and large language models to improve digital experiences, accelerate development, and turn feedback into useful product improvements.

•
Member Experience & Insights: Use research, data, experimentation, and feedback from digital products to understand user needs and surface actionable insights.

•
Digital Quality: Help test experiences and services for usability, accessibility, performance, reliability, and task completion across supported devices and platforms.

•
Agile Collaboration: Participate in daily stand-ups, sprint planning, and retrospectives. Collaborate with developers, designers, analysts, and stakeholders to deliver useful digital experiences.

•
Capstone Project: Tackle a real-world digital experience challenge, such as improving a web or mobile journey, building an AI-supported experience, creating an automated feedback loop, or developing an analytics solution.

•
Mentorship & Training: Receive guidance from experienced engineers and participate in workshops to grow your technical, product, and professional skills.
