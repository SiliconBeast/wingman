Position: Customer Technical Support Co-op/Intern
Number of Position(s): 3
Duration: 4 Months
Date: Jan. 4th - Apr. 30th, 2027
Location: Onsite Ottawa, ON, CANADA

EDUCATIONAL RECOMMENDATIONS

Currently a candidate for diploma, bachelor’s or master’s degree in network technology, Computer/Software/Electrical/Telecommunications Engineering, Computer Science, or related field with an accredited school in Canada.

What you will do:

• Troubleshoot customer network issues across various IP Service Router platforms.

• Manage and support network environments using Nokia SR Linux (SRL), including configuration, troubleshooting, and upgrades.

• Assist in reproducing customer issues in the lab and documenting test results.

• Perform basic configuration tasks on routers and switches under supervision.

• Identify opportunities for automation and develop solutions.

• Help prepare presentations or summaries based on findings from lab tests or customer cases.

• Participate in continuous learning through internal workshops, trainings, and shadowing opportunities.

You Have:

• Knowledge of concepts like the OSI model, TCP/IP, subnetting, routing protocols (e.g., OSPF, BGP), and switching technologies (e.g., VLANs, STP)

• Strong knowledge of Linux platforms

• Strong knowledge of coding and automation

• Excellent communication skills and ability to work well in a team environment

• Excellent written communication skills

• Quick learner and willing to share knowledge with others on the team

•

It Would Be Nice If You Also Had

• IP Certifications or equivalent

• Basic knowledge of how to configure routers and switches

• Exposure to tools such as Wireshark and IXIA
