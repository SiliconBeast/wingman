Position: Learning Technical Editor Co-op/Intern
Number of Position(s): 2
Duration: 4 Months
Date: January 4th - April 16th, 2027
Location: Hybrid in Ottawa, Canada

Education Requirements

Currently a candidate for a Bachelor’s degree or diploma in Arts, Communications, English, Rhetorical Writing, Technical Editing, Technical Writing, or a related field within an accredited school in Canada.

As part of the team, you will:

• Identify and correct errors in our program content that is delivered globally to customers.

• Work with subject matter experts on the content development team to clarify awkward or confusing sections of a course and exam content, considering the diverse backgrounds of students.

• Help design and implement standardized formats for PowerPoint and lab diagrams, including updating PowerPoints according to standardized templates provided.

• Review and correct exam questions for grammar and clarity, additionally helping to proctor exams taken by candidates.
Develop processes to improve the revision and publication of new content.

You have:

• Experience in technical editing.

• Strong English language and editing skills.

• Thorough attention to detail

• Experience with MS Word and PowerPoint.

It would be nice if you also had:

• Excellent interpersonal, organizational, and communication skills.

• Experience with illustration and/or video editing software is considered an asset.
