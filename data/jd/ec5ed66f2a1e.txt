About the role

Launching your career at Intact means joining a diverse community of experts who value your skills in an industry that is constantly evolving. Here, you will receive guidance in a dynamic environment where the work is hands-on and meaningful. You will innovate and help move our organization forward, benefiting our customers and communities. You will grow both personally and professionally through leading-edge learning platforms and build lasting connections across the organization. Above all, you’ll see just how exciting the working world can be.

Here, your career can take off 🚀

The support you will receive

· Coaching and mentorship from actuarial experts and team members.

· Ongoing training and access to modern tools and learning platforms.

· Hands-on project work, with regular feedback and clear objectives.

· Cross-functional collaboration (Underwriting, Marketing, Risk Management, Finance) to broaden your experience.

· Networking opportunities to support your professional development within the organization.

What you will accomplish with us

• Deepen your knowledge of the insurance industry and expand your internal network by collaborating with various partners (Underwriting, Marketing, Risk Management, Finance) on analytical and decision-making assignments.

• Participate in development projects and share your learnings, methods, results, and recommendations with colleagues and stakeholders—including senior leadership—through summaries and presentations.

• Monitor the profitability and competitiveness of insurance portfolios and contribute to preparing the action plan for the coming years.

• Assess the impact of emerging trends (e.g. geopolitical context, climate change) on frequency, severity, and risk profile in order to guide business responses.

• Apply actuarial techniques to support adequate reserves, an appropriate level of reinsurance, and sufficient capital under various catastrophe scenarios.

• Contribute to innovation initiatives (machine learning, customer experience, telematics, climate change) to analyze and improve pricing models in personal and commercial insurance.

What you will bring

• ·Ongoing university studies in one of the following fields: Actuarial Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Business Intelligence, or a related discipline.

• ·Interest in IT, technology, and innovation (an asset).

• ·Strong adaptability, teamwork, autonomy, and sense of accountability.

• ·Strong communication skills (written and verbal).

• ·Analytical and synthesizing skills. · Bilingualism (French and English): required to interact regularly with colleagues across the country.

• ·Must be available 35 hours per week and able to come into the office at least two days per week.

• The intern must be considered an actively enrolled student for the entire duration of the internship.
