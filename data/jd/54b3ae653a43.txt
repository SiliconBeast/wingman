Job Description

This position is for a Software Engineering internship opportunity at Motorola Solutions in Plantation, Florida. We are looking for software engineers with a strong interest in embedded systems to help expand our team and deliver the next generation of communication devices to our customers.

Responsibilities include, but are not limited to, the following:

•
Work in an Agile/Scrum team to analyze, design, implement, and test new two-way radio products and features

•
Teamwork in a multi-site, world-wide development environment

•
Finding, tracking, and repairing RTOS device software defects

•
Create and execute unit, integration, system, load, and acceptance tests

Preferred Skills:

•
Experience in C/C++ is a plus

•
Experience in real-time embedded software is a plus

•
Strong communication and problem solving skills is a plus

•
Experience using version control; Git is a plus

•
Experience with Linux is a plus

•
Experience with embedded development/debug tools (such as emulators/JTAG, etc.) is a plus
