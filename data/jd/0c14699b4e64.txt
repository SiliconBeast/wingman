Description

Embark on a career journey with Franklin Electric, where your passion meets purpose, and every day is an opportunity to make a difference in our global communities in providing access to water, fuel and electricity.

The Engineering Co-op supports Franklin Electric engineering teams through hands-on technical assignments that connect academic learning with real-world product, process, testing, and manufacturing challenges. The role works under the direction of experienced engineers and cross-functional partners to complete defined projects, analyze data, document results, and contribute to the safe, reliable, and cost-effective development and improvement of products and processes.

Franklin Electric is a diverse team of innovators making a positive global impact since 1944. We offer innovative water solutions, ensuring access, treatment, and efficient management of this vital resource. Our commitment to excellence is reflected in our innovative products, industry leading quality products, exceptional customer service, employee development, and passion for social responsibility.

Join us to create a better future while growing professionally in a supportive environment!

MOVE FORWARD WITH US

What you will be doing:

• Assist engineers with the design, development, testing, and continuous improvement of products, systems, manufacturing processes, and tooling.

• Collect, analyze, and interpret engineering, test, and process data to support product validation, problem-solving, and decision-making.

• Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including Engineering, Manufacturing, Quality, and Sourcing, to support project execution and deliverables.

• Prepare technical documentation, present project results, and contribute to continuous improvement initiatives throughout the co-op assignment.

If you are a self-motivated and collaborative individual who is passionate about making a positive impact, we encourage you to apply for this exciting opportunity.

• Education & Experience

• Currently pursuing an accredited bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, Mechatronics, Industrial Engineering, or a related engineering discipline.
