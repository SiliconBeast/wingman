Title: Domestic Scouting Analyst Fellow

Department: Domestic Scouting

Location: Baltimore
Reports To: Assistant Director, Draft Operations
FLSA Status: Part-time/Seasonal, Hourly

Role Overview:
This position will assist in all functions pertaining to the First-Year Player Draft including day-to-day front office operations, statistical collection & review, draft research, and player evaluation. This is a seasonal position that is based out of Baltimore, MD.

Key Responsibilities:

Primary duties to include, but not limited to, the following:

• General administrative and logistical support for Domestic Scouting Department

• Hone player evaluation skills through in-person looks, video, statistics, and other relevant sources of information

• Collaborate with front office, scouts, and other departments during pre-draft process

• Troubleshoot technical and equipment issues and aid scouts

• Operate various technologies to collect performance data on players at club workouts

• Edit and upload video to share as necessary

• Conduct special projects and perform other duties as assigned

Preferred Qualifications:

• General knowledge of baseball and scouting landscape

• Attention to detail

• Strong oral and written communication skills

• Strong interpersonal skills and the ability to work well with staff members of diverse backgrounds

• Must possess intellectual curiosity, an open mind, and desire to learn

• Proficiency with Microsoft Excel, SQL preferred

• Ability to work long hours; evenings, weekends, holidays, and travel as needed

The anticipated hourly rate for this role is $20/hour.

Benefits include concession and merchandise discounts, free parking, and limited complimentary baseball tickets to home games.
