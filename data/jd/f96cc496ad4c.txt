Job Title
Intern- Service Contracts Lifecycle Operations- Nashville, TN-Summer 2027

Job Description

Are you interested in an internship opportunity with Philips? We welcome individuals who are currently pursuing an undergraduate (BS) degree to participate in a 3 month paid internship opportunity at our site in Nashville. Through this role, you will gain meaningful, hands-on experience working for a health technology company.

Your role:

• Support reporting, data validation, and analysis for operational work and team performance.

• Document current reporting tools, data sources, user needs, and improvement opportunities.

• Assist with developing a centralized Power BI dashboard or reporting proof of concept.

• Explore opportunities to use automation and AI for insights, trend identification, and reporting efficiency.

• Help maintain process documentation, team reference materials, and project trackers. Participate in team meetings, improvement projects, and other assigned operational support activities.

You're the right fit if:

• Currently pursuing a bachelor’s degree in Business, Data Analytics, Information Systems, Finance, Operations, Computer Science, or a related field.

• Strong attention to detail, organization, communication, and problem-solving skills.

• Proficiency in Microsoft Excel; experience or interest in Power BI, data visualization, AI, or automation is preferred.

• Comfortable learning new systems and working collaboratively in a professional environment.

• You must be able to successfully perform the following minimum Physical, Cognitive and Environmental job requirements with or without accommodation for this position.
