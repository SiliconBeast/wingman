About the Program

RF-SMART's Summer Internship Program is more than a resume builder. It's a 12-week, full-time, immersive experience designed to give you real work, real responsibility, and real access to leaders across the organization. From day one, you'll be embedded in a team contributing to projects that support our mission of making a difference and changing lives.

About the Role

The Software Support Intern is responsible for collaborating and assisting our Developers resolve a wide range of technical software issues on our product by troubleshooting via telephone, email and/or remote session. A solid foundation in JavaScript is required.

What Makes This Internship Different

• Foundations onboarding program

• CliftonStrengths assessment to discover your natural talents

• Professional development workshops on communication, presentation skills, and workplace presence

• Weekly intern standups with our Organizational Development team

• LinkedIn Learning access for ongoing skill building

Direct Access to Leaders

Through our Meet the Leader series, you'll hear directly from company leaders about their career journeys, industry insights, and professional advice. These sessions are interactive and built to help you form real connections inside the organization.
