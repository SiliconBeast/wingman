We are seeking a Mechanical Engineering Intern for Photonics Physics Package R&D in our Broomfield, CO location to support trapped-ion integrated photonics R&D team.
Our diverse team of engineers and scientists develops optics and integrated photonics technologies that enable scaling of our trapped-ion quantum computers from our current 98-qubit Helios system to our future Lumos system with hundreds of thousands of qubits. The data, designs, and expertise from our group inform our next-generation systems, from the overall architecture to the tiniest of details. We are looking for more collaborative engineers and scientists to join our cross-disciplinary team.   


Key Responsibilities:
• Support the trapped-ion integrated photonics R&D effort through one or more of the following: 

• Support the design, build, integration, and/or operation of cryogenic ultra-high vacuum (UHV) test systems used to evaluate and scale next-generation quantum computing technologies.  

• Create and maintain 3D CAD models, detailed engineering drawings, and assembly documentation for custom mechanical components, fixtures, and test hardware.  

• Collaborate with engineers and scientists to fabricate, assemble, troubleshoot, and improve experimental ion trap test systems.  

• Collect and analyze data to validate hardware performance, reliability, and manufacturability. 

• Support root cause investigations and failure analysis activities to identify design improvements and enhance system performance.  

• Contribute to design reviews, engineering documentation, and configuration management processes.  

• Work closely with the Integrated Photonics team to design and build fixtures, tooling, and test hardware supporting photonic device characterization and validation.

WE VALUE:
• Curiosity towards vacuum systems, cryogenics, precision mechanics, optics, photonics, or scientific instrumentation and their connection to quantum computing

• Strong problem-solving skills with a willingness to troubleshoot complex multidisciplinary systems. 

• Ability to communicate technical concepts clearly and work effectively in multi-disciplinary teams.  

• Experience with CAD software such as SolidWorks or Onshape. 

• Hands-on experience designing, fabricating, or assembling mechanical systems in a laboratory, research, academic, or industry setting.

• Experience operating rapid prototyping tools or working with machine shops and vendors.  

• Familiarity with structural, thermal, vibration, or finite element analysis methods.  

• Programming or scripting experience (Python, MATLAB, LabVIEW, or similar) for automation, analysis, or test equipment control.
