Job Description

We encourage candidates to apply for no more than three internship opportunities within a 90-day period. Our recruiting team will review your application and ensure you are considered for the opportunities that best align with your skills and interests.

The sustainability space is rapidly evolving, and businesses need strong data and analytics to support their sustainability goals. As a Data Analyst Intern, you will gain hands-on experience supporting Cox Conserves’ sustainability initiatives. You’ll work closely with the Data Analysis Manager and Sr. Director, Environmental Sustainability, learning how data analysis drives strategic decision-making. 

This internship is designed for students or recent graduates passionate about environmental sustainability and eager to develop their analytical skills. You’ll receive mentorship, training, and exposure to real-world sustainability data challenges. 

Roles & Responsibilities:  

Primary Responsibilities and Essential Functions:  

• Drive proof-of-concept AI projects to enrich the team’s AI toolkit and catalyze AI solution development.

• Assist in executing recurring jobs to support data pipeline, quality assurance (QA), and reporting workstreams under supervision. 

• Support the automation of manual tasks to improve efficiency and quality, leveraging AI when appropriate.

• Participate in the design and development of future state data pipelines, learning new technologies as needed. 

• Help simplify and standardize internal reports for use across the organization. 

• Learn to identify data quality issues and assist in resolving them, including presenting findings to team members. 

• Contribute to the documentation of the data pipeline, QA, and reporting processes for business use. 

• Prioritize and deliver assigned work using a logical and methodical process. 

Day-to-day duties may include:  

• Assist with recurring QA jobs and analyze output with guidance. 

• Support investigation and resolution of data quality issues. 

• Help maintain dashboards and identify modification needs as data sources evolve. 

• Participate in tool and platform migrations of existing solutions. 

• Work with internal and external data sources to support environmental data collection and quality. 

• Ensure data/reporting complies with applicable environmental reporting standards (e.g., GHG, GRI) and company policies. 

Qualifications:  

This individual will have:  

• Passion for Environmental Sustainability. 

• Willingness to learn and tackle new problems. 

• Strong attention to detail and accuracy. 

• Interest in developing analytical and technical skills. 

• Ability to communicate ideas clearly. 

• Ability to collaborate and work effectively with various teams. 

• Flexibility and adaptability to changing work environments. 

• Strong verbal and written communication skills. 

What We’re Looking For:  

Required:  

• Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Finance, Mathematics, Environmental Science, or a related discipline. 

• Coursework or experience with analytics, reporting, data mining, or data visualization (class projects, internships, etc.). 

• Familiarity with SQL and/or Python (coursework or personal projects). 

• Proficient in MS Office, especially Excel. 

Preferred:  

• Experience with data visualization tools (Tableau, PowerBI) through coursework or projects. 

• Exposure to cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, Microsoft Fabric) is a plus. 

• Interest in sustainability metrics, reporting, or environmental data. 

• Familiarity with sustainability regulations or standards is a plus. 

This internship offers the opportunity to learn from experienced professionals, contribute to meaningful sustainability projects, and develop skills for a future career in data analytics and environmental sustainability.
