Your Team, Your Impact

The Custom Cloud Solutions (CCS) Hardware Validation Group is responsible for ensuring the quality, reliability, and performance of next-generation data center ASIC and SoC products — spanning cloud infrastructure, AI accelerators, network processors, NICs, custom ASICs, SSD controllers, CXL devices, and domain-specific accelerators. Our team owns end-to-end hardware validation, working across the full product lifecycle from early silicon bring-up through system-level qualification. As an intern, you will work alongside experienced engineers in a fully instrumented hardware lab, gaining hands-on exposure to cutting-edge silicon validation across high-speed interfaces and complex platform environments. This is a unique opportunity to contribute to real engineering challenges that directly impact the future of data infrastructure.

What You Can Expect

•
Assist in functional hardware validation and debug activities for platforms utilizing high-speed interfaces such as PCIe, CXL, and Ethernet

•
Support system bring-up tasks by running test cases, collecting data, and documenting results under the guidance of senior engineers

•
Write and execute automation scripts (Python or similar) to streamline silicon testing and validation workflows

•
Operate standard lab equipment — including logic analyzers and oscilloscopes — to assist with signal integrity analysis and system debugging

•
Collaborate with cross-functional teams (design, firmware, and validation) to investigate and reproduce hardware or protocol-level issues, and contribute to root-cause analysis

What We're Looking For

•
Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's Degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field with an expected graduation date of Fall 2027 – Summer 2028

•
Basic understanding of digital logic, computer architecture, or hardware design principles gained through coursework or personal projects

•
Familiarity with at least one scripting or programming language (Python, C, or similar) for writing test scripts or automating tasks

•
Ability to work in a hands-on lab environment and a willingness to learn how to use standard lab equipment (e.g., oscilloscopes, logic analyzers)

•
Strong communication skills and a collaborative mindset, with the ability to document findings clearly and work effectively within a team

Preferred Qualifications:

•
Coursework or project experience related to high-speed interfaces (PCIe, Ethernet, USB, or similar protocols)

•
Exposure to hardware bring-up, FPGA/ASIC design, or embedded systems through academic projects or internships

•
Experience with version control tools (e.g., Git) and basic familiarity with Linux/Unix environments
