Role Overview:
Help build and validate the next generation of cloud-based software designed to help employees develop their skills and fulfil their potential. As a Software Developer Intern/Co-op in Test on the Agent Development team, you will contribute across the software development lifecycle, including design, implementation, testing, automation, debugging, and verification of production-quality services. You will gain hands-on experience with Java, Python, Selenium, Playwright, AWS, and other technologies selected to solve real engineering challenges involving scalability, security, quality, and usability. You will work alongside experienced engineers in an agile environment, contributing meaningful code and test automation while developing strong foundations in software engineering and quality practices. At Genesys, you will collaborate with a global team focused on building technology that helps organizations create better experiences for employees and customers.

Key Responsibilities:

•
Design and verify high-quality full-stack software collaboratively across the development lifecycle, from implementation and testing through deployment and ongoing improvement

•
Develop and maintain automated tests that improve software quality, test coverage, and confidence in production releases

•
Build and enhance automated test pipelines that support reliable and efficient software delivery

•
Validate resilient, scalable, secure, and maintainable cloud applications built on AWS infrastructure

•
Investigate and debug software issues, collaborating with developers and quality engineering partners to identify effective solutions

•
Explore modern technologies and testing approaches that strengthen automation, engineering efficiency, and continuous improvement

•
Demonstrate ownership of software quality by contributing to design discussions, identifying risks, and helping ensure features meet expected standards
