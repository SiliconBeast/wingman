What you’ll be doing...

As a Network Intern, you will work at the heart of the world’s most reliable 5G network in our new Network &Technology Digital Group. You will support the engineers who design, deploy, and maintain the infrastructure that connects millions of people. This isn't a "shadowing" role—you will own projects that directly impact network performance and customer experience. You won’t just be writing code from scratch; you’ll act as a critical integrator and verifier, using your judgment to stress-test, evaluate, and deploy agentic AI solutions into our enterprise workflows.

In this hybrid internship, based out of Basking Ridge, NJ you’ll have a defined work location that includes work from home and assigned office days set by your manager. If relocation is required, relocation assistance may be available.

You’ll also join us for the Intern Marquee event in Basking Ridge, NJ, a unique, in-person experience designed exclusively for all US interns. This multi-day event is your chance to build your connections through structured networking, and be inspired by top executives in a professional environment.
