GreatAmerica is a highly successful entrepreneurial company providing equipment financing to businesses across the United States. Our exemplary customer service, our principle-centered business philosophy and our team-based operating approach are key to our success and growth. We have also recently become a bank!

We are Looking to Add a Key Member to Our Finance Group!

The GreatAmerica Accounting Internship provides hands-on experience as part of a strong and collaborative Accounting team. The intern will receive mentorship from experienced team members while independently owning recurring responsibilities and contributing to meaningful accounting work. Through this experience, the intern will strengthen their accounting knowledge, develop practical Excel skills, and gain capabilities they can apply in school and future career opportunities.

As an Accounting Intern, you will:

•
General ledger account reconciliation

•
Participate in month end closing

•
Assist with annual financial statement audit

•
Prepare and audit recurring operational reports

•
Prepare monthly reporting for various external customer programs

•
Participate in Accounting team strategic initiatives

•
Provide an outstanding GreatAmerica experience to all customers

To be successful in this role you will need:

Education

•
Candidate must be pursuing a bachelor’s degree or graduate degree in Accounting with a minimum GPA of 3.25

Experience

•
No previous work experience required, but prior internships or work in finance/accounting are considered a plus

Computer Skills

•
Experience with Microsoft Excel

Other Requirements

•
Must be detail oriented and have strong interpersonal and communication skills

•
Must demonstrate initiative, respond well to challenges and have a strong desire to learn

•
Must demonstrate sound business judgment
