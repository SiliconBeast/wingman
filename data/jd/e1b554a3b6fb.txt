As a Software Engineering Intern ,  you’ll gain hands-on experience developing applications and systems that support business and customer solutions. You’ll work with modern development tools and frameworks to build scalable, high-quality software while contributing to the full software development lifecycle. We offer multiple roles within this program, providing support from our diverse, passionate, and talented technology teams.  You'll  receive guidance from skilled technical leaders who value your teamwork and coordination abilities, and gain exposure to industry-leading, enterprise technologies. 

We are currently looking for highly motivated students to join us in our Waterloo, Ontario, Canada office for a 12-month co-op/ internship, running from April 29 th , 2027 – April 27 th , 2028 . This is a hybrid position, where you are expected to be in the office Tuesday-Thursday, with the opportunity to work remotely on Mondays and Fridays.
