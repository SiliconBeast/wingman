Job Description:

Length of term : 8 months (Winter/Summer 2027) January 2027 - August 2027

Hybrid: This co-op opportunity will have a combination of in-person and remote work. Remote work outside of Canada will not be a possibility.

Reporting to the Manager, Financial Reporting, the Accounting Analyst, Co-op will support the Financial Reporting team with quarter and year end processes as well as other related projects and initiatives.

What will you do?

•
Quarter end/year end reporting responsibilities for financial statement preparation

•
Help in process improvement projects and initiatives during the off-quarter/ year end work

•
Review external financial statements including proof reading, checking data inputs, and validation checks

•
Participate in a world-class training program as you work towards your Chartered Professional Accountant (CPA) designation

•
Under supervision, prepare financial statements for non-public companies for governance and tax reporting

•
Ensure effective delivery, filing, and record keeping to support the external audit process

•
Provide project control of audit process by collecting and tracking audit requirements and ensuring timely deliverables

•
Other ad-hoc projects as required

What do you need to succeed?

•
Pursing a university level degree, with the intention to obtain a CPA designation.

•
Ability to work independently and with minimal direction and frequent interruptions

•
Ability to be proactive - thinking several steps ahead to understand and address needs

•
Ability to thrive in an environment with pressing deadlines and constantly changing conditions

•
Demonstrated academic excellence and leadership ability

•
Good problem-solving and decision-making skills

•
Excellent communication skills, both verbal and written

•
Energetic, enthusiastic, and excellent interpersonal relations to interact professionally with people at all levels of the company

•
Proficiency working with Windows and Microsoft Office

•
Ability to learn new software applications quickly

•
Strong organizational and time-management skills

•
Must possess a high level of commitment and dedication to the role and the team

•
Flexibility and commitment to work significant overtime as required
