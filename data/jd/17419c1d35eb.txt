What You Will Do:

Collins Aerospace is a leader in technologically advanced, intelligent solutions that help redefine the aerospace and defense industry. With a comprehensive portfolio and deep technical expertise, we help customers meet the demands of the global market. Join us and help shape the future of aerospace and defense.

Perform assignments in technical/engineering organizations while enrolled in an accredited degree program. This position is for a summer intern to support the Network and Enterprise Services Product Teams on C2BMC in Colorado Springs, CO. The selected candidate will be given tasks appropriate for their level and will work with mentors to develop their skills as needed.

• Assisting in the installation, configuration, and maintenance of network hardware and software (e.g., routers, switches, firewalls).

• Performing routine network updates, backups, and maintenance tasks.

• Working closely with IT teams, software developers, and other departments to ensure the network supports all business functions efficiently.

Qualifications You Must Have:

• Must be pursuing Bachelor’s or Advanced degree in Mechanical or Aerospace Engineering and actively enrolled through the completion of the internship/co-op session.

• Must have a minimum of 60 hours of credit by the start of the internship / co-op session.

• Must have taken basic IT courses that focus on the function and use cases for devices such as routers, switches, servers, storage, and/or firewalls. Additionally, must have taken a course on systems/software engineering fundamentals.

• Experience may be obtained through on-the-job experience, research, coursework, or projects.

Preferred Qualifications:

• Familiarity with Agile development cycle.

• Hands-on experience with creating, loading, or troubleshooting network configurations a plus.

• Ability to obtain a Secret Security Clearance prior to the start date is required.

• Having an 8570 IAT II compliant certification at start is desired.
