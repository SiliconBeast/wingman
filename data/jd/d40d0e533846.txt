Trimble is an industrial technology company transforming the way the world works by delivering solutions that enable our customers to thrive. In this role, you will sit at the intersection of customer needs and technical execution, directly influencing product feature development, prototyping novel solutions, and leading beta programs that shape the market strategy of cutting-edge technologies.

Discover and translate real-world customer needs into high-value product requirements and feature sets.

•

•
Partner cross-functionally across development and business teams on complex, multi-dimensional projects while building core leadership capabilities.
