What impact you’ll make

As an intern on the Product Lifecycle Program Management team, you will be an active contributor to the business by identifying and eliminating process inefficiencies that hinder our Product Management organization. You will apply Lean methodologies to standardize our internal workflows, ensuring we can deliver on our product commitments with greater speed and consistency.

Job Description

What you’ll do (Job Responsibilities)

• Partner with Lifecycle Program Managers to identify operational bottlenecks and develop Lean solutions to streamline product lifecycle workflows.

• Formalize "Standard Work" and process documentation to ensure consistency across programs and prevent work duplication across product development initiatives.

• Optimize cross-functional communication by creating standardized workflows that synchronize responsibilities between engineering and commercial teams.

• Analyze lifecycle program data to identify opportunities for cycle-time reduction, ensuring technical objectives remain aligned with product strategy.

What you’ll bring (Basic Qualifications)

• Current enrollment in undergrad or graduate degree.

• Completed at least two full years of college.

• Strong proficiency in the Microsoft toolset (Excel, Word, etc.); familiarity with project planning tools such as Smartsheet is a plus.

• Must be 18 years of age or older

• Must have reliable transportation and a valid driver’s license

• Must be willing to work out of the Longmont, CO facility for duration of role.

What will make you stand out (Desired Qualifications)

• You have a passion for Lean methodologies and a desire to see how they drive product management strategy.

• Minimum 3.0 GPA (without rounding)

• You are someone who brings strong interpersonal skills and an ability to influence cross-functional teams in a fast-paced environment.

• You have experience with or academic exposure to Value Stream Mapping, Kaizen events, or complex program planning.

• You possess a strategic mindset and the ability to connect granular project tasks to broader business and customer goals.

• Prior internship experience in project, program, or process improvement roles.
