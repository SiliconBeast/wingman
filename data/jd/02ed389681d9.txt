The Enterprise AI Enablement team helps employees successfully adopt and scale the use of AI technologies across Xcel Energy. This intern will support AI adoption programs, employee training initiatives, communications, success story collection, and business value realization activities. The role works closely with business units, Copilot Champions, and AI leaders to help employees discover new ways to improve productivity and business processes using AI.

Typical intern responsibilities may include but are not limited to:

•
Assist with AI adoption and employee enablement programs.

•
Help develop training materials, guides, communications, and learning content.

•
Support AI workshops, demonstrations, and employee engagement activities.

•
Collect and document AI success stories and business value outcomes.

•
Analyze usage and adoption data to identify trends and opportunities.

•
Support ongoing improvements to AI resources, websites, and knowledge repositories.

This position is for a May 24, 2027 start date. This position may have the possibility to extend beyond the internship’s initial term based on the candidate’s successful performance and Xcel Energy’s business needs.
