At Howmet Aerospace, we’re always in airplane mode! Our global teams are passionate about engineered solutions that advance the world through the aerospace, automotive, defense and commercial transportation industries. Become a part of our diverse culture of learning, development, and mentorship as a Cybersecurity Intern in Summer 2027.

Your education is just the beginning – you’ll become an active member of our Corporate Information Security team located in Pittsburgh, PA . During our 12-week internship program, you’ll tackle real-world challenges through impactful assignments, and work alongside our industry changemakers.

The Cybersecurity Intern will focus primarily on Policy, Risk and Governance, while gaining experience in Cyber Data Loss Prevention (DLP), Cyber investigations, Vulnerability Management (VM), Security Awareness, and Cyber Incident Response. The intern will receive a well-rounded cybersecurity experience, gaining exposure to both the governance and risk functions, and the technical security operations that support the organization’s security posture.

A Day in the Life at Howmet…

In this internship, you will:

•
Support efforts to identify, assess, and mitigate cybersecurity risks

•
Develop cybersecurity policies and standards

•
Assist with risk assessments and risk documentation, developing reports, dashboards/insights that support risk-informed decision-making

•
Review security controls and control evidence

•
Work on compliance activities and audit readiness

•
Framework alignment, particularly NIST, CMMC, and ISO 27001

•
Identify and track gaps/remediation

•
Understand how governance translates technical cybersecurity requirements into business expectations

•
Interact with the other members of the Howmet Corporate Information Security team and learn the roles/responsibilities of all the team members
