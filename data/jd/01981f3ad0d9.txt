The Test Solutions Engineering team within the Hardware Discipline Center is at the heart of our innovation, focusing on developing cutting-edge electrical solutions for our products. Our systems are vital to the full product lifecycle, supporting everything from high-tech engineering labs to critical field operations and sustainment. As an Electrical Engineer on this team, you will have a hands-on opportunity to design and integrate state-of-the-art test systems using the industry's latest tools. You will be a key contributor to the evolution of our products, ensuring our systems meet technical and performance goals in a highly collaborative environment.

What You Will Do

•
Design, develop, and integrate test products for complex systems within a multidisciplinary team

•
Create test interface adapters, cabling, schematics, and detailed circuit designs

•
Develop engineering and production test systems that align with cost and schedule goals

•
Troubleshoot and resolve complex technical issues during the development and implementation phases

•
Utilize modern equipment including oscilloscopes, network analyzers, and power meters for validation

•
Collaborate independently or as part of a team to uncover and resolve test product challenges

Qualifications You Must Have

•
Pursuing Bachelor’s Degree in Science, Technology, Engineering, or Mathematics (STEM)

•
Knowledge of electrical components including resistors, capacitors, and transistors

•
Experience with test and measurement equipment such as oscilloscopes, multimeters, and spectrum analyzers

Qualifications We Prefer

•
Experience with circuit design and analysis for analog, digital, and mixed-signal circuits

•
Proficiency in programming languages such as Python, C/C++, or MATLAB for simulation and automation

•
Strong problem-solving and troubleshooting capabilities in a dynamic environment

•
Proven ability to manage multiple tasks and priorities to meet assigned deadlines

•
Experience in technical writing and delivering formal presentations
