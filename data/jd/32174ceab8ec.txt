About the Role

As a Product Applications Engineer Intern, you will gain hands-on experience supporting the evaluation, integration, and application of semiconductor products in real-world systems. Working alongside experienced engineers, you will assist with product testing, technical problem-solving, application development, and customer-focused engineering activities while building a strong foundation in applications engineering.

Key Responsibilities

• Assist in product evaluation, validation, and testing activities.

• Support the development of application tools, demonstrations, and reference designs.

• Help troubleshoot technical issues and analyze system performance.

• Conduct laboratory measurements and analyze test data.

• Collaborate with cross-functional teams including Design, Product, Test, and Marketing Engineering.

• Contribute to technical documentation, reports, and presentations.

Must Have Skills

• Basic understanding of analog and digital electronics.

• Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

• Ability to collect, analyze, and interpret technical data.

• Familiarity with laboratory test equipment and measurement techniques.

• Basic programming or scripting knowledge (Python or similar).

• Strong written and verbal communication skills.

• Ability to work effectively in a collaborative team environment.

Preferred Education and Experience

• Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's Degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field.

• Experience through coursework, projects, research, or internships involving electronics, embedded systems, or hardware/software development.

• Familiarity with communication interfaces, system-level design, or product testing is a plus.

• Interest in applications engineering, customer support, and semiconductor technologies.
