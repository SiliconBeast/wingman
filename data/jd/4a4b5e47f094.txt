Censys internships are competitively paid, ~12-week summer opportunities built around real ownership. Every intern is paired with a manager and a mentor, meeting with both of them regularly, and works on production-impacting projects from day one — whether that's a customer-facing feature, an internal tool, or original security research. The internship also includes a six-part "Career Kickstart" series (professional foundations, organizational navigation, feedback, ownership, personal branding, and interviewing), and a week together in person at our Ann Arbor office.

Censys is small enough that intern work ships to production and reaches real customers — not a shelved capstone project. You'll work directly with the engineers and researchers who built the company's core products, get direct feedback and mentorship rather than a checklist of tasks, and leave with concrete, real-world outcomes (shipped features, published research, measurable infrastructure improvements) you can point to in your next interview.
