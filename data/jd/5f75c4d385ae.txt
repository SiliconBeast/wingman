Position Overview:

The Single-Family Business Technology Office (BTO) is seeking Software Developer Interns to help deliver technology solutions that support Freddie Mac’s business, modernize cloud capabilities, manage technology risk, and improve delivery speed and resiliency.

Interns will gain firsthand experience through training, mentorship, Agile project work, application development, business intelligence, data visualization, reporting, and emerging technologies.

Our Impact:

• The Single-Family Business Technology Office (BTO) delivers technology solutions that support Freddie Mac’s mission, strengthen business operations, modernize cloud capabilities, manage technology risk, and improve delivery speed and resiliency.

• Our team partners across Single-Family to develop applications, improve data and reporting capabilities, automate processes, and support reliable technology platforms that help the business serve customers effectively.

• Through innovation, collaboration, and responsible use of emerging technologies, BTO helps create scalable solutions that advance Freddie Mac’s housing mission and prepare interns to contribute to meaningful technology work.

Your Impact:

• Refine user stories, assess acceptance criteria, and support sprint work.

• Develop or enhance code, unit tests, automation scripts, dashboards, and reports.

• Assist with production support, root-cause analysis, releases, and bug resolution.

• Collaborate in an Agile team and communicate the business value of technical solutions.

• Use AI-enabled tools responsibly and participate in intern trainings, bootcamps, and hackathons.

Qualifications:

• Pursuing a bachelor’s degree in computer science, Computer Engineering, Information Systems, Software Engineering, Data Analytics, or related field.

• Expected graduation in December 2027 or May 2028; rising juniors preferred, with availability May–August 2027.

• Introductory coding experience with Java, C/C++, Python, Angular/Spring Boot, or similar.

• Exposure to Agile, business tools, reporting, analytics, Tableau, or data visualization preferred.

Keys to Success in this Role:

• Effective communication, collaboration, curiosity, and initiative.

• Interest in learning new technologies and Freddie Mac business processes.

• Ability to explain technical concepts, data, and visualizations to business audiences.

• AI literacy includes effective prompting and validation of AI-generated responses.
