2027 Business Technology Solutions (BTS) Internship

Envision spending your summer working with energetic colleagues and inspirational leaders, all while gaining world-class experience in one of the most dynamic organizations in the pharmaceutical industry. This is a reality for Business Technology Solutions (BTS) Interns at AbbVie.

The Cloud Engineering Intern will help keep AbbVie's business platforms running and evolving — supporting, designing, and delivering technical solutions end to end. Specific responsibilities could include:

•
Troubleshoot issues and resolve incidents to keep platforms running smoothly

•
Partner with business and IT teams to deliver enhancements and integrations

•
Support the improvement of platform performance, scalability, and reliability

•
Help track milestones, coordinate teams, and support on-time delivery

•
Maintain documentation, support testing and deployment, and follow IT governance requirements

•
Assess risk and support cybersecurity and data protection with business, vendor, and technical teams

This is an 11-week onsite internship. The majority of BTS Interns will be placed in Lake County, IL, though there is a possibility of being placed within any U.S. AbbVie or Allergan Aesthetics location. While location preferences are considered, placements will ultimately be based on business need.

Minimum Qualifications:

•
Pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, AI or related majors with a minor or concentration in one of the above fields

•
Graduation date between December 2027 – June 2028

•
Must be enrolled in university the semester following your internship and graduate within 1-year post internship

Preferred Qualifications:

•
Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP) and DevOps tooling (Docker, Kubernetes, Jenkins, CI/CD)

•
Working knowledge of architecture and how structure and solutions fit together

•
Experience with low-code platforms (Pega, OutSystems) and troubleshooting/configuring platforms such as ServiceNow, UKG Pro WFM, or Workday Time Tracking/Absence

•
Experience with XML, XSLT, WebServices, and Dell Boomi

•
Previous internship or related experience

•
Technical and business insight, strong analytical skills, and creative problem-solving abilities

•
Proven track record of teamwork, adaptability, innovation, initiative, and integrity

•
Excellent communication, leadership, and project management skills

•
Cumulative GPA of 3.00 or higher

Benefits and Amenities:

• Competitive pay

• Relocation support for eligible students

• Select wellness benefits and paid holiday / sick time
