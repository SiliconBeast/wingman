About the role
Efficient Computer is building a new energy-efficient processor architecture from the ground up. As a Silicon Engineering Intern, you'll join our hardware team and work on production silicon, not a sandbox project. Each intern owns a well-scoped project, has a dedicated mentor, and presents results to the engineering team at the end of the internship.

What you'll do

• Help define micro-architecture for blocks and features, and implement RTL in SystemVerilog.

• Turn specs into verification plans, build testbench components, and write and debug tests.

• Run synthesis, place-and-route and timing analysis, and evaluate power, performance and area trade-offs.

• Build and improve design, verification and implementation flows, including automation and AI-assisted tooling.

• Work with architecture, design and verification engineers to find and fix issues before tapeout.

Required qualifications

• Currently pursuing a BS, MS or PhD in Electrical Engineering or Computer Engineering with graduation expected by Summer 2028.

• At least one term of school remaining after summer 2027.

• Coursework or project experience in digital logic design, verification, VLSI/physical design, or EDA tooling.

• Experience with at least one programming or scripting language, such as Python, C/C++, Tcl or Perl.

How to stand out

• Coursework in computer architecture.

• Verilog and System Verilog proficiency

• Experience with synthesis, static timing analysis, or place-and-route tools

• Experience with simulators and waveform debug

• FPGA design experience

• Interest in applying LLMs/agents to engineering workflows.

Details
